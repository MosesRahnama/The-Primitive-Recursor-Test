This requires that `S(n) > n` (which holds by the interpretation of `S`) and that `S(n) > x` and `S(n) > y` (which may not hold if `x` or `y` are larger than `S(n)`). However, LPO with lexicographic comparison of arguments (left-to-right) can succeed here because the third argument is compared first, and `S(n) > F(x, y, n)` can be shown by induction on `n`.
