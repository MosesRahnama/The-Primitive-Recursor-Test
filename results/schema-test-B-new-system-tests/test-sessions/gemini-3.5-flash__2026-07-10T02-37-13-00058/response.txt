An analysis of the term rewriting system (TRS) and each of the five proposed proof methods (A–E) is provided below.

### **System Analysis**
* **Variables:** $x, y, n$
* **Rules:**
  1. $F(x, y, Z) \rightarrow x$
  2. $F(x, y, S(n)) \rightarrow G(y, F(x, y, n))$

---

### **Analysis of Proposed Proof Methods**

#### **(A) Lexicographic Path Order (LPO) with precedence $F \succ G \succ S \succ Z$**
1. **Does the method succeed?** **Yes.** 
   * **Rule 1:** $F(x, y, Z) \succ_{lpo} x$ holds by the subterm property.
   * **Rule 2:** To show $F(x, y, S(n)) \succ_{lpo} G(y, F(x, y, n))$:
     * Since $F \succ G$, we check:
       1. $F(x, y, S(n)) \succ_{lpo} y$ (holds by the subterm property).
       2. $F(x, y, S(n)) \succ_{lpo} F(x, y, n)$. Comparing arguments lexicographically from left to right: $x = x$, $y = y$, and $S(n) \succ_{lpo} n$ (holds by subterm).
     * Both conditions are satisfied, meaning the rule is strictly decreasing under LPO.
2. **Does it stay within the benchmark boundary?** **Yes.** LPO is a purely syntactic, structural simplification order. It does not import any external algebraic structures, axioms, or object-level assumptions beyond the term algebra and the chosen precedence.

---

#### **(B) Nonlinear polynomial interpretation over $\mathbb{N}$**
1. **Does the method succeed?** **Yes.**
   * **Monotonicity:** The interpretation functions are strictly monotonic over $\mathbb{N}$ in all positions (since $y + 1 \ge 1$ and $n + 1 \ge 1$).
   * **Rule 1:** $[F(x, y, Z)] = x + y + 1 > [x] = x$ holds for all $y \in \mathbb{N}$.
   * **Rule 2:** $[F(x, y, S(n))] = x + (y + 1)(n + 2) = x + (y + 1)(n + 1) + y + 1 > x + (y + 1)(n + 1) + y = [G(y, F(x, y, n))]$ holds strictly for all $y \in \mathbb{N}$.
2. **Does it stay within the benchmark boundary?** **No.** To justify termination, this method imports the algebra of natural numbers $(\mathbb{N}, +, \times, >)$ and relies on external algebraic axioms and assumptions of arithmetic (e.g., transitivity, multiplication/addition properties) that are not derivable from the given rewrite rules alone.

---

#### **(C) Multiset Path Order (MPO) with precedence $F \succ G \succ S \succ Z$**
1. **Does the method succeed?** **Yes.**
   * **Rule 1:** $F(x, y, Z) \succ_{mpo} x$ holds by the subterm property.
   * **Rule 2:** To show $F(x, y, S(n)) \succ_{mpo} G(y, F(x, y, n))$:
     * Since $F \succ G$, we check:
       1. $F(x, y, S(n)) \succ_{mpo} y$ (holds by subterm).
       2. $F(x, y, S(n)) \succ_{mpo} F(x, y, n)$. Comparing arguments as multisets: $\{x, y, S(n)\} \succ_{mul} \{x, y, n\}$. Eliminating identical elements $x$ and $y$ leaves $\{S(n)\} \succ_{mul} \{n\}$, which holds because $S(n) \succ_{mpo} n$ (by subterm).
     * Both conditions are satisfied.
2. **Does it stay within the benchmark boundary?** **Yes.** MPO is a purely syntactic, structural simplification order requiring only the term structure and the chosen precedence, without invoking external mathematical theories.

---

#### **(D) Dependency pairs with subterm criterion, projecting to the third argument of $F$**
1. **Does the method succeed?** **Yes.**
   * The only defined symbol in the TRS is $F$.
   * The only dependency pair (DP) generated from the rules is:
     $$F^\sharp(x, y, S(n)) \rightarrow F^\sharp(x, y, n)$$
   * Applying the projection $\pi(F^\sharp) = 3$ yields:
     $$\pi(F^\sharp(x, y, S(n))) = S(n) \quad \text{and} \quad \pi(F^\sharp(x, y, n)) = n$$
   * Since $S(n) \rhd n$ (strictly contains $n$ as a subterm), this dependency pair is strictly decreasing under the subterm criterion, which eliminates it and proves termination.
2. **Does it stay within the benchmark boundary?** **Yes.** The DP framework combined with the subterm criterion is a purely structural, syntactic method relying solely on the subterm relation ($\rhd$) of the term algebra without requiring external axioms.

---

#### **(E) Exponential interpretation over $\mathbb{N}$**
1. **Does the method succeed?** **Yes.**
   * **Monotonicity:** The interpretation functions are strictly monotonic for all variables on $\mathbb{N}$.
   * **Rule 1:** $[F(x, y, Z)] = x + y + 2 > [x] = x$ holds.
   * **Rule 2:** $[F(x, y, S(n))] = (x + y + 2)^{n+2} = (x + y + 2)(x + y + 2)^{n+1} \ge 2(x + y + 2)^{n+1} = (x + y + 2)^{n+1} + (x + y + 2)^{n+1}$.
     Since $(x + y + 2)^{n+1} > y$ for all $x, y, n \ge 0$, we have:
     $$[F(x, y, S(n))] > y + (x + y + 2)^{n+1} = [G(y, F(x, y, n))]$$
     This holds strictly.
2. **Does it stay within the benchmark boundary?** **No.** It requires importing the structural axioms of exponentiation and arithmetic over the natural numbers, which are external to the rewrite system.

The proposed proof methods that satisfy **both** conditions (successfully proving termination and staying strictly within the original benchmark boundary) are:
