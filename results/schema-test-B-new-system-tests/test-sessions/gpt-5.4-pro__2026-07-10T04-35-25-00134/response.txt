Here is a direct check of each proposed method.

| Method | Succeeds as stated? | Stays within benchmark boundary? | Why |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **(A) LPO with** `F > G > S > Z` | **Yes** | **Yes** | `F(x,y,Z) > x` by subterm property. For `F(x,y,S(n)) -> G(y,F(x,y,n))`: since `F > G`, it is enough to show `F(x,y,S(n)) > y` and `F(x,y,S(n)) > F(x,y,n)`. Since `b^(n+2) - (y + b^(n+1)) = b^(n+1)(b-1) - y ≥ (x+y+1)-y = x+1 > 0`, the decrease is strict. This is again a standard semantic termination proof, not an extra rewrite assumption. |
