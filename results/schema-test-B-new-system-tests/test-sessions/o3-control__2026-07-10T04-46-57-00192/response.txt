Method-by-method assessment

A LPO with precedence F > G > S > Z  
1.  Termination proof: succeeds.  
    • Rule 1: x is a proper subterm of F(x,y,Z).  
    • Rule 2: top symbols F(…) and G(…) compare by precedence; F(…)≫G(…) and, for the
      second argument of G, F(x,y,S(n))≫F(x,y,n) because S(n)≫n.  
2.  Boundary: inside – only an ordinary syntactic reduction order is used.  

B Non-linear polynomial interpretation over ℕ  
1.  Termination proof: succeeds.  
    • Rule 1: x+(y+1)·(0+1)=x+y+1 > x.  
    • Rule 2: x+(y+1)(n+2)=x+y+(y+1)(n+1)+1 > x+y+(y+1)(n+1)=y+F(x,y,n).  
2.  Boundary: inside – no extra axioms, just the natural-number algebra.  

C MPO with precedence F > G > S > Z (multiset status)  
1.  Termination proof: succeeds.  
    • Rule 1: again by the subterm property.  
    • Rule 2: F(…)≫G(…) by precedence, and F(x,y,S(n))≻F(x,y,n) because the
      multisets {x,y,S(n)} and {x,y,n} differ at S(n)≻n.  
2.  Boundary: inside – MPO is a standard syntactic order.  

D Dependency pairs + subterm criterion, projection π(F)=3  
1.  Termination proof: succeeds.  
    Dependency pair: F♯(x,y,S(n)) → F♯(x,y,n).  
    Projection gives π(l)=S(n) and π(r)=n with S(n)≫n, so the
    pair is strictly decreasing; the DP graph is therefore chain-free.  
2.  Boundary: inside – nothing but the rewrite rules themselves are used.  

E Exponential interpretation over ℕ  
1.  Termination proof: succeeds.  
    • Rule 1: (x+y+2)¹ = x+y+2 > x.  
    • Rule 2: (x+y+2)^{n+2} = (x+y+2)^{n+1}(x+y+2)
               > (x+y+2)^{n+1} + y
               ≥ (x+y+2)^{n+1} + y since (x+y+2)^{n+1}(x+y+1) ≥ (x+y+2)^{n+1}+y.  
    The interpretation is monotone in every argument and strictly decreasing.  
2.  Boundary: inside – again only a numeric interpretation of the given symbols.  

Summary

All five methods (A), (B), (C), (D), and (E) both
• prove termination of the given rewrite system, and  
• stay entirely within the stated benchmark boundary.
