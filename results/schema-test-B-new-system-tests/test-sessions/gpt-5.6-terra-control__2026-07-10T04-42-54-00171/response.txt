For rule 2, \(F(x,y,S(n)) >_{\mathrm{LPO}} G(y,F(x,y,n))\): \(F>G\), the left side exceeds \(y\), and it exceeds \(F(x,y,n)\) lexicographically because \(S(n)>n\).
