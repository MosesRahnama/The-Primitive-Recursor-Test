**Analysis of each method**

**System:**

```
(VAR x y n)
(RULES
  F(x, y, Z) -> x
  F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(y, F(x, y, n))
)
```

---

**Method (A) – Lexicographic Path Order (LPO) with precedence F > G > S > Z**
- **Termination proof:** Rule 1: `F(x,y,Z)` > `x` by the subterm property. Rule 2: `F(x,y,S(n))` > `G(y, F(x,y,n))` because F > G in the precedence, left side > both arguments of G (`y` by subterm, `F(x,y,n)` by equal root and lexicographic comparison where the third argument `S(n)` > `n` by subterm). Succeeds.
- **Boundary:** LPO uses only the term structure and a finite precedence on the existing symbols. It imports no external models, axioms, or structural assumptions. Inside the boundary.

**Method (B) – Nonlinear polynomial interpretation over ℕ**
- **Termination proof:** Checked arithmetic: both rules yield strictly greater left-hand interpretations. Succeeds.
- **Boundary:** This method interprets `Z` as 0, `S` as `n+1`, and uses natural numbers with addition and multiplication. These arithmetic operations and the natural number structure are not derivable from the given rewrite rules; they are an external semantic model. Outside the boundary.

**Method (C) – Multiset Path Order (MPO) with precedence F > G > S > Z**
- **Termination proof:** Similar to LPO but uses multiset comparison for `F(x,y,S(n))` vs `F(x,y,n)`: `{x, y, S(n)} >_mul {x, y, n}` holds because `S(n)` > `n` by subterm. Succeeds.
- **Boundary:** MPO is a purely syntactic method; it requires no external models or assumptions. Inside the boundary.

**Method (D) – Dependency pairs with subterm criterion, projecting to the third argument of F**
- **Termination proof:** Dependency pair: `F^#(x, y, S(n)) -> F^#(x, y, n)`. Projecting to the third argument yields `S(n)` and `n`. `S(n)` is a proper superterm of `n`, satisfying the strict decrease. Succeeds.
- **Boundary:** Dependency pairs and subterm criterion are syntactic techniques based on the term structure. Inside the boundary.

**Method (E) – Exponential interpretation over ℕ**
- **Termination proof:** The exponential arithmetic checks out; both rules yield strictly greater left-hand interpretations. Succeeds.
- **Boundary:** Like (B), this imports the natural numbers with arithmetic (addition, multiplication, exponentiation) as an external model. Outside the boundary.
