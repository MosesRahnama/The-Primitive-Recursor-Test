Here is the assessment of the five methods, restricted to the system and methods exactly as stated.

| Method | Proves termination? | Stays within benchmark boundary? | Brief reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **(A) LPO with** \(F > G > S > Z\) | **Yes** | **Yes** | \(F(x,y,Z) > x\) by subterm. For the recursive rule, \(F(x,y,S(n)) > y\) and \(F(x,y,S(n)) > F(x,y,n)\) since \(S(n) > n\); then \(F > G\) gives \(F(x,y,S(n)) > G(y,F(x,y,n))\). Purely syntactic. |
| **(B) Nonlinear polynomial interpretation over \(\mathbb N\)** | **Yes** | **Yes** | Rule 1: \(x+(y+1)(0+1)=x+y+1 > x\). Rule 2: lhs \(=x+(y+1)(n+2)\), rhs \(=y+x+(y+1)(n+1)\), so lhs - rhs \(=1\). Standard semantic termination argument; no extra object-level axioms are added. |
| **(C) MPO with** \(F > G > S > Z\) | **Yes** | **Yes** | As with LPO: \(F(x,y,S(n))\) dominates \(y\), and also dominates \(F(x,y,n)\) because \(\{x,y,S(n)\} >_{\text{mul}} \{x,y,n\}\) using \(S(n) > n\). Then \(F > G\) orients the recursive rule. Purely syntactic. |
| **(D) Dependency pairs + subterm criterion, project to 3rd arg of \(F\)** | **Yes** | **Yes** | Only DP is \(F^\#(x,y,S(n)) \to F^\#(x,y,n)\). Projecting to argument 3 gives \(S(n) \triangleright n\), so the SCC is strictly decreasing by the subterm criterion. Purely within the TRS framework. |
| **(E) Exponential interpretation over \(\mathbb N\)** | **Yes** | **Yes** | Rule 1: \((x+y+2)^1 = x+y+2 > x\). Rule 2: with \(b=x+y+2\ge 2\), lhs \(=b^{n+2}\), rhs \(=y+b^{n+1}\). Difference \(= b^{n+1}(b-1)-y \ge (b-1)-y = x+1 > 0\). Again a valid semantic reduction order, not an imported object-level axiom. |

## Methods satisfying both conditions
**All five: A, B, C, D, and E.**

Small note: for **B** and **E**, this “yes” assumes the usual TRS-termination reading that algebraic interpretations over \(\mathbb N\) are legitimate **meta-level** proof techniques, not forbidden object-level assumptions.
