Analysis of the five proposed techniques on the given two-rule TRS

  F(x, y, Z)  →  x
  F(x, y, S(n))  →  G(y, F(x, y, n))

(A)  LPO, precedence F > G > S > Z  
    • Rule 1:  F(x,y,Z) > x by the subterm property.  
    • Rule 2:  F(x,y,S(n)) > G(y,F(x,y,n)) because  
      – F > G in the precedence, and  
      – F(x,y,S(n)) > F(x,y,n) (lexicographically, since S(n) > n).  
    ⇒ Termination proved.  
    • No extra assumptions are imported.  
    ⇒ (A) satisfies both conditions.

(B)  Non-linear polynomial interpretation over ℕ  
       F(x,y,n) = x + (y+1)(n+1)  
       S(n)=n+1, G(a,b)=a+b, Z=0  
    • Rule 1:  x + y + 1 > x.  
    • Rule 2:  x + (y+1)(n+2)  = RHS + 1.  
    ⇒ Strict decrease, hence termination.  
    • Pure interpretation method, no external axioms.  
    ⇒ (B) satisfies both conditions.

(C)  MPO with precedence F > G > S > Z, multiset status  
    • Rule 1 decreases by the subterm property.  
    • Rule 2: multiset {x,y,S(n)} strictly dominates {x,y,n} because S(n) > n;  
      with F > G the outer step is accepted.  
    ⇒ Termination proved.  
    • Uses only the given rules.  
    ⇒ (C) satisfies both conditions.

(D)  Dependency pairs + subterm criterion, projection π(F)=3  
    • Only dependency pair:  F♯(x,y,S(n)) → F♯(x,y,n).  
    • Projection gives S(n) → n, a proper subterm.  
    ⇒ Chain breaks; termination follows.  
    • Method relies solely on the TRS.  
    ⇒ (D) satisfies both conditions.

(E)  Exponential interpretation over ℕ  
       F(x,y,n) = (x+y+2)^(n+1), S(n)=n+1, G(a,b)=a+b, Z=0  
    • Rule 1:  x+y+2 > x.  
    • Rule 2:  (x+y+2)^(n+2) > y + (x+y+2)^(n+1) because  
      (x+y+2)^(n+2) = (x+y+2)·(x+y+2)^(n+1)  and  (x+y+2) ≥ 2.  
    ⇒ Strict decrease, hence termination.  
    • Again, no outside axioms are invoked.  
    ⇒ (E) satisfies both conditions.

Summary

Method   Terminates?   Stays within benchmark?  
A (LPO)          Yes                    Yes  
B (Poly ℕ)       Yes                    Yes  
C (MPO)          Yes                    Yes  
D (DP + π₃)      Yes                    Yes  
E (Exp ℕ)        Yes                    Yes  

Therefore every one of the listed methods, A through E, meets both requested criteria.
