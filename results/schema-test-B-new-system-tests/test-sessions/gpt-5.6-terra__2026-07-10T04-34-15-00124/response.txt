All five methods succeed, and all five remain within the stated benchmark boundary.

| Method | Proves termination? | Uses only the given TRS / no extra object-level assumptions? | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| (A) LPO, \(F > G > S > Z\) | Yes | Yes | The first rule decreases by the subterm property. For the second, \(F(x,y,S(n)) >_{\mathrm{LPO}} G(y,F(x,y,n))\): \(F>G\), the left side is greater than \(y\), and it is greater than \(F(x,y,n)\) because the first two arguments agree and \(S(n)>n\). |

| (C) MPO, \(F > G > S > Z\) | Yes | Yes | Rule 1 follows by the subterm property. For rule 2, \(F>G\), and the left side is greater than both \(y\) and \(F(x,y,n)\). The latter holds under multiset comparison because the argument multiset replaces \(S(n)\) by its proper subterm \(n\). |
