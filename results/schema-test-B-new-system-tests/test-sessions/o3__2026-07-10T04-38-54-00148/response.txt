System recap  
Rules  
1.  F(x ,y ,Z)          →  x  
2.  F(x ,y ,S(n))       →  G(y ,F(x ,y ,n))

The system is in fact terminating because the third argument of F
strictly decreases from S(n) to n until it becomes Z, after which no
rule can be applied.

Below, “✓” means “yes”, “✗” means “no”.

────────────────────────────
(A)  Lexicographic Path Order (LPO)  
•  Termination proof?          ✓  
   – Rule 1:  F(x,y,Z)  ≻  x  (x is a proper sub-term).  
   – Rule 2:  F(x,y,S(n))  ≻  G(y,F(x,y,n)) because  
     (i)  F ≻ G in the precedence and  
     (ii) F(x,y,S(n)) ≻ F(x,y,n) since the argument list is compared
          lexicographically and S(n) ≻ n.  
•  Inside benchmark boundary?  ✓  
   No auxiliary axioms are used; only the ordering itself is employed.

(B)  Non–linear polynomial interpretation over ℕ  
     F(x,y,n)= x+(y+1)(n+1) , S(n)= n+1 , G(a,b)= a+b , Z=0  
•  Termination proof?          ✓  
   – Rule 1:  x+(y+1)(0+1)=x+y+1 > x.  
   – Rule 2:  x+(y+1)(n+2) − [y+x+(y+1)(n+1)] = 1 > 0.  
•  Inside benchmark boundary?  ✓  
   A standard semantic interpretation; no extra object-level axioms.

(C)  Multiset Path Order (MPO) with precedence F ≻ G ≻ S ≻ Z  
•  Termination proof?          ✓  
   – Rule 1:  sub-term property.  
   – Rule 2:  root comparison F ≻ G; furthermore  
     {x,y,S(n)} ≻_{mul} {x,y,n} because S(n) ≻ n, hence  
     F(x,y,S(n)) ≻ F(x,y,n) and thus ≻ G(y,F(x,y,n)).  
•  Inside benchmark boundary?  ✓

(D)  Dependency pairs + subterm criterion (projection π(F)=3rd arg)  
   Dependency pair:  F♯(x,y,S(n)) → F♯(x,y,n)  
   π(left)=S(n) , π(right)=n , and n is a strict sub-term of S(n).  
•  Termination proof?          ✓  (the subterm criterion breaks every
   infinite DP chain).  
•  Inside benchmark boundary?  ✓  
   Only the given rules are analysed; no extra assumptions.

(E)  Exponential interpretation over ℕ  
     F(x,y,n)=(x+y+2)^{n+1} , S(n)=n+1 , G(a,b)=a+b , Z=0  
•  Termination proof?          ✓  
   – Rule 1: (x+y+2)^1 = x+y+2 > x.  
   – Rule 2: (x+y+2)^{n+2} − [y+(x+y+2)^{n+1}]  
             = (x+y+2)^{n+1}(x+y+1) − y ≥ 2 > 0.  
•  Inside benchmark boundary?  ✓

────────────────────────────
Methods satisfying both conditions  
A, B, C, D and E — in other words, every one of the five proposed
methods succeeds and remains within the stated benchmark boundary.
