**Methods that satisfy both conditions**

**A, C, and D** succeed in proving termination and remain within the original benchmark boundary. They rely solely on syntactic structural comparisons (path orders, dependency‑pair projections, and the subterm relation) that can be derived from the presented rewrite system alone.

**B and E** also prove termination, but they cross the benchmark boundary because they import an external arithmetic model (the natural numbers with addition, multiplication, and exponentiation) and assign object‑level numerical semantics to the function symbols that are not derivable from the given rules.
