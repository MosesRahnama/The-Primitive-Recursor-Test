    - `F(x,y,S(n)) > y` (subterm property).
    - `F(x,y,S(n)) > F(x,y,n)` via lexicographic comparison of arguments: first two arguments (`x`, `y`) are identical; third argument `S(n) > n` holds by subterm property (n is a proper subterm of `S(n)`), which is preserved under substitution due to LPO's recursive clause ensuring the subterm relation is embedded.
