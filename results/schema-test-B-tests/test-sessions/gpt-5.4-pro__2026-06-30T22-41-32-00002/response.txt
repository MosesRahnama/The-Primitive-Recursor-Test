Interpreting the benchmark boundary as allowing standard **meta-level termination techniques** but not extra **object-level axioms/assumptions**, the assessment is:

| Method | Succeeds? | Stays within boundary? | Brief reason |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **A. LPO with \(F > G > S > Z\)** | **Yes** | **Yes** | \(F(x,y,Z) > x\) by subterm/variable occurrence. For \(F(x,y,S(n)) \to G(y,F(x,y,n))\): \(F>G\), the lhs is \(>\,y\), and lhs \(>\,F(x,y,n)\) since with same root \(F\), the third argument decreases \(S(n) > n\). |
| **B. Polynomial interpretation** | **No** | **Yes** | The root rules decrease, but as stated this is **not a valid direct termination proof** because the interpretation is not strictly monotone in all arguments: \(F\) ignores \(y\), \(G\) ignores its first argument. So rewrite steps inside those positions need not decrease. Example: \(F(Z, F(Z,Z,Z), Z) \to F(Z,Z,Z)\) but both sides interpret to \(1\). |
| **C. KBO with all symbol weights 1** | **No** | **Yes** | Rule 2 cannot be oriented: KBO’s variable condition fails since \(y\) occurs once on the lhs but twice on the rhs. Also with uniform weights the rhs is heavier anyway. |
| **D. Dependency pairs + subterm criterion, project arg 3 of \(F\)** | **Yes** | **Yes** | The only DP is \(F^\#(x,y,S(n)) \to F^\#(x,y,n)\). Projecting to the 3rd argument gives \(S(n) \triangleright n\), so the SCC is chain-free. |
| **E. Direct measure \(\mu\) on third argument** | **No** | **Yes** | As stated, \(\mu\) ignores rewrite-active positions (e.g. the second argument of \(F\), first argument of \(G\)), so not every rewrite step strictly decreases it. Example: \(F(Z, F(Z,Z,Z), Z) \to F(Z,Z,Z)\) leaves \(\mu\) unchanged at \(1\) (taking \(\mathrm{depth}_S(Z)=0\)). |

## Methods satisfying both conditions
**A and D**.
