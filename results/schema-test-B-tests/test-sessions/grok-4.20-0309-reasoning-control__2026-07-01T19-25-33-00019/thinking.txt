(A) Lexicographic Path Order (LPO) with precedence F > G > S > Z.

LPO: lexicographic path order.

Precedence: F > G > S > Z (assuming total on symbols)

LPO requires: for rule l->r, l > r, and l > each arg of r.

For first rule: F(x,y,Z) -> x

F > x (var), yes since F highest.

For second: F(x,y,S(n)) -> G(y, F(x,y,n) )

Left head F > G head right, so need left > y and left > F(x,y,n)

F(x,y,S(n)) > F(x,y,n): same head, lex compare args: x=x, y=y, S(n) vs n.

S > Z, and S(n) > n by subterm property (LPO has t > any proper subterm t'), yes S(n) > n.

So yes, works. Succeeds.
