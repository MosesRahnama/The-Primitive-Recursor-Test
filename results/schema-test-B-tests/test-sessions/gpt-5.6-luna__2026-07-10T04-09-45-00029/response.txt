Correction to **(B)**:  
\[
[F(x,y,S(n))]=x+(n+1)+1=x+n+2,
\]
whereas
\[
[G(y,F(x,y,n))]=[F(x,y,n)]=x+n+1.
\]
So the recursive rule strictly decreases under the given interpretation.

Therefore, the methods satisfying **both** conditions are:

\[
\boxed{\text{(A), (B), and (D)}}
\]

All three use only standard termination reasoning applied to the given rewrite rules; none requires additional object-level assumptions about \(n\).
