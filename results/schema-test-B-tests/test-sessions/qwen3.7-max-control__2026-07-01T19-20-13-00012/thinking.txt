A polynomial interpretation over N assigns to each n-ary function symbol f a polynomial f_N: N^n -> N.

The interpretation extends to terms: [x]_α = α(x) for variables, [f(t1,...,tn)]_α = f_N([t1]_α, ..., [tn]_α).

For >_pol to be a reduction order:
1. Well-founded: yes (since > on N is well-founded).
