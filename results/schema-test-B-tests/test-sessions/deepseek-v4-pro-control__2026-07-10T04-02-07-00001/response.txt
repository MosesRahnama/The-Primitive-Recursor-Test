**A: LPO with precedence F > G > S > Z**  
- **Termination proof**: Yes. The first rule is oriented by the subterm property. For the second rule, F > G and the left side is greater than both y (subterm) and F(x,y,n) (same root, third argument S(n) > n by subterm). LPO succeeds.  
- **Within boundary**: Yes, uses only the given syntax and an arbitrary (but allowed) precedence; no structural axioms or object-level assumptions are imported.  

**B: Polynomial interpretation over ℕ**  
- **Termination proof**: Yes. Rule 1: x+1 > x. Rule 2: x+n+2 > x+n+1. All interpretations are monotone and the measure strictly decreases.  
- **Within boundary**: Yes, it is a standard meta-level interpretation; does not add rewrite rules or equate symbols.

**D: Dependency pairs with subterm criterion, projecting to the third argument of F**  
- **Termination proof**: Yes. The only dependency pair is F#(x,y,S(n)) → F#(x,y,n). Projecting to the third argument gives S(n) and n; n is a proper subterm of S(n), satisfying the subterm criterion.  
- **Within boundary**: Yes, it is ordinary metatheoretic reasoning on the given rules, adding only a marker symbol (which is part of the method).  

**E: Direct descent measure on the third argument**  
- **Termination proof**: Yes. Define depth_S (count of S symbols). Rule 1: depth_S(Z)+1 = 1 > 0 = µ(x). Rule 2: µ(F(x,y,S(n))) = depth_S(n)+2 > depth_S(n)+1 = µ(G(y,F(x,y,n))). The measure strictly decreases.  
- **Within boundary**: Yes, the measure is constructed directly from the term structure, without external axioms.
