A termination measure must satisfy  
\[
\mu(\text{target}) < \mu(\text{source})
\]
for every constructor of `Step`.

Let \(|t|_i = \mu_i(t)\). The source-minus-target differences are:

| Rule | `mu1` | `mu2` | `mu3` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `R_int_delta` | \(2+|t|_1\) | \(3+|t|_2\) | \(3+|t|_3\) |
| `R_merge_void_left/right` | \(1\) | \(2\) | \(1\) |
| `R_merge_cancel` | \(1+|t|_1\) | \(2+|t|_2\) | \(1+|t|_3\) |
| `R_rec_zero` | \(1+|s|_1\) | \(5+|s|_2\) | \(1+|s|_3\) |
| `R_rec_succ` | \(-|s|_1\) | \(-|s|_2\) | \(-1-|s|_3\) |
| `R_eq_refl` | \(3+2|a|_1\) | \(5+2|a|_2\) | \(4+2|a|_3\) |
| `R_eq_diff` | \(1\) | \(1\) | \(1\) |

Thus, every rule except `R_rec_succ` strictly decreases all three candidates.

For `R_rec_succ`:

- Under `mu1`, source and target have measures
  \[
  2+|b|_1+|s|_1+|n|_1
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  2+|b|_1+2|s|_1+|n|_1.
  \]
- Under `mu2`, they have
  \[
  6+|b|_2+|s|_2+|n|_2
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  6+|b|_2+2|s|_2+|n|_2.
  \]
- Under `mu3`, they have
  \[
  2+|b|_3+|s|_3+|n|_3
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  3+|b|_3+2|s|_3+|n|_3.
  \]

A concrete counterexample is obtained with `b = n = void`:

- If `s = void`, `mu1` and `mu2` are unchanged, while `mu3` increases.
- If `s = delta void`, all three measures increase.

### Verdict

- **`mu1`: invalid** as a strict termination measure.
- **`mu2`: invalid** as a strict termination measure.
- **`mu3`: invalid** as a strict termination measure.

The problem is that `R_rec_succ` duplicates `s` on the right-hand side. None of these simple additive size measures compensates for that duplication. This only shows that the three candidates fail; it does not by itself show that the rewrite calculus is nonterminating.
