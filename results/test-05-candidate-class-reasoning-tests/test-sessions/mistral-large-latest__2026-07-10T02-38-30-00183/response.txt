### Additional Observations
The issue with all three measures is the `R_rec_succ` rule, which has the form:
```
recDelta b s (delta n) → app s (recDelta b s n)
```
The left side has a `delta` constructor around `n`, while the right side has an extra `app s` around the recursive call. The measures struggle to account for this because:
- The `delta` on the left adds `1` to the measure of `n`.
- The `app s` on the right adds the measure of `s` (which could be large) to the measure of the recursive call.

A successful measure would need to ensure that the "cost" of the `delta` on the left outweighs the "cost" of the `app s` on the right. None of the three candidates achieve this.
