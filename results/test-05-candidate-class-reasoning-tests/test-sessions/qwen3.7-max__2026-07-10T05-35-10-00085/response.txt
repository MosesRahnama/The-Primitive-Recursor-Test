### Root Cause of Failure
All three candidates fail for the exact same structural reason regarding the `R_rec_succ` rule. 

In the rewrite `recDelta b s (delta n) -> app s (recDelta b s n)`, the subterm `s` appears **once** on the left-hand side, but it is duplicated and appears **twice** on the right-hand side (once as the left argument to `app`, and once preserved inside the recursive `recDelta` call). 

For a measure to strictly decrease across this rule, the penalty/weight assigned to `s` inside `recDelta` must be strictly greater than the combined weight of `s` in `app` and `recDelta` on the right side. Because all three candidates use a simple additive sum (weight of `1` for `s` in `recDelta` on the LHS, versus a combined weight of `1 + 1 = 2` for `s` on the RHS), the measure will always increase or stay the same when `s` is duplicated. 

To fix this, a valid measure would need to either assign a heavier multiplicative weight to the `s` argument inside `recDelta`, heavily penalize the `delta` constructor to offset the duplication, or assign a weight of `0` to the `app` constructor (provided it doesn't break other rules).
