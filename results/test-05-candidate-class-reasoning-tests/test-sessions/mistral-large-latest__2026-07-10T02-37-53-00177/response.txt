All three candidates (`mu1`, `mu2`, `mu3`) fail to satisfy the termination condition for the `R_rec_succ` rule. The issue is that the `recDelta` case's measure does not account for the fact that `s` appears twice on the right-hand side of `R_rec_succ` (once as `app s ...` and once inside `recDelta`).

### Suggested Fix

```lean

| .void => 0
| .delta t => 1 + mu_fixed t
| .integrate t => 2 + mu_fixed t
| .merge a b => 1 + mu_fixed a + mu_fixed b
| .app a b => 1 + mu_fixed a + mu_fixed b
```
