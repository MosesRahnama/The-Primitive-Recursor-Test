None of the three proposed measures (`mu1`, `mu2`, `mu3`) is a valid termination measure for the rewrite system defined by `Step`. The critical failure occurs in the rule `R_rec_succ`, where the left-hand side is `recDelta b s (delta n)` and the right-hand side is `app s (recDelta b s n)`. For any choice of constants, the inequality required for a termination measure — that the measure strictly decreases — reduces to `const_delta > const_app + mu(s)`. Since `mu(s)` can be arbitrarily large (e.g., by taking `s` to be a large nested `delta` term), this inequality cannot hold for all terms. Concrete counterexamples (e.g., `b = void`, `s = void`, `n = void`) show that `mu1` and `mu2` give equal measures, while `mu3` even increases. Hence all three candidates fail.
