## Conclusion

**All three candidates fail** on rule `R_rec_succ`. The right-hand side `app s (recDelta b s n)` duplicates the subterm `s`, adding an extra copy of `mu(s)` that no additive constant can compensate for. No simple additive measure over the term structure can handle this duplication; a more sophisticated measure (e.g., one that tracks the recursion depth multiplicatively with the size of `s`, or a multiset/lexicographic ordering) would be required.
