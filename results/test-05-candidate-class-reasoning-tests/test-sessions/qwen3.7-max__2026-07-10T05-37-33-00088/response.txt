To assess the three proposed termination measures (`mu1`, `mu2`, and `mu3`), we must verify if they strictly decrease for every rewrite rule defined in the `Step` relation. That is, for every rule `Step lhs rhs`, we must have `mu(lhs) > mu(rhs)`. 

### Verdict
**None of the three candidates are valid termination measures.** All three fail on the `R_rec_succ` rule due to the duplication of a subterm.

---

### Detailed Analysis

Let's analyze the critical point of failure, which is the `R_rec_succ` rule:
```lean
R_rec_succ : forall b s n, Step (recDelta b s (delta n)) (app s (recDelta b s n))
```

This rule rewrites `recDelta b s (delta n)` into `app s (recDelta b s n)`. Notice that the subterm `s` appears **once** on the left-hand side (LHS) but is duplicated and appears **twice** on the right-hand side (RHS). 

Because all three proposed measures are linear (they simply sum the measures of the subterms plus a constant), we can algebraically evaluate the LHS and RHS for any generic measure `mu` with constants $C_r$ for `recDelta`, $C_a$ for `app`, and $C_d$ for `delta`:

**LHS Measure:**
$$ \mu(\text{recDelta } b \ s \ (\text{delta } n)) = C_r + \mu(b) + \mu(s) + C_d + \mu(n) $$

**RHS Measure:**
$$ \mu(\text{app } s \ (\text{recDelta } b \ s \ n)) = C_a + \mu(s) + \mu(\text{recDelta } b \ s \ n) $$
$$ = C_a + \mu(s) + (C_r + \mu(b) + \mu(s) + \mu(n)) $$
$$ = C_a + C_r + \mu(b) + 2\mu(s) + \mu(n) $$

**Difference (LHS - RHS):**
$$ \text{LHS} - \text{RHS} = C_d - C_a - \mu(s) $$

For the measure to be valid, this difference must be strictly greater than $0$ for all possible traces $s$. However, because $s$ can be an arbitrarily large trace (e.g., deeply nested `delta` terms), $\mu(s)$ can grow infinitely large. No constant values for $C_d$ and $C_a$ can satisfy $C_d - C_a > \mu(s)$ for all $s$.

Let's look at the specific constants for each candidate:

1. **`mu1`**: $C_d = 1$, $C_a = 1$. 
   Difference: $1 - 1 - \mu_1(s) = -\mu_1(s)$. 
   *Fails for any $s$ where $\mu_1(s) > 0$.*

2. **`mu2`**: $C_d = 1$, $C_a = 1$. 
   Difference: $1 - 1 - \mu_2(s) = -\mu_2(s)$. 
   *Fails for any $s$ where $\mu_2(s) > 0$.*

3. **`mu3`**: $C_d = 1$, $C_a = 2$. 
   Difference: $1 - 2 - \mu_3(s) = -1 - \mu_3(s)$. 
   *Fails for all $s$ since $\mu_3(s) \ge 0$.*

### Assessment of the Remaining Rules
For completeness, it is worth noting that all three candidates **do** successfully satisfy the strict decrease condition for the other seven rules in the `Step` relation:

* **`R_int_delta`**, **`R_merge_void_left/right`**, **`R_merge_cancel`**, and **`R_rec_zero`** all drop constructors without duplicating remaining subterms, and the constants chosen in all three measures are large enough to guarantee a strict decrease.
* **`R_eq_refl`** drops `eqW` and its subterms, trivially decreasing to `void` (0).
* **`R_eq_diff`** rewrites `eqW a b` to `integrate (merge a b)`. The constant dropped from `eqW` ($C_e$) is strictly greater than the sum of the constants added by `integrate` ($C_i$) and `merge` ($C_m$) in all three candidates:
  * `mu1`: $3 > 1 + 1$ (Pass)
  * `mu2`: $5 > 2 + 2$ (Pass)
  * `mu3`: $4 > 2 + 1$ (Pass)

### Conclusion
While the candidates correctly handle constructor dropping and non-duplicating rewrites, they fundamentally fail to account for the non-linear duplication of the subterm `s` in `R_rec_succ`. A valid termination measure for this calculus would require either a non-linear polynomial interpretation (e.g., multiplying subterm measures) or assigning a coefficient strictly greater than $2$ to the $s$ argument inside `recDelta` to absorb the duplication on the right-hand side.
