**Assessment of termination measure candidates**

The `Step` relation defines 8 contraction rules on `Trace` terms (no congruence rules are present, so reductions are top-level only). A candidate measure `mu` proves termination if `Step t1 t2` always implies `mu t1 > mu t2` (strict descent on `Nat` is well-founded).

All three measures are structurally recursive size/weight functions that assign different base weights to constructors while adding the measures of immediate subterms. They were checked exhaustively on all 8 rules by symbolic expansion of `mu` on both sides of each rule.

- **All candidates succeed on 7 rules** (strict descent):
  - `integrate (delta t) → void`: descent by at least 2 (or 3 for `mu2`).
  - Both `merge void t → t` and `merge t void → t`: descent by at least 1.
  - `merge t t → t`: descent by at least 1 + `mu t`.
  - `recDelta b s void → b`: descent by at least 1 + `mu s`.
  - `eqW a a → void`: descent by at least 3 (or 5 for `mu2`).
  - `eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)`: descent by exactly 1 in all three measures (the base weight on `eqW` was chosen to compensate for the weights on `integrate` + `merge`).

- **All candidates fail on `recDelta b s (delta n) → app s (recDelta b s n)`**:
  - `mu1`: `mu1(LHS) - mu1(RHS) = -mu1(s)`. Equal when `mu1(s) = 0` (e.g., `s = void`); strictly increases when `mu1(s) > 0`.
  - `mu2`: identical arithmetic: `mu2(LHS) - mu2(RHS) = -mu2(s)`. Equal or increases.
  - `mu3`: `mu3(LHS) - mu3(RHS) = -1 - mu3(s)`. Strictly increases on every step (extra +1 from the base weight on `app`).

Because a single contraction can make the measure non-decrease (or increase), none of the three candidates establish strict descent on every `Step`. Consequently none of them can be used (as written) to prove termination of the rewrite calculus via a single well-founded order on `Nat`.

The root cause is semantic: the `R_rec_succ` rule duplicates the subterm `s`. No assignment of positive integer base weights that count each constructor a fixed number of times can compensate for this duplication while remaining strictly positive on all other rules. A more complex measure (e.g., lexicographic, multiset, or potential function weighted by the numeric "height" of the third argument to `recDelta`) would be required.

**Conclusion**: All three candidates (`mu1`, `mu2`, `mu3`) are invalid for proving termination.
