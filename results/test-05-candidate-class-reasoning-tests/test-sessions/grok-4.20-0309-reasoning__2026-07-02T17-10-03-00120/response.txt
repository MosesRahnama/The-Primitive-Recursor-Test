**Assessment of termination measure candidates**

None of the three candidates (mu1, mu2, mu3) is a valid termination measure for the given rewrite system. A valid measure must satisfy `Step t1 t2 → mu t1 > mu t2` for every rule (strict decrease on ℕ). All three fail on `R_rec_succ`; the other seven rules hold for every candidate.

**Failure on `R_rec_succ`**  
`recDelta b s (delta n) → app s (recDelta b s n)`

- mu1(before) = 2 + μb + μs + μn  
  mu1(after)  = 2 + μb + 2·μs + μn  
  difference = −μs  
  → measure stays the same when μs = 0 and *increases* when μs > 0.

- mu2(before) = 6 + μb + μs + μn  
  mu2(after)  = 6 + μb + 2·μs + μn  
  difference = −μs (identical sign defect).

- mu3(before) = 2 + μb + μs + μn  
  mu3(after)  = 3 + μb + 2·μs + μn  
  difference = −1 − μs < 0 always (measure strictly increases).

The structural reason is identical for all three: the successor rule duplicates the subterm `s` (once in the outer `app` and once inside the recursive `recDelta` argument). Because every candidate uses a linear additive measure with coefficient 1 for every immediate subterm, the right-hand side gains an extra +μs that cannot be cancelled by the supplied constants.

**All other rules succeed for every mu***  
(Brief verification; all use the supplied constants and the uniform +1 coefficient on subterms.)

- `integrate (delta t) → void`: at least 3 + μt > 0.  
- `merge void t → t`, `merge t void → t`: at least 1 + μt > μt.  
- `merge t t → t`: at least 1 + 2·μt > μt.  
- `recDelta b s void → b`: at least 1 + μb + μs > μb.  
- `eqW a a → void`: at least 3 + 2·μa > 0.  
- `eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)`: left is at least one larger than the RHS (3 > 2 for mu1, 5 > 4 for mu2, 4 > 3 for mu3).

Because `R_rec_succ` can make the measure non-decreasing (or increasing), none of mu1–mu3 can be used to witness well-foundedness of `Step` via simple natural-number decrease.

**Summary**  
- mu1: fails (non-strict or increasing on successor).  
- mu2: fails (identical linear defect).  
- mu3: fails (always increases on successor).  

No candidate establishes termination of the rewrite calculus.
