The rewrite system is given by the eight root rules in the inductive family `Step`. (Whether or not the full one-step relation is understood to be closed under arbitrary contexts is immaterial for the argument below: any valid global measure must already be strictly decreasing on every root instance.)

A function \(\mu:\mathsf{Trace}\to\mathbb{N}\) is a termination measure only if
\[
t\mathrel{\mathsf{Step}}t'\quad\implies\quad\mu(t)>\mu(t')
\]
holds for every pair of terms.  It is enough to examine the single rule
\[
\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,(\mathsf{delta}\,n)
\;\longrightarrow\;
\mathsf{app}\,s\,(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,n).
\]
Write \(\mu_b=\mu(b)\), \(\mu_s=\mu(s)\) and \(\mu_n=\mu(n)\).  Direct expansion of the three candidates yields

- \(\mu_1(\text{lhs})=2+\mu_b+\mu_s+\mu_n\),
  \(\mu_1(\text{rhs})=2+\mu_b+2\mu_s+\mu_n\),
  hence \(\mu_1(\text{lhs})-\mu_1(\text{rhs})=-\mu_s\le0\);

- \(\mu_2(\text{lhs})=6+\mu_b+\mu_s+\mu_n\),
  \(\mu_2(\text{rhs})=6+\mu_b+2\mu_s+\mu_n\),
  hence \(\mu_2(\text{lhs})-\mu_2(\text{rhs})=-\mu_s\le0\);

- \(\mu_3(\text{lhs})=2+\mu_b+\mu_s+\mu_n\),
  \(\mu_3(\text{rhs})=3+\mu_b+2\mu_s+\mu_n\),
  hence \(\mu_3(\text{lhs})-\mu_3(\text{rhs})=-1-\mu_s<0\).

In every case the measure fails to decrease (it is non-increasing for \(\mu_1\) and \(\mu_2\), and strictly increases for \(\mu_3\)).  Concrete counter-examples are obtained by taking \(b=s=n=\mathsf{void}\) (measures stay equal or rise from 2 to 3) or \(s=\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void}\) (all three measures strictly increase).

The same three functions *do* decrease on the other seven rules, but a single failing rule already disqualifies a candidate.  Consequently none of \(\mu_1,\mu_2,\mu_3\) is a termination measure for the calculus.

(The underlying reason is structural: every candidate is linear, while the recursor rule duplicates the arbitrary sub-term \(s\).  No choice of constant coefficients can compensate for that duplication.)

Final answer: all 3 fail (on R_rec_succ)
