### Reasoning

The system is strongly normalizing, and this can be proven using the **Lexicographic Path Ordering (LPO)**, a standard termination technique for first-order rewrite systems that requires no additional axioms or assumptions.

**Precedence used:** `cherry > banana > mango > peach > grape > pear > plum`

**Rule-by-rule verification that LHS >ₗₚₒ RHS:**

| Rule | Justification |
|---|---|
| `mango(grape(t)) → plum` | `mango > plum` in precedence |

| `peach(t, plum) → t` | `t` is a subterm of LHS |

**Why this suffices:** LPO is a well-founded simplification ordering that is closed under contexts and substitutions. Since every rewrite rule is oriented strictly left-to-right by LPO, the compatible closure of the rewrite relation (rewriting at any subterm position) is a subrelation of `>ₗₚₒ`. Well-foundedness of LPO then directly implies there are no infinite reduction sequences.
