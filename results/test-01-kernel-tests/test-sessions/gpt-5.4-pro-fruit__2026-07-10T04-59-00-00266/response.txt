- `⟦plum⟧ = 0`
- `⟦grape t⟧ = ⟦t⟧ + 1`
- `⟦mango t⟧ = ⟦t⟧ + 1`

- `mango (grape t) → plum`: `⟦t⟧ + 2 > 0`
- `peach plum t → t`: `⟦t⟧ + 1 > ⟦t⟧`

- `peach t t → t`: `2⟦t⟧ + 1 > ⟦t⟧`

- `banana b s (grape n) → pear s (banana b s n)`: lhs exceeds rhs by `1`
- `cherry a a → plum`: `2⟦a⟧ + 3 > 0`
