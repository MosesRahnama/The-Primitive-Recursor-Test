Yes—of the relation actually defined by `Step`, strong normalization can be established from the file alone.

The rules are root rules only; no contextual/congruence closure is defined. Every rule either:

- reduces to `void`,
- produces a term whose outer constructor is not a left-hand-side redex (`app` or `integrate (merge …)`), or
- returns one of its proper subterms (`t` or `b`).

Thus there is no rule-generated root cycle, and a well-founded structural measure on terms (or a lexicographic measure tracking the reducible root structure and term size) suffices to rule out infinite `Step` chains.

If the intended relation was the usual rewrite relation closed under rewriting inside arbitrary subterms, that closure is not present in this Lean file and would need to be defined and analyzed separately. The conclusion above is therefore specifically for the presented `Step` relation, without adding structural rewrite rules or other assumptions.
