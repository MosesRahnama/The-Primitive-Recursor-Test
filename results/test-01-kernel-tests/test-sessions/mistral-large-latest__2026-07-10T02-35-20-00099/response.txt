#### 3. **Termination Proof Methods**
Given the above, we can use:
- **Lexicographic Path Ordering (LPO):**
  Assign a precedence to constructors (e.g., `void` > `delta` > `integrate` > `merge` > `app` > `recΔ` > `eqW`) and show that the left-hand side of every rule is greater than the right-hand side in the LPO. For example:
  - `integrate (delta t) > void` (since `integrate > void` in the precedence).
  - `merge t t > t` (since `merge` is greater than its arguments, and `t` is compared to itself).
  - `recΔ b s (delta n) > app s (recΔ b s n)` (since `recΔ` is greater than `app`, and `delta n > n`).
- **Multiset Path Ordering (MPO):**
  Alternatively, treat arguments as multisets and show that the multiset of the left-hand side is strictly greater than the right-hand side for every rule.
- **Recursive Path Ordering (RPO):**
  Similar to LPO but more flexible for nested recursion (useful for `recΔ`).
