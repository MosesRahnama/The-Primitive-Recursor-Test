Although the rule syntactically expands the term by wrapping the recursive call in `app s`, the recursion depth is bounded by the finite depth of `delta` constructors in the original third argument. Since no rule creates new `delta` constructors around the third argument of a `recΔ`, and the base case `R_rec_zero` terminates the recursion, any sequence of `recΔ` reductions is finite.

The `app` constructor is inert (has no rewrite rules), so it cannot participate in loops.
