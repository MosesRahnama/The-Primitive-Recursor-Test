**Assessment: Yes**, strong normalization can be established from the presented calculus alone.

**Reasoning:**

The system is strongly normalizing because every rewrite rule strictly decreases a well-founded measure defined on terms. A suitable measure can be constructed as a lexicographic ordering of three components:

1.  **Count of `eqW` constructors:** Rules `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff` both eliminate an `eqW` at the top level (decreasing this count from 1 to 0). No rule introduces new `eqW` constructors. Thus, any reduction sequence can apply these rules only finitely many times.

2.  **Ordinal height of `recΔ` arguments:** For the recursion operator `recΔ`, assign an ordinal weight $\omega \cdot w(n) + \dots$ where $w(n)$ is the "natural number" depth of the third argument (with $w(\text{void}) = 1$ and $w(\text{delta } t) = w(t)+1$).
    *   `R_rec_zero` replaces $\text{recΔ}\,b\,s\,\text{void}$ (weight $\omega + \dots$) with $b$ (finite weight).
    *   `R_rec_succ` replaces $\text{recΔ}\,b\,s\,(\text{delta } n)$ (weight $\omega \cdot (w(n)+1) + \dots = \omega \cdot w(n) + \omega + \dots$) with $\text{app}\,s\,(\text{recΔ}\,b\,s\,n)$ (weight $\omega \cdot w(n) + \dots$). Since $\omega > \text{any finite size}(s)$, this strictly decreases the ordinal component.

3.  **Term size (number of constructors):** For rules that do not affect the first two components (`R_int_delta`, `R_merge_*`), the total syntactic size strictly decreases:
    *   `integrate (delta t) \to void` (size drops by at least 2)
    *   `merge` rules eliminate either a `void` argument or collapse a duplicate, strictly reducing size.

Since every rule decreases the lexicographic triple (eqW-count, recΔ-ordinal, size), and this ordering is well-founded, no infinite reduction sequence exists. The non-left-linear rule `R_merge_cancel` ($\text{merge}\,t\,t \to t$) is size-decreasing, and `R_eq_diff` (which temporarily increases size) is absorbed by the first component (eqW-count) decreasing. No hidden cycles exist because no rule re-introduces `eqW` or `recΔ` constructors at the top level once eliminated.
