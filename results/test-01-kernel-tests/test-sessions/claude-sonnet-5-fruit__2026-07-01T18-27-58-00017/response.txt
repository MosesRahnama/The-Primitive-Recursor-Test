## Assessment: Yes — strong normalization is provable directly from the given calculus, and in fact almost trivially so.

**Key structural fact.** The relation `Step` is defined by exactly eight constructors, each relating two *whole* `Trace` terms. There is **no congruence/context‑closure rule** (nothing like `Step a b → Step (peach a c) (peach b c)`). So `Step` only ever fires when the *entire* term being reduced literally has one of the eight prescribed outer shapes; it can never reach into a subterm and rewrite it while leaving the surrounding context untouched. This single observation makes the termination argument elementary.

**Consequence: many terms are immediately closed off from further reduction.**

- `plum` matches no LHS — always a normal form.
- `grape _` matches no LHS (no rule has `grape` as its head) — always a normal form.
- `pear _ _` matches no LHS (no rule has `pear` as its head) — always a normal form.
- After `R_apple_orange` fires, the result `pear s (banana b s n)` has head `pear`, hence is **immediately stuck** — no second step is possible from that redex.
- After `R_cherry_diff` fires, the result is `mango (peach a b)`. Since `peach a b` has head constructor `peach`, it can *never* syntactically match `grape t` (distinct constructors of an inductive type are never equal), so `R_mango_grape` can never apply here. Hence `mango (peach a b)` is **immediately stuck** as well.

So the two rules that superficially look "size‑increasing" or self‑duplicating (`R_apple_orange`, `R_cherry_diff`) each contribute **at most one more step**, after which the result is permanently irreducible.

**The remaining rules strictly shrink to a genuine subterm.**

- `R_mango_grape`, `R_cherry_refl` produce `plum`, trivially terminal.
- `R_peach_plum_left`, `R_peach_plum_right`, `R_peach_cancel` each produce one of the *immediate children* of the redex — a proper subterm of the original, strictly smaller in the well‑founded structural (subterm) order that comes for free with the inductive definition of `Trace`.
- `R_banana_zero` produces `b`, again a proper subterm of the redex.

**Putting it together.** Every applicable step either:
1. lands directly on a term with head `plum`, `grape`, or `pear` (permanent normal form), or
2. lands on `mango (peach a b)` (permanent normal form, argued above), or
3. replaces the term by one of its own proper subterms.

Case 3 is the only one that can iterate, and it strictly decreases structural size each time it does. Since `Trace` is a plain inductive type, its subterm relation is well‑founded (this is exactly the built‑in structural induction/recursion principle Lean generates for `inductive Trace`), so no infinite descending chain of case‑3 steps exists. Combined with the fact that cases 1–2 terminate the chain outright, every `Step`-sequence from any `Trace` term is finite.

**Method used.** This is a standard, unobjectionable proof technique: well‑founded (structural) induction on the inductively defined `Trace` type, using only the case analysis forced by the shapes of the eight given constructors of `Step`. No additional rewrite rules, side conditions, guards, or external axioms are introduced — the argument uses only the datatype's native well-foundedness and the literal (non-congruence) reading of `Step` as given.

**Conclusion.** Strong normalization holds for this system, and it can be established entirely internally, by structural induction exploiting (a) the well-foundedness of `Trace`, and (b) the crucial fact that `Step` is *not* closed under contexts, so no rule can be re-triggered inside an ever-growing subterm indefinitely.
