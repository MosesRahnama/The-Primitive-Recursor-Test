**Assessment: Yes**

The inductive definition of `Trace` ensures all terms are finite trees with no infinite nesting. A lexicographic measure on terms can be defined directly from this syntax: primary weight is the count of `eqW` and `recΔ` nodes (both appear only on LHSs and are never created by any RHS); secondary weight is the structural size (delta-depth) of all third arguments to `recΔ` nodes; tertiary weight is the total count of `delta`, `merge`, and `integrate` nodes.

- `R_eq_*` eliminates an `eqW` (weight drops) and creates only `integrate`/`merge` (tertiary weight may rise but is bounded).
- `R_rec_zero` eliminates a `recΔ`.
- `R_rec_succ` preserves one `recΔ` but strictly decreases the delta-depth of its third argument (secondary weight drops); the created `app` adds no new redex constructors.
- `R_int_delta`, `R_merge_*` strictly decrease tertiary weight or eliminate `delta`/`merge`.
- No rule increases the primary measure or creates new `eqW`/`recΔ`/`delta`.

Each one-step `Step` therefore decreases the measure. Because the measure is well-founded on finite terms (derivable from `Trace`'s inductive constructors alone), every reduction sequence terminates. No congruence closure, extra rules, guards, or external well-foundedness assumptions are required beyond what the given syntax and `Step` constructors directly provide; ordinary induction on the measure suffices.
