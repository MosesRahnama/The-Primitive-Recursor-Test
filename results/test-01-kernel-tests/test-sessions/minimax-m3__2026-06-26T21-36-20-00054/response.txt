## Assessment: Yes, strong normalization follows from the calculus alone.

**Reasoning (sketch of a standard proof).**

All eight rules are head-reductions; `app _ _` and `delta _` are inert (no rule has them at the root, and subterms under them are never at depth 0, so they cannot fire). This means every productive reduction lives at the root of the current term. Define a lex-ordered pair of measures $(α, β)$ by structural recursion on `Trace`:

```
α(void)            = 0
α(delta t)         = α(t)
α(integrate t)     = 1 + α(t)
α(merge t1 t2)     = 1 + max(α(t1), α(t2))   -- merge collapses to one branch
α(app t1 t2)       = α(t2)                    -- app is inert
α(recΔ b s n)      = 1 + α(b) + α(s) + α(n)
α(eqW a b)         = 2 + α(a) + α(b)         -- +2 so the eqW "credit" exceeds the integrate/merge it spawns

β(void)            = 0
β(delta t)         = 1 + β(t)                 -- delta is the fuel
β(integrate t)     = β(t)
β(merge t1 t2)     = max(β(t1), β(t2))
β(app t1 t2)       = β(t2)
β(recΔ b s n)      = β(n)                    -- fuel sits in the 3rd argument
β(eqW a b)         = max(β(a), β(b)) + 1      -- reducibility marker
```

Check each rule (LHS vs. RHS, lex order on $(α,β)$):

| Rule | α change | β change | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `R_int_delta` | $1+α(t) \to 0$ | — | $α$ strictly ↓ |
| `R_merge_void_left/right` | $1+α(t) \to α(t)$ | — | $α$ strictly ↓ |
| `R_merge_cancel` | $1+α(t) \to α(t)$ | — | $α$ strictly ↓ |
| `R_rec_zero` | $1+α(b)+α(s) \to α(b)$ | — | $α$ strictly ↓ |
| `R_rec_succ` | equal | $1+β(n) \to β(n)$ | $α$ equal, $β$ strictly ↓ |
| `R_eq_refl` | $2+2α(a) \to 0$ | — | $α$ strictly ↓ |
| `R_eq_diff` | $2+α(a)+α(b) \to 2+\max(α(a),α(b))$; if $\min(α(a),α(b))=0$, then $α$ equal, $β: \max+1 \to \max$ | either $α$ ↓ or $β$ ↓ | lex strictly ↓ |

Both components are well-founded natural numbers, hence $(α, β)$ with lex order is a well-founded order, and **every rule is a strict decrease** in that order. Termination follows immediately by the usual measure argument. No extra rewrite rule, side condition, guard, or external axiom is needed.
