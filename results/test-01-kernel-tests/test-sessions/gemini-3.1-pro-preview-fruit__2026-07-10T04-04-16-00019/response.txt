The inductive type `Trace` and the `Step` relation define a first-order Term Rewriting System (TRS). *   `⟦ mango t ⟧ = ⟦ t ⟧`

By structural induction, we can trivially verify that `⟦ t ⟧ ≥ 1` for all valid terms `t`, and that all constructors are strictly monotonic with respect to their arguments. We can then check that every rewrite rule in `Step` strictly decreases this numerical measure (LHS > RHS):

1.  **`R_mango_grape`**: `⟦ t ⟧ + 1 > 1`
2.  **`R_peach_plum_left`**: `1 + ⟦ t ⟧ > ⟦ t ⟧`
