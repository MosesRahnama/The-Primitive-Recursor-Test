This pattern of structural recursion on a subterm is the key feature that well-behaved terminating systems exhibit. In an RPO, with a precedence `recΔ > app`, this rule is oriented correctly because the third argument of `recΔ` decreases lexicographically (`delta n > n` since `n` is a subterm).
