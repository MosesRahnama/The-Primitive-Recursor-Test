**Yes**

A suitable termination measure can be defined directly from the inductive syntax of `Trace` and the given `Step` rules (via compatible closure). Order terms first by the multiset of all `eqW` and `recΔ` occurrences, weighted by a lexicographic tuple of:
- the `delta`-height (nesting depth of `delta` constructors) appearing in every third argument of `recΔ`,
- the total number of `delta` constructors (non-increasing under every rule except controlled duplication by `R_rec_succ`),
- ordinary term size (number of constructors).

Every rule decreases this measure:
- `R_eq_refl`/`R_eq_diff` eliminate an `eqW` and introduce neither fresh `eqW` nor `recΔ`;
- `R_rec_zero` eliminates a `recΔ`;
- `R_rec_succ` decreases the `delta`-height of the recursor argument while the extra copy of `s` is multiplied only by a strictly smaller height (standard primitive-recursion ordinal weighting, e.g., ω raised to the new height);
- the four `merge`/`integrate` rules strictly decrease `delta` count or size and never re-introduce `eqW` or `recΔ`.

No rule creates new `delta`, `eqW`, or top-level `recΔ` outside the strictly descending cases above, so every reduction sequence is finite. The proof uses only induction on the syntax, multiset extension of the well-founded order on naturals, and the listed rules; no extra rewrite rules, side conditions, or non-derivable axioms are required.
