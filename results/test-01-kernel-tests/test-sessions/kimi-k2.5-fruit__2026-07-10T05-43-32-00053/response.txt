This is exactly the recursion pattern of Gödel's System T or primitive recursors for inductive types. The "fuel" for the recursion—the depth of the third argument—strictly decreases with each application of the step rule. Even though duplication can increase the syntactic size or the number of embedded redexes, the well-foundedness of the structural ordering on `Trace` ensures termination.

Each reduction decreases this measure.
