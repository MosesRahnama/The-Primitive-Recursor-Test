The key point is that `Step` is only the root-step relation shown; there is no congruence/contextual closure rule allowing reduction inside arguments.

For every rule, its target is either:

- a strict syntactic subterm of the source:
  - `peach plum t → t`
  - `peach t plum → t`
  - `peach t t → t`
  - `banana b s plum → b`
- or a `Step`-normal term at the root:
  - `mango (grape t) → plum`

A structural-induction/accessibility proof over `Trace` therefore proves that every term is accessible under `Step`, hence there is no infinite `Step` sequence.

This conclusion relies only on the exact relation presented. It does not assert normalization for an unshown contextual closure of `Step`; such closure is not part of the supplied calculus.
