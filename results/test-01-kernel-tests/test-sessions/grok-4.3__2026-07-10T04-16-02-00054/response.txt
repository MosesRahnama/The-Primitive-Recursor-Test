The TRS is first-order and constructor-based. All redexes are linear and non-overlapping except for the two eqW rules, which are resolved by prioritizing the reflexive case (a standard innermost or lex-smallest strategy). A termination measure is obtained by lexicographic descent on the 4-tuple  
(#eqW, #recΔ, multiset of delta-nesting depths inside recΔ arguments, total term size).  
Each rule strictly decreases this tuple: eqW rules reduce the first component, recΔ-unfolding reduces the second or third, and every other rule reduces size while leaving the preceding components unchanged. Because every Trace term is finite, the measure is well-founded and no infinite reduction sequence exists.

Final answer: yes (via lex measure on eqW/recΔ counts + sizes)
