## Assessment: **Yes** — strong normalization is provable from the presented calculus alone.

## Reasoning

**The system is strongly normalizing.** Each rule is locally size-bounded and the only "productive" rule, `R_apple_orange`, peels a single `grape` from the *third* argument of a `banana` while duplicating `s` inside a `pear`. Since `pear` is inert (no rule has `pear` on the LHS), the duplicated `s` is frozen and cannot feed more `R_apple_orange` steps. The "growth" rule `R_cherry_diff` increases the term by one constructor, but the resulting `mango (peach a b)` is a normal form unless `a` or `b` is `plum`, in which case at most one further `peach` step is possible — and then `mango` is again inert unless its argument is `grape _`. So `cherry` chains have bounded length (≤ 2 steps to a stuck form or `plum`).

**The proof goes through a well-founded measure.** A workable one is a lexicographic triple:

1. For each `banana b s n` subterm, the grape-depth of `n` (number of leading `grape` wrappings; 0 if `n` is not of the form `grape _`). Sum this over all banana subterms.
2. The number of `cherry` subterms whose two arguments are not syntactically equal.
3. The total syntactic size.

Each rule strictly decreases this triple under the lexicographic order (which is well-founded on `ℕ³`):

- `R_mango_grape`, `R_peach_*`, `R_cherry_refl`, `R_banana_zero`: size drops.
- `R_apple_orange`: the active banana's third-arg grape depth drops by 1; the duplicated `s` is wrapped in a `pear` and contributes no further banana-subterm to the sum, so the total grape-depth sum strictly drops.
- `R_cherry_diff`: the cherry count drops by 1.

**No extra axioms are needed.** In Lean 4, the induction is carried out with `Nat.lt_wfRel` / `WellFoundedRecursion` over this measure; the only data required are the inductive definitions of `Trace` and `Step` as given. Nothing about the rewrite system itself (confluence, unique normal forms, productivity) needs to be assumed — those are not prerequisites for SN, and the calculus does not provide them in any case (e.g., `cherry plum (mango (grape t))` has two distinct normal forms, `plum` and `mango (mango (grape t))`, so confluence fails, but this is irrelevant to termination).

So within the stated boundary, SN is derivable.
