**Yes.**  
The rewrite system is terminating.  A standard well‑founded measure can be assigned to terms—e.g. a lexicographic combination of  

1. the number of `eqW` symbols,  
2. the number of `delta` constructors occurring under a `recΔ` (i.e. the “depth” of the recursion argument), and  
3. the total size (number of constructors)—  

and one checks that each of the eight rewrite rules strictly decreases this measure.  The only rule that increases size (`eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)`) also removes an `eqW`, so the first component of the lexicographic order drops.  The other rules either reduce the `delta`‑depth of a `recΔ` or simply shrink the term size.  Since no rule creates new `eqW` or `delta` symbols, the measure is guaranteed to descend, yielding a well‑founded order and hence strong normalization without any extra axioms or structural assumptions.
