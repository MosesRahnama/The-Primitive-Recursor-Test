**Assessment:** No.

The calculus as given cannot establish strong normalization from its syntax and rules alone. The core obstacle is R_cherry_diff: it is size-increasing (cherry a b rewrites to a term containing both mango and peach) and is guarded by an external disequality premise a ≠ b. Any termination metric or interpretation must therefore simultaneously (i) justify that the premise is always decidable from the inductive definition and (ii) guarantee that the expansion cannot be repeated indefinitely. Both requirements lie outside the rewrite rules themselves; they presuppose either an auxiliary well-founded measure on Trace or an external lemma about constructor injectivity and distinctness that is not derivable from the eight Step constructors. Standard syntactic methods (lexicographic path order, recursive path order, or plain size induction) fail exactly on this rule, and no other rule supplies a compensating contraction that would restore a decreasing measure. Consequently, SN is not provable inside the presented system without importing additional structure.

(The remaining rules are all strictly decreasing on a natural size measure and would be SN by themselves; the single conditional expansion is what blocks a self-contained argument.)
