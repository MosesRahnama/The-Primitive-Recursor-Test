Yes, strong normalization can be established directly from the given Lean 4 definitions without any extra axioms or structural strengthening.

**Reasoning**
The rewrite relation `Step` is defined only on the whole term (no congruence rules for rewriting inside subterms). Examining each rule:

- `R_mango_grape`, `R_cherry_refl`, `R_apple_orange`, `R_cherry_diff` produce terms (`plum`, `pear …`, `mango (peach …)`) that do not match any left‑hand side of the rules. Hence these terms are irreducible, and any sequence using them stops after at most one step.
- `R_banana_zero` reduces `banana b s plum` to `b`. The right‑hand side `b` is a proper subterm, which may itself be reducible, but only via the remaining rules.
- `R_peach_plum_left`, `R_peach_plum_right`, `R_peach_cancel` reduce a `peach` term to a proper subterm `t`. Again any possible continuation must originate from that strictly smaller subterm.

Therefore, after at most one application of a “dead‑end” rule, every subsequent step applies one of `R_peach_plum_left`, `R_peach_plum_right`, `R_peach_cancel`, or `R_banana_zero`, each of which strictly decreases the syntactic size of the term (number of constructors). Since terms are finite, no infinite reduction sequence can exist. The system is strongly normalizing.
