Yes. Strong normalization can be established directly from the given syntax and rules without any external assumptions. The calculus lacks congruence (reduction only at the root), so every step replaces the entire term with a new one. All rules either:

- produce **void**, which is terminal;
- produce a term headed by **app** or **integrate** (non-redex constructors), which are normal forms (rec_succ, eq_diff);
- produce a strict subterm (merge rules, rec_zero, int_delta), decreasing size.

Since terms are finite, the size decrease bounds the length of any reduction chain. No terms can cause infinite sequences, and no additional structural axioms or side conditions are needed for the proof.
