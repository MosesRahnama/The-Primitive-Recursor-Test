**No**

The rewrite rules alone do not suffice. All rules except the three projections (peach rules, banana-zero) produce terms that are irreducible under Step: plum, pear _ _, or mango (peach _ _). Chains therefore continue only by descending to a proper subterm (structural decrease). However, proving those rhs truly irreducible requires that distinct constructors are unequal (peach ≠ grape, pear has no matching lhs, etc.). Those disjointness/injectivity facts are structural axioms about the inductive syntax; they cannot be derived from the Step constructors or their side condition a ≠ b. Without them, models with additional identifications exist in which rhs remain reducible, permitting infinite chains, so SN is not provable from the given calculus. Simple size or weight measures also fail (banana-succ and cherry-diff can increase size), confirming no purely syntactic termination argument works inside the stated boundary.
