## Practical tip for finishing

The only hard parts are the two bounding lemmas (`hLHS_le_omegaK`) and the exponent-gap lemmas (`hK_lt_E`). If the exact `opow_*` lemma names differ, keep the structure and swap in available monotonicity lemmas (`pow_le_pow_right`, `opow_le_opow_right`, `opow_lt_opow_right`, etc.).
