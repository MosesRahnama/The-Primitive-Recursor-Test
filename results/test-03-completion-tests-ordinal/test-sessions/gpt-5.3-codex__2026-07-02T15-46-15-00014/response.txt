### 3) `R_eq_diff` skeleton

| _, _, Step.R_eq_diff a b => by
  simp [mu]
  /-
    Goal shape:

    omega0 ^ (4:Ordinal) *
      (omega0 ^ (3:Ordinal) * (mu a + 1) + omega0 ^ (2:Ordinal) * (mu b + 1) + 1 + 1) + 1
    <
    omega0 ^ (mu a + mu b + (9:Ordinal)) + 1
  -/

  let F : Ordinal := mu a + mu b + (9 : Ordinal)

  -- Step 1: show the multiplicative block is < omega0 ^ F
  have hmain :
      omega0 ^ (4 : Ordinal) *
        (omega0 ^ (3 : Ordinal) * (mu a + 1) + omega0 ^ (2 : Ordinal) * (mu b + 1) + 1 + 1)
      < omega0 ^ F := by
    -- prove inner parenthesis is bounded by some small power, e.g. < omega0^(mu a + mu b + 5),
    -- then multiplying by omega0^4 gives < omega0^(...+9)=omega0^F
    -- uses monotonicity/exponent-addition arithmetic (`opow_add`-style lemmas)

  -- Step 2: add `+1` on both sides
  have hplus :
      omega0 ^ (4 : Ordinal) *
        (omega0 ^ (3 : Ordinal) * (mu a + 1) + omega0 ^ (2 : Ordinal) * (mu b + 1) + 1 + 1) + 1

## Practical note

The two hard cases (`rec_succ`, `eq_diff`) usually need a small local lemma library like:

- monotonicity of `(^)` in exponent for base `omega0` (`opow_le_opow_right`-style),
- positivity of powers (`opow_pos`),
- bounds `x < x+1`, `x ≤ A*x` for positive `A`,
- adding/multiplying preserves inequalities on positive ordinals,
- optionally normal-form facts about `omega0^k` dominance.
