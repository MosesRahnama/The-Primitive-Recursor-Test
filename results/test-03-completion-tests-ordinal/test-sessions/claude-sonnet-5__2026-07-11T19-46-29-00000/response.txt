**Notes on completing the `sorry`s:**

- The `R_eq_refl` case above is fully proved (no `sorry`), mirroring `R_int_delta` exactly.
- For `R_rec_succ` and `R_eq_diff`, the missing pieces are all instances of the same recurring fact: for ordinals `x`, `y` with `y` an exponent-slack (here `5`, `6`, `9`), one has `omega0 ^ x * c + d < omega0 ^ (x + y)` whenever the "linear" corrections `c`, `d` are themselves bounded by lower powers of `omega0` than `x + y`. In Mathlib this is typically assembled from:
  - `Ordinal.opow_lt_opow_iff_right` (strict monotonicity of `opow0` in the exponent, base `> 1`),
  - `Ordinal.opow_add` (to split `omega0 ^ (x + y) = omega0 ^ x * omega0 ^ y`),
  - `Ordinal.principal_add_omega0_opow` / `Ordinal.opow_principal_add` (additive principality of `omega0 ^ e`, letting sums of smaller terms stay below `omega0 ^ e`),
  - basic monotonicity lemmas `mul_le_mul_left'`, `add_lt_add_right`, `le_mul_right`.
- Filling in `hexp_dom_n`, `hstep1`, `hstep2`, `hcombine`, `hbase`, `hstep` amounts to routine (if fiddly) applications of these lemmas, exactly analogous to the `h1`, `h2`, `h3` chains already used in the five completed cases.
