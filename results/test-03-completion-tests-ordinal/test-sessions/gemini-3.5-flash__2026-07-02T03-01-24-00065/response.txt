### Mathematical Insights Used in the Skeletons:
1. **`R_eq_refl`**: Simplifies directly to proving $0 < \alpha + 1$, which is handled by transitively applying $0 < 1 \le \alpha + 1$ via `le_add_left`.
2. **Left-Distributivity of Ordinal Multiplication**: While right-distributivity $(A+B)C = AC + BC$ is false for ordinals, left-distributivity $A(B+C) = AB + AC$ is a theorem in Mathlib (`mul_add`). This is used in `R_eq_diff` and `R_rec_succ` to cleanly flatten the LHS expressions.
3. **Exponent Dominance**: For any ordinals $\beta < \gamma$, any finite sum of terms bounded by $\omega^\beta$ is strictly dominated by $\omega^\gamma$. This is the core driving property behind both `R_eq_diff` and `R_rec_succ`.
