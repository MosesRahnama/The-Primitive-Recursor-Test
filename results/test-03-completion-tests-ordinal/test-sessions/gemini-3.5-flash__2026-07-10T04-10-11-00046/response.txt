### Key Mathlib Lemmas for Completion
To fill in the `sorry` parts in the skeleton, the following standard Ordinal library lemmas are key:
* `Ordinal.opow_lt_opow_iff_right` to transform power inequalities $x^a < x^b$ into exponent inequalities $a < b$.
* `Ordinal.add_lt_add_iff_right` and `Ordinal.add_lt_add_left` for canceling common additives.
* `Ordinal.mul_lt_mul_of_pos_left` to distribute multiplication strictly over strict inequalities.
* `Ordinal.le_add_right` / `Ordinal.le_add_left` to introduce minor terms during upper bounding.
