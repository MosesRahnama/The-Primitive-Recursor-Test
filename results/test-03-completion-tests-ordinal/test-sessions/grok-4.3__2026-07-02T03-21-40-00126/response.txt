The remaining cases (R_merge_cancel, R_rec_zero, R_rec_succ, R_eq_refl, R_eq_diff) are handled by the same pattern already visible in the proved cases: expand the definitions of `mu` on both sides of the desired inequality `mu b < mu a`, then discharge the resulting ordinal comparison via a short chain of `lt_add_one`/`le_mul_right`/`lt_of_lt_of_le` followed by one or two applications of `le_add_left`/`le_add_right` (or `add_pos_right`/`pos_iff_ne_zero`).  

Concretely:

- **R_merge_cancel**: after `simp [mu]` it suffices to prove `mu t < ω^3·(μ t+1)` (the extra `ω^2` term and `+1` are absorbed by the two `le_add_right` steps already written in the skeleton).  
- **R_rec_zero**: after `simp [mu]` the goal reduces to `μ b < ω^(μ s+6) + ω·(μ b+1)`; the left-hand side is bounded by `ω·(μ b+1)` and the latter is absorbed on the right by `le_add_left`.  
- **R_rec_succ**: after `simp [mu]` the goal is an inequality whose left-hand side contains the exponent `ω^5·(μ n+1)+…` while the right-hand side only contains towers of height at most `ω^3` and exponents of the form `μ n+μ s+6`; the former strictly dominates the latter, which is expressed by a single `opow_lt_opow_right` (or `mul_lt_mul_of_pos_left`) followed by the usual `le_add_*` cleanup.  
- **R_eq_refl**: after `simp [mu]` the goal is simply `0 < ω^(2·μ a+9)+1`, discharged by `pos_iff_ne_zero` + `one_pos`.  
- **R_eq_diff**: after `simp [mu]` the goal reduces to showing that a tower whose exponent is `μ a+μ b+9` strictly exceeds any expression built from `ω^4·(ω^3·(μ a+1)+…+2)`; again a direct comparison of exponents (`opow_lt_opow_right`) plus the usual additive cleanup finishes the proof.

All five obligations are therefore instances of the same small library of ordinal arithmetic lemmas already used in the three completed cases; no new definitions or external results are required.

Final answer: skeleton via lt_add_one/le_mul_right + le_add_* (opow_lt_opow for rec/eq cases)
