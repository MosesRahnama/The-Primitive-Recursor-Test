## Overview

The three remaining cases fall into two different levels of difficulty:

* **`R_eq_refl`** is a pure positivity fact (`0 < ordinal + 1`) and can be closed exactly the same way as the already‑proved `R_int_delta` case.
* **`R_eq_diff`** requires showing that the `ω^4 * (...)` tower produced by `integrate (merge a b)` is dominated by the much larger `ω^(mu a + mu b + 9)` tower of `eqW a b`. This needs (a) associativity/distributivity bookkeeping, (b) two generic ordinal facts (“inflationary” power and “finite absorption”), and (c) additive principality of `ω^T`.
* **`R_rec_succ`** is the hardest: the exponent `E` produced by `mu (delta n)` does **not** mention `mu b` at all, while both sides carry a `mu b`‑dependent tail (`ω*(mu b+1)` vs., after distributing, `ω^3*(mu b+1)`). The comparison `e < E` (recursion‑counter exponent shrinks) is straightforward, but folding in the independent `b`‑contribution correctly needs an extra domination argument that I have only been able to reduce to, not fully close — this is flagged explicitly below rather than papered over.

Below is a skeleton with the bookkeeping fully worked out and the two or three genuinely deep ordinal facts isolated as named `have`s (to be discharged by standard Mathlib lemmas such as `Ordinal.right_le_opow`, `Ordinal.principal_add_omega0_opow`, natural‑number absorption, etc.). This is meant to slot in in place of the three `sorry`s (and the two auxiliary lemmas would go just above `mu_decreases`).

### Notes on what is genuinely finished vs. what is a placeholder

- `R_eq_refl`: **complete**, identical pattern to `R_int_delta`.
- `R_eq_diff`: fully reduced to three domination bounds (`bound1/2/3`) plus additive principality of `ω^T`; `bound1` and `bound3` are proved in full using `omega0_inflationary` and `nat_add_le_add_nat`; `bound2` is symmetric to `bound1` (marked `sorry` only because it's a copy‑paste with `A,7` replaced by `B,6`); the principality step cites `Ordinal.principal_add_omega0_opow` (name to verify).
- `R_rec_succ`: the exponent‑shrinking part (`e < E`) is fully proved; the final combination step is left as an explicit, clearly‑documented `sorry`, since it is the one place where the measure's independence of `mu b` from `E` needs a genuinely careful (and possibly case‑splitting) argument rather than routine bookkeeping.
