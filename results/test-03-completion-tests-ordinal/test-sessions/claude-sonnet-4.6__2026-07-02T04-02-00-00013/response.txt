## Case Analysis

### `R_eq_refl`: Show `0 < omega^(mu a + mu a + 9) + 1`

This is straightforward positivity.

### `R_rec_succ`: Show `mu(app s (recDelta b s n)) < mu(recDelta b s (delta n))`

The LHS expands to `omega^3*(mu s+1) + omega^2*(omega^(mu n+mu s+6)+omega*(mu b+1)+2) + 1`.
The RHS is `omega^(omega^5*(mu n+1)+1+mu s+6) + omega*(mu b+1) + 1`.

The key: the RHS exponent `omega^5*(mu n+1)+mu s+7` greatly exceeds anything in LHS.

### `R_eq_diff`: Show `mu(integrate(merge a b)) < mu(eqW a b)`

LHS = `omega^4*(omega^3*(mu a+1)+omega^2*(mu b+1)+2) + 1`, which is at most `omega^(4+3+1) = omega^8` times a coefficient. RHS = `omega^(mu a+mu b+9)+1`. The exponent `mu a+mu b+9 >= 9` while LHS exponent arithmetic yields at most `omega^7`-class terms.
