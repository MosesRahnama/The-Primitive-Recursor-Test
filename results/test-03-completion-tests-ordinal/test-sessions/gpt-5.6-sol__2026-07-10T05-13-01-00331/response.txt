The proof cannot be completed as stated: the `R_rec_succ` case is false for the given measure. A large `mu s` can absorb the contribution from `mu (delta n)` in ordinal addition.

Let
```lean
v := Trace.void
d := Trace.delta v
s := Trace.delta d
```
and write `ω` for `omega0`. Then
\[
\mu(d)=\omega^5+1
\]
and
\[
S:=\mu(s)
 =\omega^5(\omega^5+2)+1
 =\omega^{10}+\omega^5\cdot2+1.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\mu(d)+S=S
\]
because the leading \(\omega^{10}\) term of `S` absorbs `μ d`.

Set \(E=S+6\). With `b = n = void`, the source of `R_rec_succ` has measure
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mu(\operatorname{recDelta}\ v\ s\ d)
 &=\omega^{\mu(d)+S+6}+\omega+1\\
 &=\omega^E+\omega+1.
\end{aligned}
\]
The recursive call has exactly the same measure:
\[
\mu(\operatorname{recDelta}\ v\ s\ v)
 =\omega^{0+S+6}+\omega+1
 =\omega^E+\omega+1.
\]

Its enclosing `app` has measure
\[
\omega^3(S+1)+
\omega^2(\omega^E+\omega+2)+1.
\]
Here \(2+E=E\), so
\[
\omega^2\omega^E=\omega^{2+E}=\omega^E.
\]
Moreover, \(\omega^3(S+1)<\omega^E\), and hence it is absorbed when added before the \(\omega^E\) term. Therefore the target measure is
\[
\omega^E+\omega^3+\omega^2\cdot2+1,
\]
which is strictly greater than
\[
\omega^E+\omega+1.
\]

Thus this instance of the rule satisfies
\[
\mu(\operatorname{recDelta}\ v\ s\ d)
<
\mu(\operatorname{app}\ s\ (\operatorname{recDelta}\ v\ s\ v)),
\]
the reverse of the required inequality.

The `eq_refl` hole itself is routine, for example:
```lean
| _, _, Step.R_eq_refl a => by
    simp [mu]
    exact add_pos_right _ one_pos
```
or, if simplification leaves a slightly different goal shape:
```lean
| _, _, Step.R_eq_refl a => by
    simp [mu]
    apply Ordinal.pos_iff_ne_zero.mpr
    exact ne_of_gt (add_pos_right _ one_pos)
```

An `eq_diff` ordinal-bound proof may also be possible, but no proof skeleton can complete `mu_decreases` because of the counterexample above. The `recDelta` measure must be redesigned—specifically, its encoding of the counter and step argument must preserve a strict decrease in the counter even when `mu s` is large, rather than using `mu n + mu s`, where the left contribution can be absorbed.
