Looking at the three remaining cases, I need to analyze what each `simp [mu]` reduces to and provide proofs.

## Case 1: `R_eq_refl` — `eqW a a → void`

Need: `mu void < mu (eqW a a)`, i.e., `0 < omega^(mu a + mu a + 9) + 1`.

This is straightforward positivity.

## Case 2: `R_rec_succ` — `recDelta b s (delta n) → app s (recDelta b s n)`

Need: `mu (app s (recDelta b s n)) < mu (recDelta b s (delta n))`.

## Case 3: `R_eq_diff` — `eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)`

Need: `mu (integrate (merge a b)) < mu (eqW a b)`.
