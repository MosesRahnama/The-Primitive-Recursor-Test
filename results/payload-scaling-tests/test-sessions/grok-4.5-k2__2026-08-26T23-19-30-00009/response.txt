The given system is a first-order TRS over finite terms. It is strongly normalizing by a direct syntactic argument that uses only the two rules as written:

- Order the function symbols by the precedence \(F > G > S > Z\).
- The recursive path order (or the multiset path order) induced by this precedence orients both rules strictly left-to-right.
- The well-foundedness of that order rests solely on the fact that every term is finite (the subterm relation is well-founded); no induction principles, injectivity/no-confusion axioms for \(Z\)/\(S\), or any other object-level theory are required.

Consequently every rewrite sequence is finite.
