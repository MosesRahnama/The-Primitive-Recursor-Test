### Reasoning

The system has one defined symbol (`F`) and three constructor/uninterpreted symbols (`G`, `S`, `Z`), which have no rewrite rules of their own.

**Structural analysis of the rules:**

| Rule | Requirement | Verification |
|------|------------|--------------|
| 1 | `[x] + ([y]+1)·9¹ + 1 > [x]` | `9[y] + 10 > 0` ✓ |
