The given TRS is strongly normalizing on the free term algebra generated by the signature. The only redexes are rooted at \(F\). Rule 1 replaces an \(F\)-term by a proper subterm. Rule 2 replaces \(F(x,y,S(n))\) by a term whose single recursive \(F\)-subterm is \(F(x,y,n)\), i.e., the third argument is a proper subterm of the original third argument. \(G\) (and the constructors \(Z,S\)) carry no rules, so they introduce no new redexes.

Consequently every reduction sequence strictly decreases a simple well-founded measure (e.g., the multiset of third-argument depths of all \(F\)-redexes, or a recursive-path-order ranking with precedence \(F>G\)). The measure is defined directly from the syntax of the finite terms and the two rewrite rules; no extra object-level equations, induction principles on an external sort of numbers, or structural axioms beyond the ordinary inductive definition of terms are required.
