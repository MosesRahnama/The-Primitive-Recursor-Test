The system is SN. The two rules involve a single recursive function symbol \(F\) whose reduction either deletes an \(F\) outright (first rule) or replaces an \(F\)-redex by an inert \(G\)-term containing exactly one recursive call on a proper subterm of the third argument (second rule). No rule introduces new \(S\)-constructors, and \(G\) has no rules, so the only possible redexes are \(F\)-redexes. A simple exponential interpretation (or the multiset extension of the strict subterm order on third arguments of all \(F\)-occurrences, lexicographically combined with term size) is therefore strictly decreased by every rewrite step. The interpretation is read off directly from the given rules on the free term algebra; no additional structural axioms, induction principles, or object-level assumptions are required.
