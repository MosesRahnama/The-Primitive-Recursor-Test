Yes. Strong normalization can be established directly from the given rewrite rules.

Reason: the only recursive F-rule is

F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(y, y, y, y, y, y, y, y, F(x, y, n))

and its recursive call has third argument `n`, an immediate subterm of `S(n)`. There are no rules for `G` or `S` that could generate new `S`-terms or otherwise reverse this structural decrease. A recursive path ordering with precedence `F > G` also orients both rules, so SN follows syntactically from the rules alone.
