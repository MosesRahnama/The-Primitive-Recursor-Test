**No.**

The given rules alone determine a one-step reduction relation on first-order terms. Strong normalization is the meta-level statement that every term admits only finite reduction sequences under that relation. Any proof of this fact must appeal to induction on term structure (or an equivalent well-founded measure, e.g., the number of leading `S` constructors on the third argument of `F`, together with the fact that `G` is inert). Those induction/well-foundedness principles are structural assumptions about the ambient term algebra; they are not derivable from the two rewrite rules themselves.
