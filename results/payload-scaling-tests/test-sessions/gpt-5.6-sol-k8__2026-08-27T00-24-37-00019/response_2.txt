4. **Strong normalization itself:** Yes, I still believe the TRS is strongly normalizing. Each recursive \(F\)-call replaces \(S(n)\) by \(n\); the surrounding \(G\) introduces no further rewrite rule, and although \(y\) is duplicated, only finitely many copies are introduced at each finite recursion depth. This is a meta-level termination argument, distinct from claiming that termination is internally derivable using only the rewrite rules.
