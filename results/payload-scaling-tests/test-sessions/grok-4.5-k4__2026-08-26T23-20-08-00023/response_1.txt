**No.**

The rules alone do not suffice to establish strong normalization. Any proof that every reduction sequence is finite must appeal to an external well-founded measure (term size, multiset of third-argument depths, RPO/LPO status, etc.) or to structural induction over the free term algebra. Those justifications are not derivable from the two rewrite rules; they are imported meta-level / structural assumptions about how terms are built. (The duplication of the second argument is harmless once such a measure is supplied, but the measure itself lies outside the given system.)
