Yes. The rewrite system is strongly normalizing on finite first-order terms using only its syntactic structure.

A recursive path ordering can orient both rules, taking \(F \succ G\) and giving \(F\) lexicographic status:

- \(F(x,y,Z) \succ x\) by the subterm property.
- For the second rule, \(F(x,y,S(n)) \succ F(x,y,n)\), since \(S(n) \succ n\); it also exceeds each occurrence of \(y\). Hence, with \(F \succ G\),
  \[
  F(x,y,S(n)) \succ G(y,y,y,y,F(x,y,n)).
  \]

Although the rule duplicates \(y\), the ordering still strictly decreases at every rewrite step. Thus no additional axioms or object-level assumptions are needed.
