**Reasoning.** The system is compatible with the Lexicographic Path Ordering (LPO) using the single precedence **F > G** (and no other ordering assumptions).  
- Rule 1 orients because the right‑hand side *x* is a proper subterm of the left‑hand side, so `F(x,y,Z) > x`.

In particular, for the recursive call `F(x,y,n)`, the top symbols agree and the argument tuples compare lexicographically: the first two arguments are identical, and the third argument `S(n)` is larger than its proper subterm `n`. By the subterm property the left‑hand side is also larger than `x`, `y`, and `n`, hence larger than each of the four copies of `y`.

Since LPO is a well‑founded simplification ordering closed under contexts and substitutions, containment of the rewrite relation in LPO proves strong normalization directly. No induction on natural numbers, no axioms for `Z`/`S`, and no imported structural axioms are needed.
