**No.**

The rules alone define a rewrite relation that is intuitively terminating (the second rule peels one `S` from the third argument of `F` while the first rule eliminates `F` at `Z`; `G` has no rules). Establishing *strong* normalization (every term has only finite rewrite sequences), however, requires a well-foundedness/induction argument on term structure (or an equivalent termination order/measure whose well-foundedness is not given by the two rules). Those structural principles are not derivable from the rewrite rules themselves.
