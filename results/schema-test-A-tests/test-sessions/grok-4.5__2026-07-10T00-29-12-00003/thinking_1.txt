The problem asks: can strong normalization of this system be established from the presented rewrite system alone, without importing structural axioms or object-level assumptions beyond what is derivab...Yes, strong normalization can be established from the given rules alone.

The key is that every reduction strictly decreases a well-founded measure on the third argument of F. Define the size of a term t (built from Z, S, variables, G, and F) as the number of S-symbols plus the number of Z 0's in its constructor structure (ignoring G and F subterms). For any F-term, its "height" is the size of its third argument.

- The base rule F(x,y,Z) → x replaces an F of height 0 with x (height 0).
- The recursive rule F(x,y S(n)) → G(y, F(x,y,n)) replaces an F of height h+1 with a G-term whose F-subterm has height h.
