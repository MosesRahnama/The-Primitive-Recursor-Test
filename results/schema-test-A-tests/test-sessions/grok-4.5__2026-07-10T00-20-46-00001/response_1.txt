### Assessment
Yes, strong normalization (SN) of the given TRS can be established from the rules alone.

### Reasoning
The signature is the free first-order term algebra generated by the symbols appearing in the rules (ternary \(F\), binary \(G\), unary \(S\), constant \(Z\)) together with the variables. The rewrite relation is the standard one generated by the two rules (closed under contexts and substitutions); there are no further equations, no binding, and no side conditions.

Both rules are oriented by the recursive path order with
- precedence \(F > G\) (and \(F\) larger than the constructors),
- lexicographic status on the arguments of \(F\) (left-to-right).

- Rule 1 is immediate by the sub-term property.
- Rule 2 holds because the head symbols decrease (\(F > G\)) and the sole recursive call satisfies \(F(x,y,n) <_{\mathrm{rpo}} F(x,y,S(n))\) by the third arguments (\(n <_{\mathrm{rpo}} S(n)\)).

A well-founded order compatible with the rules yields SN of the whole TRS. No external well-foundedness hypothesis on a sort of “naturals”, no induction axiom, and no interpretation of \(S\)/\(Z\) beyond the free term model are required—the ordering argument works for arbitrary finite terms.
