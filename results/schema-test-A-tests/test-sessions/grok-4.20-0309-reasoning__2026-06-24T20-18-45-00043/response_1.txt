The rules encode a recursive unfolding of F that peels S constructors (or bottoms out at Z) while introducing G (which has no rules). However, proving there cannot be infinite chains requires well-foundedness of the inductive structure generated by Z/S (i.e., every ground term of that sort is reachable by finite application of those constructors, with no infinite descent). This is a structural axiom on the signature that is not entailed by the given rules alone; without it, termination is not guaranteed in arbitrary models. (Standard finiteness of terms is itself such an assumption.)
