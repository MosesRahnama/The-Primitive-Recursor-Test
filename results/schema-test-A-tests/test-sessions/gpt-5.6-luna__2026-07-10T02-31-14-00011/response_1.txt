Yes. Strong normalization can be established directly from the rewrite rules, without importing structural axioms or object-level assumptions.

The recursive rule strictly decreases the third argument: a term of the form `F(x,y,S(n))` rewrites to a term containing `F(x,y,n)`. The base rule eliminates `F` when the third argument is `Z`. A suitable recursive path ordering, for example with precedence `F > G` and using the subterm ordering for `S(n) > n`, or an equivalent well-founded measure, orients both rules from left to right.

Thus there are no infinite rewrite sequences, including for terms containing variables.
