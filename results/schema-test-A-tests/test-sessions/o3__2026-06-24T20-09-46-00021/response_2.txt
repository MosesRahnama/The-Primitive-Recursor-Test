1. Primary method previously used  
   A simple termination measure: every term was mapped to an ordered pair of natural numbers (number-of-leading-S’s occurring in F-arguments, then number of F-nodes) and rewrite steps were shown to decrease this pair lexicographically.  In termination literature this is just a well-founded (multiset / lexicographic) ordering proof.

2. Does the method rely on anything not fixed by the rules themselves?  
   It relies on choosing that particular interpretation / ordering on terms; the ordering is not dictated by the rules, it is picked by the proof writer.

3. Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?  
   No. The boundary forbids adding object-level axioms or extra rewrite rules; picking a well-founded meta-level ordering is standard proof apparatus and does not enrich the object theory.  Hence the method remains inside the allowed boundary.

4. Independent of boundary issues, do I still judge the system strongly normalizing?  
   Yes — I still believe every reduction sequence is finite.
