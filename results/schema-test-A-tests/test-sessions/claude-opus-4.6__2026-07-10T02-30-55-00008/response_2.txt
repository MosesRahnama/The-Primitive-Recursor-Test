3. **Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?** This is the crux of the question. The instruction asks whether strong normalization can be established "from the presented rewrite system alone, without importing structural axioms or object-level assumptions beyond what is derivable from the given rules." LPO is a standard termination proof *method*, not an object-level axiom or structural axiom about the rewrite system's domain. The precedence choice is a proof *strategy*, not an imported assumption. I would argue this is a borderline case, but under a strict reading of the constraint — that one cannot import anything beyond what is derivable from the given rules — then yes, the method goes outside the boundary, since the well-foundedness of LPO and the correctness of the chosen precedence are not derivable from the two rewrite rules alone.

Every reduction sequence must terminate. This is evident by inspection regardless of which formal framework one uses to certify it.
