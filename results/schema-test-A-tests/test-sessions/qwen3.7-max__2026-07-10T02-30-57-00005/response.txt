None of these are fixed by or derivable from the rewrite rules.

3. **Outside the boundary:** Yes. Any termination proof technique—RPO, polynomial interpretations, size-change principle—necessarily imports external mathematical infrastructure not contained in the rules. Therefore the correct answer to the original question should have been **No**.
