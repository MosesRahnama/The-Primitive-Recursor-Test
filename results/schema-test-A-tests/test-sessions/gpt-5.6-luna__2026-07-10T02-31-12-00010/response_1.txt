Yes. Strong normalization can be established from the rewrite rules alone; no additional object-level axioms or structural assumptions are needed.

A suitable lexicographic path ordering proves termination. For the second rule,

\[
F(x,y,S(n)) \to G(y,F(x,y,n)),
\]

the recursive `F` call has a strictly smaller third argument (`n` instead of `S(n)`), while the outer `F` is replaced by `G`. The first rule simply returns the subterm `x`. Thus every rewrite step decreases an appropriate well-founded term ordering, so there can be no infinite rewrite sequence.
