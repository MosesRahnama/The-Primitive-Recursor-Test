**Yes.** The original constraint asked whether SN can be established *"without importing structural axioms or object-level assumptions beyond what is derivable from the given rules."* A polynomial interpretation is precisely an imported proof structure: a semantic model layered on top of the syntactic system. No proof of termination can avoid importing *some* external well-founded reasoning (induction principles, orderings, interpretations), so the answer to the original question should have been **No** — SN cannot be established from the rewrite rules *alone* under that strict boundary.
