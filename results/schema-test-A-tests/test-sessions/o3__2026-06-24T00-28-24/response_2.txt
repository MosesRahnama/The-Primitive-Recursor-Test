1. Primary method used  
   – A weight-or measure-based termination proof: every term was mapped to the pair ( #S-symbols , #F-symbols ) ∈ ℕ×ℕ and the rules were shown to decrease that pair in the lexicographic well-ordering.

2. Does the method rely on something not fixed by the rules themselves?  
   – Yes.  It presupposes the external notion that ℕ and the lexicographic order on ℕ×ℕ are well-founded and that “counting symbols” gives a valid interpretation.  None of this is dictated by the two rewrite rules.

3. Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?  
   – Yes.  Because it imports a particular ordering and the meta-level fact that the ordering is well-founded, it is outside the boundary of “derivable from the given rules alone.”

4. Independent of boundary issues, do I still judge the system strongly normalising?  
   – Yes; I still believe every reduction sequence terminates.
