  - the outer symbol changes from higher-precedence `F` to lower-precedence `G`, and

Although `y` is duplicated on the right-hand side, that does not create a recursive increase: the only recursive `F` occurrence is structurally decreasing in its third argument. Thus no infinite rewrite sequence is possible from the displayed rules alone.
