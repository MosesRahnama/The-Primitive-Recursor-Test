Yes — **strong normalization can be established from these rules alone**.

Reason (brief): each rewrite step on `F(x,y,...)` strictly decreases the third argument structurally from `S(n)` to `n` before recurring:
- Base: `F(x,y,Z) -> x` (stops),
- Step: `F(x,y,S(n)) -> G(y, F(x,y,n))` (recursive call on smaller `n`).

No rule rewrites `G`, `S`, or `Z`, and there is no rule that increases that third argument again. So every reduction sequence must terminate after finitely many `S`-peelings, then hit the base case. Hence the TRS is strongly normalizing.
