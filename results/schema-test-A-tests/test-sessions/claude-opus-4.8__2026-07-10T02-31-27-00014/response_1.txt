## Reasoning

The system has two rules:

```
F(x, y, Z)    -> x
F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(y, F(x, y, n))
```

**Key structural observations derivable directly from the rules:**

1. **`G` is a constructor here, not a defined symbol.** No rule has `G(...)` at the root of its left-hand side. The only defined symbol is `F`. So `G` never triggers a rewrite; it only accumulates context.

2. **Every rule strictly decreases a natural termination measure.** Consider the number of `F`-redexes together with the size of the third argument of `F`. More precisely, take as measure the multiset of the third-argument subterms occurring under an `F` symbol, or simply argue via a well-founded ordering:

   - Rule 1 removes an `F` entirely (replaced by `x`, a subterm of the LHS).
   - Rule 2 replaces `F(x, y, S(n))` with `G(y, F(x, y, n))`, where the new `F` has third argument `n`, strictly smaller than `S(n)` in the subterm order.

3. **Nesting cannot regenerate work.** In rule 2 the right-hand side contains exactly one `F`, whose third argument (`n`) is a strict subterm of the original third argument (`S(n)`). The variables `x`, `y` are simply carried along; no rule can grow the third argument of `F` or introduce new `F` symbols beyond the single copy already accounted for.

**Termination argument (recursive path / polynomial style):**

Assign an interpretation to the third argument, e.g. by counting `S`-symbols. Each application of rule 2 strictly reduces the `S`-height of the controlling argument of the innermost `F`, and rule 1 eliminates an `F`. Since `Z` and `S` are constructors with no rules acting on them, the third argument can only shrink or stay fixed under context, never grow. This yields a strictly decreasing well-founded measure on every reduction step.

Equivalently, a lexicographic/subterm argument on the third argument of `F` suffices, and this is exactly the kind of structural decrease that follows **purely from the shape of the two given rules** — no additional structural axioms, typing assumptions, or imported object-level facts are needed.

## Conclusion

The system is strongly normalizing, and this is provable from the rewrite rules alone: `F` is the sole defined symbol, its recursion is guarded by a strictly decreasing third argument built from the constructors `S`/`Z`, and `G` is inert. A simple well-founded (subterm/`S`-count) measure certifies termination without external assumptions.
