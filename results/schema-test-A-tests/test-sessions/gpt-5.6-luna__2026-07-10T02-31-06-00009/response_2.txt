1. **Primary method:** Recursive path ordering (RPO), using \(F > G\) and lexicographic argument comparison.

2. **Additional assumptions:** Yes. The precedence \(F > G\), the lexicographic status, and the chosen well-founded ordering are not fixed by the rewrite rules themselves.

3. **Boundary:** Under a strictly literal reading that forbids any externally chosen ordering or proof apparatus, that particular presentation lies outside the boundary. However, it introduces no object-level axioms about \(Z\) or \(S\); it is a metatheoretic termination certificate.

4. **Strong normalization:** Yes. Independently of those choices being implicit, the system is strongly normalizing: recursive calls replace \(S(n)\) by \(n\), and the outer \(F\)-call cannot regenerate a larger recursive argument.
