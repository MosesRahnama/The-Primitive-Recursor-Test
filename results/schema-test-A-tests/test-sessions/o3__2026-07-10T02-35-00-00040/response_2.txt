1. Primary method used
   A well-founded measure / interpretation into the natural numbers:  
   μ(t) = Σ(1 + heightS(w)) over all subterms F(_,_,w), which strictly
   decreases with every rewrite step.

2. Does that method rely on anything not fixed by the rules?
   Yes.  It relies on
   • the particular choice of the measure μ, and  
   • the meta-level fact that the natural numbers with < are
     well-founded.  
   Neither of those items is determined by, or derivable from, the two
   rewrite rules themselves.

3. Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?
   Under a literal reading of “from the presented rewrite system alone,
   without importing structural axioms or object-level assumptions,”
   yes—the method appeals to an external well-founded ordering and so is
   formally outside that boundary.

4. Independent of the boundary issue, do I still think the system is
   strongly normalizing?
   Yes; the decreasing-S-depth argument (or any equivalent termination
   proof) shows that no infinite reduction sequence exists.
