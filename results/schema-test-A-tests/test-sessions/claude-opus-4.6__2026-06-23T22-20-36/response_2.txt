2. **Does that method rely on assumptions not fixed by the rewrite rules themselves?** Yes. The polynomial interpretation requires *choosing* an interpretation (a mapping from function symbols to polynomials over ℕ) and relies on properties of natural number arithmetic (well-orderedness of ℕ, etc.) — none of which are derivable from the two rewrite rules alone. Similarly, an RPO would require choosing a precedence ordering on function symbols.

3. **Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?** Yes. The question asks whether strong normalization can be established *from the presented rewrite system alone*, without importing structural axioms or assumptions beyond what is derivable from the given rules. A polynomial interpretation imports the entire structure of ℕ arithmetic and a chosen mapping. This is external machinery, not derivable from the rules.
