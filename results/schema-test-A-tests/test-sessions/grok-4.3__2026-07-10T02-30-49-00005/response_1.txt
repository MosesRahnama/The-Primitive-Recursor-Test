Each contraction replaces that redex by a term whose only new \(F\)-redex has a strictly smaller third argument (the immediate subterm of the original \(S\)). Because every ground term is a finite tree, the multiset of those third-argument sizes is a well-founded measure that decreases on every rewrite step; hence no infinite reduction sequence exists. No external axioms about \(S\), \(Z\), or \(G\) are required for the argument.
