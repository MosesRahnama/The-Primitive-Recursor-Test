1. **Primary method used:**  
   A standard termination proof via a simplification ordering / lexicographic path ordering, with an assumed precedence such as `F > G`.

2. **Does that rely on something not fixed by the rules themselves?**  
   Yes. The choice of ordering, precedence, and the meta-level termination proof structure are not explicitly part of the rewrite system.

3. **Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?**  
   Under a strict reading, yes. It imports an external proof apparatus/order not derivable solely from the rules as given.

4. **Separately, is the system strongly normalizing?**  
   Yes, I still think the TRS is strongly normalizing: the only recursive call decreases the third argument from `S(n)` to `n`, and no rule can rebuild a larger recursive `F` call.
