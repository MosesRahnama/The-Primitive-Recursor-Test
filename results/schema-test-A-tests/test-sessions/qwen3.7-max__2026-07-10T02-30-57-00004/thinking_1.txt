Actually, in term rewriting, strong normalization means that ALL reduction sequences from ANY term (including open terms with variables) terminate.

For open terms, variables are treated as constants (they don't reduce). So if n is a variable, F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(y, F(x, y, n)), and then F(x, y, n) has n as a variable in the third position. Since n is a variable (not Z and not S(something)), neither rule applies, and it's in normal form.

So for open terms, this also terminates.
