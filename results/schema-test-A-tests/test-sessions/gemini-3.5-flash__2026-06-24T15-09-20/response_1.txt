     1. $F(x, y, S(n)) \succ_{lpo} y$ (which holds because $y$ is a subterm of the left-hand side).

#### Method 2: Polynomial Interpretation

* $[Z] = 1$
* $[S](N) = N + 1$
* $[G](Y, V) = Y + V$
* $[F](X, Y, N) = X + (Y + 1)N$

We verify that the left-hand side is strictly greater than the right-hand side for both rules:
1. **Rule 1:**
