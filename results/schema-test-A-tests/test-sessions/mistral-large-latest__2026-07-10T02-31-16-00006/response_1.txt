2. **Strong Normalization (SN)**:
   SN requires that *all* reduction sequences terminate, regardless of the order in which rules are applied. To prove SN, we typically need to show that:
   - The system is *terminating* (no infinite reductions exist), and
   - The system is *confluent* (or at least locally confluent), though confluence is not strictly necessary for SN if termination can be shown directly.

If the third argument is indeed a Peano numeral, then the recursion is structurally decreasing, and termination would follow from a lexicographic or recursive path ordering (RPO). However:
