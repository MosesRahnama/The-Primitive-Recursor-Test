Yes.

This TRS is strongly normalizing from the rules alone.

Reason: the only recursive behavior is in

- `F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(y, F(x, y, n))`

and the recursive call strictly decreases the third argument of `F` from `S(n)` to `n`. The base case

- `F(x, y, Z) -> x`

stops the recursion.

There are no rewrite rules for `G`, `S`, or `Z`, so they do not create any new redexes except that the recursive `F` term keeps moving toward the base case. Every rewrite step on an `F` with third argument built from `k` successors reduces that number by 1, and after finitely many steps it reaches `Z`, then rewrites to `x`.

So, over the terms generated by this signature, no infinite rewrite sequence can be produced. Hence strong normalization is established directly from the given system.
