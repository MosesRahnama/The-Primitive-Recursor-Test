**Assessment: Yes – the system is strongly normalizing, and a termination proof can be given using only the structural resources that are already present in the calculus.**

**Reasoning**

The rewrite system is finitely branching and every reduction step strictly decreases a well‑founded measure that is definable by structural recursion on the inductive type `Trace`.  
Define three components:

1. **\(E(t)\)** – the number of `eqW` constructors occurring in \(t\).  
2. **\(M(t)\)** – the multiset of natural numbers \(\{\,|n| \mid \text{`recΔ b s n` is a subterm of } t\,\}\), where \(|n|\) is the ordinary size (number of constructors) of the third argument \(n\).  
3. **\(S(t)\)** – the total size of \(t\).

Order triples lexicographically: \((E,M,S)\) with the multiset order on \(M\) and the usual order on \(E,S\).

Now inspect each rule:

* **`eqW` rules**  
  – `R_eq_refl` removes one `eqW`, so \(E\) drops by 1.  
  – `R_eq_diff` also removes the outer `eqW` (and introduces no new one), so \(E\) strictly decreases.

* **`recΔ` rules**  
  – `R_rec_zero` deletes a `recΔ` subterm, removing one element from the multiset \(M\).  
  – `R_rec_succ` replaces a subterm `recΔ b s (delta n)` with `app s (recΔ b s n)`. The new `recΔ` carries the strictly smaller third argument \(n\) (since \(|n| < |delta n|\)). Thus the multiset \(M\) decreases in the multiset ordering (a larger element is replaced by a smaller one).

* **All other rules** (`R_int_delta`, the three `merge` rules) reduce the total size \(S\) while leaving \(E\) and \(M\) unchanged.

Because each step makes the lexicographic triple smaller, and the lexicographic product of well‑founded orders is well founded, no infinite reduction sequence exists. The argument uses only:

* structural recursion on `Trace` (available from the inductive definition),
* the standard well‑foundedness of the multiset ordering (provable by structural induction), and
* elementary arithmetic on natural numbers.

No additional object‑level axioms, no guards, no side conditions, and no extra rewrite rules are required. Hence strong normalization is derivable from the presented calculus alone.
