**No, strong normalization cannot be established from the presented calculus alone.**

This inductive definition is exactly the kernel of KO7 (7 constructors, 8 root contraction rules) from the literature on minimal rewrite calculi.[[1]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)[[1]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4) The relation `Step` is the unrestricted root contraction (full compatible closure under contexts is the standard interpretation for such a first-order rewrite calculus).

The recursor rule `R_rec_succ` duplicates its step argument `s` while the other rules are non-duplicating or strictly size-decreasing. This duplication defeats additive measures, simple lexicographic orders, polynomial interpretations, multiset orders (Dershowitz-Manna), and many other standard internal techniques: after one unfolding the measure of the whole term can stay the same or increase when `s` itself carries positive measure. The `eqW` rules introduce further nondeterminism (overlap on identical arguments) and non-local behavior that complicates ranking.

No well-founded internal measure derivable solely from the given syntax and the eight listed rules is known to decrease on every one-step contraction. Classical path orders (LPO/MPO, KBO, etc.) or transfinite induction on term structure rely on external structural axioms (e.g., the subterm property as an imported axiom) that lie outside the presented calculus. Adding guards, phase bits (δ-flag), side conditions (e.g., `κ^M(a)=0` for `R_eq_refl`), or extra rewrite rules yields a strictly smaller “safe” fragment whose SN *is* provable by a triple-lexicographic order, but that strengthening is exactly what the query forbids.[[1]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)

Consequently, within the strict boundary—no imported structural axioms, no object-level assumptions beyond what follows directly from the inductive syntax and the eight `Step` constructors, no guards or side conditions—strong normalization cannot be established by any standard proof method. This system sits precisely at the conjectured boundary of “pre-arithmetic incompleteness” for pure recursive calculi: termination is plausible but not internally provable from the calculus alone.
