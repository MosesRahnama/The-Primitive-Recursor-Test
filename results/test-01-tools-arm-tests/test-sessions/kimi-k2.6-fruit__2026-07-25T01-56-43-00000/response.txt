**Assessment: Yes. Strong normalization holds for the presented system, and it can be established from the calculus alone.**

**Reasoning.**  
Even though a few rewrite rules superficially increase syntactic size, every rule strictly decreases a natural-number weight assigned to traces. Define the weight \(|\cdot|\) recursively:

- \(|{\tt plum}| = 1\)
- \(|{\tt grape}\;t| = |t| + 1\)
- \(|{\tt mango}\;t| = |t| + 1\)
- \(|{\tt peach}\;t_1\;t_2| = |t_1| + |t_2| + 1\)
- \(|{\tt pear}\;t_1\;t_2| = |t_1| + |t_2| + 1\)
- \(|{\tt banana}\;b\;s\;n| = |b| + |n|\cdot(|s|+2)\)
- \(|{\tt cherry}\;a\;b| = |a| + |b| + 3\)

Now check each rule:

1. **R_mango_grape**: \(|{\tt mango}({\tt grape}\;t)| = |t|+2 > 1 = |{\tt plum}|\).
2. **R_peach_plum_left**: \(|{\tt peach}\;{\tt plum}\;t| = |t|+2 > |t|\).
3. **R_peach_plum_right**: \(|{\tt peach}\;t\;{\tt plum}| = |t|+2 > |t|\).
4. **R_peach_cancel**: \(|{\tt peach}\;t\;t| = 2|t|+1 > |t|\) (since \(|t|\ge 1\)).
5. **R_banana_zero**: \(|{\tt banana}\;b\;s\;{\tt plum}| = |b|+|s|+2 > |b|\).
6. **R_apple_orange**:  
   LHS \(= |b| + (|n|+1)(|s|+2) = |b|+|n||s|+2|n|+|s|+2\).  
   RHS \(= |s| + |b|+|n|(|s|+2)+1 = |b|+|n||s|+2|n|+|s|+1\).  
   LHS exceeds RHS by exactly 1.
7. **R_cherry_refl**: \(|{\tt cherry}\;a\;a| = 2|a|+3 > 1 = |{\tt plum}|\).
8. **R_cherry_diff**: \(|{\tt cherry}\;a\;b| = |a|+|b|+3 > |a|+|b|+2 = |{\tt mango}({\tt peach}\;a\;b)|\).

Because the weight function is defined purely from the syntax of `Trace`, uses only addition and multiplication by positive constants, and is strictly monotone in each argument, any contextual (congruence) step also strictly decreases weight. Since the natural numbers are well-founded under \(>\), no infinite reduction sequence exists.

Thus strong normalization is provable without importing any external axioms, side conditions, or extra rewrite rules: the well-founded weight is derivable directly from the given constructors and the eight rules of `Step`.
