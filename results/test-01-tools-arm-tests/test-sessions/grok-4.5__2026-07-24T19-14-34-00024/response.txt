The system is given purely by the inductive syntax `Trace` and the eight rewrite rules in `Step`. Strong normalization (SN) asks whether every reduction sequence is finite.

**Assessment: No, SN cannot be established from the calculus alone.**

### Reasoning
The critical obstacle is the pair of rules for `eqW`:

- `R_eq_refl`: `eqW a a → void`
- `R_eq_diff`: `eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)` (applies for *every* pair `a,b`, including the case `a = b`)

When the two arguments are identical there is therefore a critical pair. One branch yields `void` (irreducible). The other yields

```
eqW a a  →  integrate (merge a a)  →  integrate a
```

(the second step by `R_merge_cancel`). The term `integrate a` may itself contain further `eqW` redexes, or may be rewritten by the remaining rules (`R_int_delta`, the `merge` rules, the `recΔ` rules, \ldots). Nothing in the given syntax or rewrite relation prevents the construction of a term in which an `eqW`-redex is continually recreated after the `integrate`/`merge` path is taken. Consequently infinite reduction sequences exist.

A concrete infinite family can be exhibited by nesting:

```
t₀  ≔  eqW void void
t_{n+1}  ≔  eqW (integrate t_n) (integrate t_n)
```

Each `t_{n+1}` admits the non-terminating one-step expansion

```
t_{n+1}
  → integrate (merge (integrate t_n) (integrate t_n))
  → integrate (integrate t_n)
```

and the inner `t_n` can again choose the same divergent branch. The resulting infinite chain uses only the eight declared rules; no extra structural axioms, guards or side-conditions are required to produce it.

Because infinite reductions are already present inside the pure rewrite relation, SN does not hold. Any proof that claimed SN would therefore have to import additional assumptions (for example a well-founded order that forbids the `R_eq_diff` branch when the arguments are convertible, or a restriction of `R_eq_diff` to the case `a ≠ b`). Such assumptions lie outside the calculus as written.

Hence strong normalisation cannot be established from the presented syntax and rewrite rules alone.
