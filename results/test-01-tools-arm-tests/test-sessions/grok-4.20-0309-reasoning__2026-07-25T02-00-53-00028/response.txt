**Yes**, strong normalization can be established from the given syntax and `Step` rules alone.

The inductive definition of `Step` axiomatizes exactly eight root-pattern matches; no congruence rules, contextual closure, or reduction-under-constructors are present (and none may be imported). Consequently every one-step reduct is obtained only when the *entire* term matches one of the listed left-hand sides exactly. The possible reducts are:

- `void` (from `R_int_delta`, `R_eq_refl`);
- a proper subterm `t` or `b` (from the three `merge` rules and `R_rec_zero`);
- an `app` term (from `R_rec_succ`);
- an `integrate (merge _ _)` term (from `R_eq_diff`).

Because `Trace` is an inductive type, every term is a finite constructor tree. We proceed by induction on the structural size of a term (number of constructors).

- If the term does not match any left-hand side it is already irreducible; the only possible sequence has length 0.
- The `merge` and `R_rec_zero` cases produce a proper subterm. By the induction hypothesis every sequence from that strictly smaller term is finite, so every sequence from the original term is finite.
- `R_rec_succ` yields an `app`-rooted term, which matches none of the eight patterns, so the sequence ends after this single step.
- `R_eq_diff` yields `integrate (merge a b)`. This term has root `integrate`. It can match `R_int_delta` only if `merge a b` were definitionally `delta t`; distinct constructors of an inductive type are never definitionally equal, so the pattern never matches. The resulting term is therefore irreducible and every sequence from the original `eqW` term has length exactly 1.
- `R_int_delta` and `R_eq_refl` both yield `void`, which is irreducible.

In all cases the induction goes through with no extra rewrite rules, no side conditions, no hidden well-founded orders beyond the inductive size of the finite syntax trees, and no object-level axioms beyond the eight given constructors and the eight given `Step` rules. Because every chain is either a root rewrite to an irreducible form or a root rewrite to a strictly smaller term (plus a finite chain therefrom by the induction hypothesis), there are no infinite reduction sequences. Hence the system is strongly normalizing.
