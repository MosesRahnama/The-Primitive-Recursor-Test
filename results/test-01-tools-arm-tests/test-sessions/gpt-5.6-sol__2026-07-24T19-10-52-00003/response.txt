For example, define a measure \(m : \texttt{Trace} \to \mathbb N\):

```text
m(void)            = 0
m(delta t)         = m(t)
m(integrate t)     = m(t) + 1
m(merge a b)       = m(a) + m(b) + 1
m(app a b)         = 0
m(recΔ b s n)      = m(b) + 1
m(eqW a b)         = m(a) + m(b) + 3
```

Every presented rule strictly decreases this measure. In particular:

- `recΔ b s (delta n) → app s (recΔ b s n)` decreases to `0`;
- `eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)` decreases from  
  \(m(a)+m(b)+3\) to \(m(a)+m(b)+2\);
- all erasing, projection, and cancellation rules decrease immediately.

Hence an infinite `Step` sequence would induce an infinite strictly descending sequence of natural numbers, which is impossible. Therefore every `Trace` is strongly normalizing for the relation shown.
