**No, strong normalization cannot be established from the presented calculus alone (within the stated boundary).**[[1]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v8)

The given `Step` rules define a first-order, operator-only term rewrite system (compatible closure under all contexts is the natural reading of the calculus). Most rules are size-decreasing or clearly eliminating constructors (`integrate(δt) → void`, the three `merge` contractions, `recΔ b s void → b`, `eqW a a → void`). However, two rules strictly increase term size:

- `recΔ b s (δ n) → app s (recΔ b s n)` duplicates `s` (and any redexes or `δ`-constructors inside it).
- `eqW a b → integrate(merge a b)` (for any `a`, `b`) inserts new `integrate`/`merge` layers.

No right-hand side ever introduces a fresh `eqW` or `δ`; both constructors are only ever eliminated or duplicated when carried inside an `s` that is copied by the recursor rule. This bounds the *total* number of eliminations for any concrete finite starting term (each initial `δ` in a counter position fuels a finite number of unfoldings, and duplications merely multiply existing “fuel”), suggesting that all sequences are in fact finite. Nevertheless, this does not yield a proof meeting the constraints.

Standard termination methods fail or violate the boundary:

- **Additive or polynomial measures** cannot orient the recursor rule: any strictly decreasing measure `M` on the whole term would have to satisfy `M(app s (recΔ b s n)) < M(recΔ b s (δ n))` while `s` is duplicated, which is impossible for additive compositional measures (the post-duplication size is essentially the pre-size minus 1 plus an extra copy of `M(s)`).[[2]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v6)
- **Multiset/path orders** or lexicographic combinations can be made to work, but only after imposing a *guard* (e.g., a “δ-flag” phase bit that distinguishes safe vs. unsafe recursion phases) or extra side conditions on contexts. That produces a *safe fragment* whose strong normalization is provable by a triple-lexicographic order (phase bit, multiset rank of redexes under a Duchene-Mahboubi-style precedence, ordinal tie-breaker) together with Newman’s lemma for the guarded subrelation. The guard is explicitly forbidden by the query.
- The two `eqW` rules overlap on `eqW a a`, producing a non-joinable critical peak (`void` vs. a reduct containing `integrate(merge a a)`). This destroys local confluence for the full relation, so confluence-based arguments are unavailable without strengthening.

The inductive definitions of `Trace` and `Step` supply only their own introduction/elimination principles; they do not derive a well-founded order or a compositional measure that orients the duplicating rule while simultaneously handling the `eqW` overlap. Any proof therefore requires either (a) extra structural axioms about the syntax (e.g., an external well-founded order on traces stronger than what the constructors alone provide), (b) hidden assumptions about contexts or evaluation strategy, or (c) strengthening the relation with guards/side-conditions—precisely the moves disallowed by the query. The existence of an “orientation boundary” for step-duplicating recursors and a conjecture that full termination is unprovable for any relational/operator-only TRS of this form formalize exactly this limitation.[[3]](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v8)

Thus, while every concrete term almost certainly terminates, strong normalization of the *system* as given cannot be established from the presented syntax and rules alone using any standard method that stays inside the boundary.
