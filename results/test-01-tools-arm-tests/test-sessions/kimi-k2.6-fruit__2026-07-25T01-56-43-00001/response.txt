The rewrite system is terminating (strongly normalizing). The key observations, all derivable from the given syntax and rules, are:

1. **Cherry is never created.** The right-hand sides of all rules contain no `cherry` constructor. Hence rules `R_cherry_refl` and `R_cherry_diff` can fire only as many times as there are `cherry` occurrences in the starting term. After those are eliminated, those two rules are permanently inactive.

2. **Grape is never created.** No right-hand side introduces a `grape`. Rule `R_mango_grape` consumes one `grape`, and rule `R_apple_orange` (the `banana`/`grape` rule) strips one `grape` from the third argument of a `banana`. Because grapes are never introduced, the number of nested `grape` constructors in the third argument of any given `banana` is a finite, non-renewable resource. Consequently each `banana` can fire rule 6 only finitely many times; after that it either fires rule 5 and disappears or becomes inert.

3. **Rule 6’s duplication is harmless.** Although `banana b s (grape n) → pear s (banana b s n)` duplicates `s`, the duplicated copies are either wrapped in the inert `pear` constructor or carried into a recursive call whose third argument is strictly smaller (one fewer `grape`). The “fuel” for this recursion is bounded as noted above, so no infinite spiral of duplications is possible.

4. **The remaining rules are size-decreasing.** Rules 1–4 and 7 strictly reduce the total size of the term. Once cherries are exhausted and bananas are inert, only these size-decreasing rules remain.

Since there exists a well-founded order that is strictly decreased by every rewrite step, every reduction sequence terminates. No additional rewrite rules, guards, side conditions, or hidden structural assumptions are required.
