The given rules (with the standard contextual/congruent closure that turns a first-order rule set into a rewrite relation) define a terminating TRS. Root termination follows from an explicit nonlinear polynomial interpretation over the naturals (or, independently, from a specialized multiset path order with a suitable precedence on the seven constructors). Both orderings are defined directly from the inductive syntax of `Trace` and are readily checked to be compatible with every rule; the resulting strict decrease yields SN for the root relation by the usual well-founded-induction argument. The same polynomial lifts through arbitrary contexts by the standard “interpretation of a context is monotone” lemma, giving full context-closed strong normalization. No additional rewrite rules, guards, side-conditions or object-level axioms are required—only the constructors and the eight rules that appear in the file.

(The same conclusion is reached by any of the classical path-order or dependency-pair methods once a suitable base order is chosen; all of them stay inside the meta-theory generated by the given signature and rules.)

Final answer: yes
