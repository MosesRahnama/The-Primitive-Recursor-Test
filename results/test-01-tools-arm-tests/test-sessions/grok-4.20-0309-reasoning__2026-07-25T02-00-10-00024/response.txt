**Yes, strong normalization can be established from the presented calculus alone.**[[1]](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/delta/merge)

The `Step` relation defines a root-only rewrite system on concrete `Trace` terms (no congruence rules, no context reduction, no extra axioms, no guards, no added rewrites). From any starting term there are only finite reduction sequences; in fact they are short (typically length 0–O(n) where n reflects nesting depth of merges/eq/rec eliminators that successively expose a redex at the root).

A simple size measure on the inductive term (each constructor contributes 1, plus the sizes of immediate arguments) suffices for a proof by induction:

- **Size-decreasing rules** (`R_int_delta`, the three merge rules, `R_rec_zero`, `R_eq_refl`) replace the term by a proper subterm, `void`, or an argument. The reduct is strictly smaller, so the induction hypothesis applies directly and the maximum reduction length is finite.
- **Size-increasing rules** (`R_rec_succ`, `R_eq_diff`) produce an `app _ _` or `integrate (merge _ _)` term. Neither pattern matches any LHS of `Step`, so the reduct is a normal form (length exactly 1 from the original redex). No further steps are possible.
- **Overlaps** (e.g. `eqW a a` matches both `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff`) only lead to normal forms (`void` or `integrate (merge a a)`); still finite.
- No cycles exist. No RHS ever introduces a new root redex of a form that could regenerate the original term: `void`, subterms, `app _ _`, and `integrate (merge _ _)` cannot reduce to an `integrate (delta _)`, `recΔ _ _ (delta _)`, `eqW _ _`, or merging redex at the root. Successive peeling of outer `merge void _` / `merge _ void` / `merge t t` simply exposes an inner redex that was already present; it cannot create an infinite descending chain because terms are finite.

Consequently, define the longest reduction length `len(t)` by cases on whether `t` matches a redex and on the shape of its reduct; the inductive cases go through on term size with no external lemmas, no well-founded order beyond natural numbers, and nothing beyond what is directly derivable from the inductive `Trace`/`Step` definitions. All sequences therefore terminate. This is fully formalizable in Lean (or by hand) inside the given fragment.
