No extra rewrite rules, guards, side conditions, or object‑level axioms need to be imported — only the automatic well‑foundedness of the subterm relation that comes for free with the inductive definition of `Trace`.

### Why a naive size argument fails, and why RPO still works

Six of the eight rules are trivially size‑decreasing (`R_int_delta`, both `R_merge_void_*`, `R_merge_cancel`, `R_rec_zero`, `R_eq_refl` are literally subterm-selections). The two non‑trivial rules are:

- **`R_eq_diff`**: `eqW(a,b) → integrate(merge(a,b))` — size‑preserving up to a constant, not a subterm rule.
- **`R_rec_succ`**: `recΔ(b,s,delta n) → app(s, recΔ(b,s,n))` — this one actually *increases* term size (it duplicates `s` while only decreasing the third argument), so a plain size measure cannot certify termination.

Both cases are exactly the pattern LPO is designed for:

- Choose a precedence `eqW ≻ recΔ ≻ integrate ≻ app ≻ merge ≻ delta ≻ void` (any total order satisfying `eqW ≻ integrate`, `eqW ≻ merge`, and `recΔ ≻ app` suffices).
- Give `recΔ` a **lexicographic status prioritizing its third argument**.

Then:
- For `R_rec_succ`, comparing `recΔ(b,s,delta n)` against `recΔ(b,s,n)` reduces to comparing `delta n` vs `n` lexicographically in the last coordinate, and `delta n ⊐ n` by the plain subterm property. Since `recΔ ≻ app` and both immediate subterms of the RHS (`s` and `recΔ(b,s,n)`) are dominated by the LHS, LPO orients the rule correctly *despite* the size increase — this is the same argument textbooks use to certify termination of primitive recursion schemes (e.g. `plus`, `times` defined by recursion).
- For `R_eq_diff`, `eqW ≻ integrate` and `eqW ≻ merge` immediately give `eqW(a,b) ⊐ integrate(merge(a,b))`, since `a,b` are subterms of the LHS.

All eight rules are oriented by this single, fixed LPO instance, and LPO is a well-founded order on any finite signature — its well-foundedness is a general combinatorial fact requiring nothing beyond structural induction on terms, which is exactly what the `inductive Trace` declaration supplies automatically in Lean. Hence **no infinite `Step` sequence exists**, i.e. the system is strongly normalizing.
