**No, strong normalization cannot be established from the presented calculus alone.**[[1]](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)[[2]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)

The given `Step` relation is the full unguarded kernel (KO7) of a minimal operator-only first-order TRS studied in the literature (exactly matching the 7 constructors and 8 root rules: `R_int_delta`, the three `merge` rules, the two `recΔ` rules, `R_eq_refl`, and `R_eq_diff`). This system is not strongly normalizing. It admits infinite reduction sequences arising from the interplay of the duplicating `recΔ` rule (`recΔ b s (delta n) → app s (recΔ b s n)`) and the unrestricted `eqW`/`merge` rules. The duplication of `s` prevents any uniform additive/size-based decrease, and the overlap at `eqW a a` (where `R_eq_refl` contracts to `void` while `R_eq_diff` expands to `integrate(merge a a)`) yields non-joinable peaks that allow divergent paths.[[1]](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)

Standard termination techniques (additive measures, polynomials, KBO-style orders, single compositional measures, plain lexicographic orders) all fail on the concrete instance without extra structure. Impossibility results (formalized in the corresponding Lean development) show that no such measure orients the full root steps while satisfying the necessary subterm/compatibility properties derivable from the syntax alone. The only verified SN proof applies to a strict *safe fragment* (`SafeStep`) that adds per-rule guards and side-conditions (δ-flags, κ^M emptiness checks, explicit `a ≠ b` for `R_eq_diff`, etc.). Those restrictions are precisely the kind of strengthening prohibited by the query.[[1]](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)

Because the full relation is not SN, and because any proof of SN would require either (a) those extra guards/side-conditions or (b) external/object-level axioms or structural compatibility rules beyond what the inductive `Trace`/`Step` definition directly provides, SN cannot be established from the presented calculus alone using any permitted method. The paper explicitly conjectures that no relational operator-only TRS of this form has its full termination provable by internally definable methods without such imports.[[1]](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)

In short: the calculus as written does not terminate, and the given rules supply neither a decreasing measure nor the restrictions needed to recover one.
