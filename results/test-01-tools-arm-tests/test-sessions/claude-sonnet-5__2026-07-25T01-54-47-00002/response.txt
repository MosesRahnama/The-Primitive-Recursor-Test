**The key structural fact.** `Step` is defined *only* by the eight rules shown — there are **no congruence/compatibility constructors** (nothing like "if `Step t t'` then `Step (delta t) (delta t')`", or the analogous closure rules for `integrate`, `merge`, `app`, `recΔ`, `eqW`). So `Step a b` holds **only** when the *entire* term `a` matches one of the eight left‑hand‑side patterns at the root. This is not a general term‑rewriting system closed under contexts; it is a "root‑only" relation. That single fact makes the SN proof elementary.

**Proof by strong induction on `sizeof` (number of constructors) of the term**, case‑split exactly on the seven `Trace` constructors — no axioms beyond structural induction on the inductive type and case analysis on the inductive `Step` are used:

- `void`, `delta t`, `app s t` — none of the eight rules has this as its outer shape, so these are always **normal forms** (0 steps).
- `integrate t` — steps only if `t = delta t'` (`R_int_delta`), and then goes to `void`, a normal form. Otherwise it's already normal. Chain length ≤ 1.
- `merge s t` — every applicable rule (`R_merge_void_left/right`, `R_merge_cancel`) sends the term to one of its **proper subterms** (`s` or `t`), strictly decreasing size; induction hypothesis finishes the argument.
- `recΔ b s n` — if `n = void`, it goes to the subterm `b` (smaller, induction applies). If `n = delta n'`, `R_rec_succ` produces `app s (recΔ b s n')` — but `app`‑headed terms are *always* normal forms (first bullet), so this branch terminates in exactly one more step, regardless of what's hidden inside. Otherwise it's already normal.
- `eqW a b` — if `a = b`, *both* `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff` are applicable (this is the one rule overlap in the system), giving two branches: `void` (normal), or `integrate (merge a b)`. The latter can never trigger `R_int_delta` since `merge a b` is never syntactically `delta _`, so it too is immediately normal. If `a ≠ b`, only the diff‑branch applies, again terminating in one step.

Every branch either strictly shrinks (handled by the induction hypothesis) or lands, after at most one extra step, on a term whose outer constructor (`void`, `delta _`, or `app _ _`, or `integrate(merge _ _)`) can *never* match any of the eight patterns again.
