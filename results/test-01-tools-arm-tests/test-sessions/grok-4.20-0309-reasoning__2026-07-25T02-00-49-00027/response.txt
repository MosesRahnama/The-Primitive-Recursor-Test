**No, strong normalization cannot be established from the presented calculus alone.**

The inductive definition of `Trace` and the eight constructors of `Step` (the unrestricted/root rules for `integrate(δ _)`, the three `merge` cases, the two `recΔ` cases, and the two `eqW` cases) generate the full one-step relation. When this relation is understood in the standard first-order sense—i.e., closed under arbitrary contexts, as is conventional for a rewrite calculus—the duplication inherent in `R_rec_succ` (`recΔ b s (δ n) → app s (recΔ b s n)`) immediately defeats every additive, simple lexicographic, polynomial, or multiset measure that can be defined internally from the syntax and rules. After one unfolding the measure *M* satisfies an identity of the form *M(after) = M(before) − 1 + M(s)*; whenever *s* itself contains redexes this produces a tie or an increase, so no strict decrease is obtainable without external arithmetic, a nonlinear interpretation that imports extra structure, or meta-level well-founded orders (MPO, etc.) that rely on the universal subterm property as a hidden axiom.[[1]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)

The `eqW` rules add a further obstacle: `eqW a a` admits two distinct one-step reducts (`void` via reflexivity and `integrate(merge a a)` via the diff rule, which cancels to `integrate a`). The resulting peak cannot be joined internally without a side-condition distinguishing the cases, so even local confluence fails for the full relation. While this alone does not preclude termination, it blocks the standard Newman route that would let a local measure lift to global SN.[[2]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v3)

All standard proof techniques (lexicographic orders on size, multiset extensions, polynomial interpretations, recursive path orders, well-founded induction directly on the inductive syntax) therefore require *either* strengthening the relation with guards/side-conditions (as done for the `SafeStep` fragment in the literature, which restores both SN and confluence via a triple-lexicographic measure on a δ-flag, Dershowitz–Manna multiset rank, and ordinal payload) *or* importing structural axioms, external well-founded relations, or object-level assumptions not derivable from the given constructors and the eight `Step` rules. The bare syntax and rules alone supply no well-founded order that decreases on every contextual instance of `R_rec_succ` while remaining internally definable. Consequently SN of the full system cannot be established inside the stated boundary. (The literature confirms this boundary by mechanizing SN and a total normalizer only for the guarded fragment and exhibiting a family of impossibility results for internally definable measures on pure duplicating recursive calculi.)[[1]](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00081v4)

In short, the duplication stressor and the unjoinable `eqW` critical pair are visible in the presented rules; any proof that overcomes them necessarily steps outside the given calculus.
