- **closed under substitution** (so the schematic orientation covers every ground instance),

every rewrite step strictly decreases the term in a well-founded order, and no infinite reduction can exist. This is the standard Dershowitz simplification-order argument, requiring no strengthening of the system.

### Why a naive measure fails (and why that's not a problem)

I first checked whether a simple linear/size-style interpretation works, and it provably **cannot**: the rule `R_rec_succ : recΔ b s (delta n) → app s (recΔ b s n)` duplicates `s` (it appears both as the top argument of `app` and inside the recursive `recΔ`). Under any strictly monotone linear interpretation this forces a negative coefficient on `s`, so no such measure exists. This is a well-known signature of structural-recursion rules — and it is precisely the case the LPO handles cleanly, because `recΔ` outranks `app` and the recursive argument `delta n` is lexicographically above `n`.

### Caveats worth stating honestly

- The system is **not confluent / not orthogonal**: `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff` critically overlap on `eqW a a` (both fire, giving `void` vs. `integrate (merge a a)`), and `R_eq_diff` has no side condition restricting it to `a ≠ b`.
