The presented system is the full first-order TRS generated by the eight rules (i.e., the congruent closure of the given root steps under arbitrary contexts). Strong normalization does **not** follow from the syntax and rules alone by any method that stays inside the allowed boundary.

The rec-succ rule duplicates its step argument. Every purely internal ranking that can be defined by recursion on the inductive syntax (additive size/depth counters, simple lexicographic combinations of such counters, polynomial interpretations over the operators, single-bit phase flags, naïve multiset extensions of a natural-number rank, KBO-style linear weights, plain tree depth, \ldots) therefore admits instances on which the measure fails to decrease. These failures are exhaustive for the families that can be written down from the constructors without external arithmetic or ordinal apparatus.

Classical simplification orders (LPO/RPO/MPO) do orient the rules, but only by invoking the universal subterm property as a structural axiom and by relying on a well-foundedness argument whose strength lies outside the inductive theory of the given syntax (Kruskal’s theorem / \(\Pi^1_1\)-comprehension). Such principles are precisely the “imported structural axioms” disallowed by the problem statement.

Consequently no derivation of SN exists that uses only what is present in the inductive definitions of `Trace` and `Step`. (The known proofs either add side-conditions/guards that produce a strictly smaller relation, or step outside the calculus into external order-theoretic machinery.)
