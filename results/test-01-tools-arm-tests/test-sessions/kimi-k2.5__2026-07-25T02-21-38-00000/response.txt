**Assessment: Yes, strong normalization holds.**

**Reasoning:**

The rewrite system is strongly normalizing (terminating). This can be established by defining a well-founded measure (weight function) on terms that strictly decreases with every reduction step. No external axioms or structural extensions are required; the measure is derivable from the given syntax.

Define the weight $w(t)$ recursively:

- $w(\text{void}) = 1$
- $w(\text{delta}\ t) = 1 + w(t)$
- $w(\text{integrate}\ t) = 2 \cdot w(t)$
- $w(\text{merge}\ t_1\ t_2) = w(t_1) + w(t_2)$
- $w(\text{app}\ t_1\ t_2) = 1 + w(t_1) + w(t_2)$
- $w(\text{rec}_\Delta\ b\ s\ n) = w(b) + (w(n) + 1) \cdot (w(s) + 2)$
- $w(\text{eqW}\ a\ b) = 2 \cdot (w(a) + w(b)) + 1$

**Verification of decrease:**

1.  **R_int_delta**: $w(\text{integrate}(\text{delta}\ t)) = 2(1+w(t)) = 2+2w(t) > 1 = w(\text{void})$.
2.  **R_merge_void_left**: $w(\text{merge}\ \text{void}\ t) = 1 + w(t) > w(t)$.
3.  **R_merge_void_right**: Symmetric to above.
4.  **R_merge_cancel**: $w(\text{merge}\ t\ t) = 2w(t) > w(t)$.
5.  **R_rec_zero**: $w(\text{rec}_\Delta\ b\ s\ \text{void}) = w(b) + 2(w(s)+2) > w(b)$.
6.  **R_rec_succ**: 
    - LHS: $w(b) + (w(\text{delta}\ n)+1)(w(s)+2) = w(b) + (w(n)+2)(w(s)+2)$
    - RHS: $w(\text{app}\ s\ (\text{rec}_\Delta\ b\ s\ n)) = 1 + w(s) + w(b) + (w(n)+1)(w(s)+2)$
    - Difference: $(w(n)+2)(w(s)+2) - 1 - w(s) - (w(n)+1)(w(s)+2) = (w(s)+2) - 1 - w(s) = 1 > 0$.
7.  **R_eq_refl**: $w(\text{eqW}\ a\ a) = 4w(a) + 1 > 1 = w(\text{void})$.
8.  **R_eq_diff**: $w(\text{eqW}\ a\ b) = 2(w(a)+w(b)) + 1 > 2(w(a)+w(b)) = w(\text{integrate}(\text{merge}\ a\ b))$.

Since every rewrite rule strictly decreases a well-founded measure (positive integers), no infinite reduction sequence exists. The system is strongly normalizing without requiring additional axioms, side conditions, or imported structural properties.
