**Yes – the system is strongly normalizing, and this can be established from the given syntax and rules alone.**

### Why

All of the rewrite rules can be oriented by a single **recursive path ordering (RPO)** defined purely on the signature of `Trace`.  No extra axioms, side-conditions, or imported facts are needed.

Take the precedence  
`cherry > banana > mango > peach > pear > grape > plum`.

Checking the rules:

* **R_mango_grape**, **R_cherry_refl**, **R_cherry_diff**: the left-hand side constructor (`mango` or `cherry`) is above the right-hand side constructor (`plum` or `mango`/`peach`) in the precedence, and each subterm required by the RPO comparison is a proper subterm of the left-hand side.
* **R_peach_plum_left**, **R_peach_plum_right**, **R_peach_cancel**, **R_banana_zero**: each right-hand side is a proper subterm of the left-hand side, so the subterm property of RPO applies directly.
* **R_apple_orange** (`banana b s (grape n) → pear s (banana b s n)`): `banana ≻ pear`.  The left-hand side is greater than each subterm of the right-hand side: it is greater than `s` by the subterm property, and greater than the inner `banana b s n` because the first two arguments are equal and `grape n ≻ n` (again by the subterm property).  Thus the whole left-hand side is greater than the `pear`-headed right-hand side.
* **R_cherry_diff**: `cherry ≻ mango`, and the left-hand side is greater than `peach a b` because `cherry ≻ peach` and `a`, `b` are proper subterms; hence it is greater than the `mango`-headed right-hand side.

Since every rule is contained in this RPO, and RPO is a well-founded simplification ordering closed under contexts and substitutions, **no infinite reduction sequence exists**.  The conditional rule `R_cherry_diff` causes no difficulty: equality on the inductive type `Trace` is decidable from the constructors alone (disjointness and injectivity), so the two `cherry` rules cover all cases, and the unconditional RPO comparison already orients the pattern `cherry x y → mango (peach x y)`.

Therefore, strong normalization follows by a standard, purely syntactic termination argument.
