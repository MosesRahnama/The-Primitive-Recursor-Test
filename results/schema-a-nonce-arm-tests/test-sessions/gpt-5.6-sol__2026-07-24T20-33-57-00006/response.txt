**Yes.** Strong normalization follows from the rewrite rules alone for ordinary finite first-order terms.

A recursive path ordering proves termination: choose a precedence with `Velk > Tarn` and give `Velk` lexicographic argument status. Then:

- `Velk(x,y,Mek) > x` by the subterm property.
- In the second rule, `Oru(n) > n` by the subterm property, so  
  `Velk(x,y,Oru(n)) > Velk(x,y,n)`.
- The left side also exceeds `y`, and since `Velk > Tarn`, it exceeds  
  `Tarn(y,Velk(x,y,n))`.

Thus every rewrite step strictly decreases a well-founded ordering. The duplication of `y` in the second rule does not invalidate this argument, and no structural or object-level axioms are needed.
