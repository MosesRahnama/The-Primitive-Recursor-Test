The user is asking about a first-order term rewriting system and whether strong normalization can be established from the presented rewrite system alone.
Yes, strong normalization can be established from the given rules alone.

The second rule is a strict recursion on the third argument: every reduction replaces `Velk(x, y, Oru(n))` with `Tarn(y, Velk(x, y, n))`, so the depth of the `Oru` structure strictly decreases. The first rule is a base case that eliminates the redex entirely. No other rules exist, so there are no ways to introduce new redexes with larger arguments or to loop back to previously seen terms. Any term therefore reduces in finitely many steps to a normal form (a term containing no ` Velk` whose third argument is `Mek` or `Oru(_)`), which is exactly what strong normalization requires. No additional structural axioms or object-level assumptions are needed; the termination property follows directly from the shape of the rules.
