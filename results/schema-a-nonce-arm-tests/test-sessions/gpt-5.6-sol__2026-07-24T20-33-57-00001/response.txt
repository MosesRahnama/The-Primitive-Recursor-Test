**Yes.** On ordinary finite first-order terms, the system is strongly normalizing using only the displayed rules.

A standard lexicographic path ordering can orient both rules. Choose `Velk` above `Tarn` in the precedence and give `Velk` a lexicographic status comparing its third argument first. Then:

- `Velk(x,y,Mek) > x` by the subterm property.
- In the recursive rule, `Oru(n) > n`, so  
  `Velk(x,y,Oru(n)) > Velk(x,y,n)`, and consequently it is also greater than  
  `Tarn(y,Velk(x,y,n))`.

Thus every rewrite step strictly decreases a well-founded reduction ordering. Duplication of `y` may increase term size, but does not invalidate this ordering argument. No exhaustiveness assumption about `Mek` and `Oru`, or other object-level axiom, is needed.
