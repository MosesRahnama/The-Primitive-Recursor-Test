**Yes.** Strong normalization follows syntactically from the rewrite rules alone for ordinary finite first-order terms.

A lexicographic path ordering can orient both rules by choosing `Velk` above `Tarn`:

- `Velk(x,y,Mek) > x`, since `x` is a proper subterm.
- For the second rule,
  `Velk(x,y,Oru(n)) > Velk(x,y,n)` because the first two arguments agree and `Oru(n) > n` by the subterm property.
- It also exceeds `y`, so
  `Velk(x,y,Oru(n)) > Tarn(y,Velk(x,y,n))`.

This ordering is well-founded and stable under substitutions and contexts. Although the second rule duplicates `y`, the whole right-hand side still decreases in the ordering. Thus no additional structural or object-level assumptions are needed.
