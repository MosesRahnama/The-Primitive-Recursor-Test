Yes— for the `Step` relation exactly as presented, strong normalization is provable from the calculus alone.

There is no contextual closure: `Step` only rewrites at the root. Every rule is of one of two kinds:

- It returns a strict subterm, as in the `merge` rules and `R_rec_zero`, so structural size strictly decreases.
- It produces a root-normal form:
  - `void`,
  - an `app`,
  - or `integrate (merge a b)`, which cannot match `integrate (delta t)` because constructors are disjoint.

Although `R_rec_succ` and `R_eq_diff` can increase term size, their results cannot take another `Step`. Thus, in any hypothetical infinite reduction, every step would have to be one of the strict-subterm steps, yielding an infinite descending sequence of natural-number term sizes, which is impossible.

No additional structural axioms, guards, or object-level assumptions are needed.
