### 1. Analysis of the Literal `Step` Relation (Without Congruence)
In the provided Lean 4 module, `Step` is defined strictly as an inductive proposition with 8 base rules and **no congruence (context) rules**. This means subterms cannot be rewritten; a step can only occur if the entire term matches the left-hand side of a rule at the root.

Under this literal definition, any sequence of steps is extremely short:
* The right-hand sides of the rules (such as `void`, `t`, `b`, `app s (recΔ b s n)`, and `integrate (merge a b)`) either do not match any left-hand side at the root, or can step at most once more (e.g., if a subterm $t$ becomes the root and happens to be reducible).
* There are no loops or infinite chains of root-level reductions. 
Thus, strong normalization holds trivially for the literal `Step` relation.

### 2. Analysis of the Compatible Closure (With Congruence)
Even if we assume the standard compatible closure of the rewrite system (allowing rewriting of subterms in any context), **strong normalization still holds**. 

We can prove this using the **Recursive Path Ordering (RPO)**, a standard well-founded termination ordering on first-order term rewrite systems:

1. **Define a precedence on the constructors:**
   $$\text{eqW} \succ \text{integrate} \succ \text{merge} \succ \text{app}$$
   $$\text{recΔ} \succ \text{app}$$
   $$\text{delta} \succ \text{void}$$

2. **Verify that all rewrite rules are strictly decreasing under RPO ($l \succ_{\text{RPO}} r$):**
   * **`R_rec_succ`**: `recΔ b s (delta n) → app s (recΔ b s n)`
     Since $\text{recΔ} \succ \text{app}$, we need to show:
     1. `recΔ b s (delta n)` $\succ_{\text{RPO}}$ `s` (holds because $s$ is a subterm of the LHS).
     2. `recΔ b s (delta n)` $\succ_{\text{RPO}}$ `recΔ b s n`. Since the head symbols are identical, we compare arguments lexicographically. The first two arguments `b` and `s` are identical, and the third argument `delta n` $\succ_{\text{RPO}}$ `n` because $n$ is a subterm. Thus, the RHS is strictly smaller.
   * **`R_eq_diff`**: `eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)`
     Since $\text{eqW} \succ \text{integrate}$ and $\text{eqW} \succ \text{merge}$, this is strictly decreasing because the subterms $a$ and $b$ are preserved.
   * **`R_int_delta`**: `integrate (delta t) → void`
     Decreases because a complex term is reduced to a constant.
   * **Simplification Rules** (`R_merge_void_left`, `R_merge_void_right`, `R_merge_cancel`, `R_rec_zero`, `R_eq_refl`):
     These are all projecting rules where the RHS is either a subterm of the LHS or a constant `void`, which strictly decreases under RPO.

Since the entire rewrite system is compatible with a well-founded Recursive Path Ordering, it is terminating (strongly normalizing) under any standard context closure. No external axioms or assumptions are needed.
