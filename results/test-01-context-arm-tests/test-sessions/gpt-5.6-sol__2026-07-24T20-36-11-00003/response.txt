Yes. For the displayed `Step` relation, strong normalization follows from the syntax and rules alone.

`Step` is a root-step relation; no contextual/congruence closure is defined. Use a lexicographic measure such as:

1. whether the term’s root matches any rule’s left-hand side, then
2. the structural size of the term.

Every step either:

- produces a root-normal term (`void`, `app ...`, or `integrate (merge ...)`), decreasing the first component; or
- returns a proper subterm (`merge` rules and `R_rec_zero`), decreasing structural size if that subterm is itself root-reducible.

Although `R_rec_succ` can increase ordinary term size, its result is headed by `app`, for which there is no `Step` rule. Likewise, `R_eq_diff` produces `integrate (merge a b)`, which cannot match `integrate (delta t)` by constructor disjointness.

Thus no infinite `Step` chain exists. No additional axioms, guards, or rewrite rules are needed.
