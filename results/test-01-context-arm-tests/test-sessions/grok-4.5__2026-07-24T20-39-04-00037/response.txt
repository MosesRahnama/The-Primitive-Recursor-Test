## Assessment: **Yes, SN can be established**

The relation `Step` is given exactly by the eight root-level constructors shown; there is no contextual/congruence closure. Consequently a reduction step can occur only at the root of a term.

Inspection of the rules yields a uniform observation:

- `R_int_delta`, `R_merge_void_left`, `R_merge_void_right`, `R_merge_cancel`, `R_rec_zero` and `R_eq_refl` all send a term to a proper subterm (or to the irreducible constant `void`);
- `R_rec_succ` sends a term to an `app`-headed term, and no rule has `app` as its left-hand-side root, so the reduct is irreducible;
- `R_eq_diff` sends a term to an `integrate`-headed term whose argument is a `merge`, which does not match `R_int_delta`, so the reduct is likewise irreducible.

Thus every one-step reduct is either a structurally smaller term or a normal form. By ordinary induction on the inductive structure of `Trace` every term is therefore strongly normalizing: any putative infinite `Step`-chain would have to leave a proper subterm after finitely many steps, contradicting the inductive hypothesis, or would have to continue from an irreducible term, which is impossible.

No additional rewrite rules, side-conditions, congruence axioms or external well-foundedness principles are required; the argument uses only the given syntax of `Trace` and the given clauses of `Step`.
