### Termination Argument
We employ the dependency pair (DP) method together with a monotone algebra interpretation over the natural numbers \(\mathbb{N}\) (with the standard order) that yields a reduction pair \((\gtrsim, >)\). The interpretation is defined on function symbols as follows (and extended homomorphically to all terms, with variables ranging over arbitrary values in \(\mathbb{N}\)):

\[

[c_0] &= 0,\\
[f_1](x) &= x + 1,\\
[f_0](x, y) &= x + y,\\
[f_2](x, y) &= x \cdot (y + 1),\\

\end{align*}
\]

(The factorial and exponential are the standard meta-level operations on \(\mathbb{N}\); \(0! = 1\).) Each interpreting function is weakly monotone in every argument. Consequently the induced relations
\[
t \gtrsim u \iff [t] \ge [u], \qquad t > u \iff [t] > [u]
\]
form a reduction pair: \(\gtrsim\) is a stable, weakly monotone quasi-order and \(>\) is a stable well-founded order compatible with \(\gtrsim\).
