I chose the **Lexicographic Path Ordering (LPO)** because it is the most direct, elegant, and purely syntactic tool for this specific shape of rewrite rules, and it perfectly obeys your strict constraint to not import external axioms or object-level assumptions.

Here is a breakdown of exactly why LPO was the ideal fit for this system:

**1. It is purely syntactic (No hidden arithmetic or axioms)**
Your prompt explicitly forbade external memory, imported assumptions, or structural axioms. Methods like *Polynomial Interpretations* require mapping terms to natural numbers and proving arithmetic inequalities (e.g., `cons(x, y) = x + y + 1`). While valid, this imports the axioms of arithmetic to prove termination. LPO, by contrast, operates entirely on the syntax of the terms themselves and requires nothing more than declaring a simple ranking (precedence) of the function symbols.

**2. The recursion is strictly structural (The Subterm Property)**
If you look at the recursive step in Rule 3:
`exec_walk_weight(w, cons(x, cons(y, xs)))` calls `exec_walk_weight(w, cons(y, xs))`
The system is just iterating down a list. The argument `cons(y, xs)` is a strict, deeply nested subterm of the original argument `cons(x, cons(y, xs))`. 
LPO is built on Kruskal’s Tree Theorem, meaning it inherently possesses the **subterm property**: it automatically "knows" that a whole structure is strictly greater than any of its constituent parts. Because the recursion shrinks structurally, a path ordering is tailor-made for it.

**3. Effortless handling of spawned symbols (`add` and `app`)**
In Rule 3, the RHS introduces function symbols that weren't at the head of the LHS: `add` and `app`. 
To prove termination, we have to prove that these newly generated terms don't cause infinite growth. Because all the variables fed into `add` and `app` (`w`, `x`, `y`) are just extracted from the LHS, LPO allows us to instantly "neutralize" these new functions simply by stating that the original head symbol (`exec_walk_weight`) has a higher precedence than the spawned symbols (`add`, `app`). LPO does all the heavy lifting to verify that these sub-branches are structurally smaller than the starting term.
