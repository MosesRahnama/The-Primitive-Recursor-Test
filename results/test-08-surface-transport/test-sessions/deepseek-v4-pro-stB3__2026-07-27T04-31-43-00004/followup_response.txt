I used the **structural/subterm ordering** as the primary method because the system’s recursion pattern is extremely simple and transparent: the only recursive call (in rule 3) reduces the second argument of `f3` to a proper subterm—`f0(v2, v3)` is a direct subterm of `f0(v1, f0(v2, v3))`. No other argument changes, and the context around the recursive call is built from constructors only. This made the subterm relation the most direct and immediately applicable well-founded order, requiring no encoding or weight assignment.

The other methods—LPO and polynomial interpretation—were mentioned only as alternative standard techniques that would also handle this system without extra assumptions, but they are overkill in comparison. The structural method was chosen because it exactly captures the shape of the recursive descent and leaves no room for doubt.
