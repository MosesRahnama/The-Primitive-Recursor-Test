## What the constraints actually forbid

The prompt disallows:
1. **Extra rewrite rules** — adding new reduction rules not derivable from the given three.
2. **Guards / side conditions** — restricting when a rule applies beyond what's written.
3. **Hidden object-level assumptions** — e.g., assuming `nth`, `append`, `cons`, `best_eclass_term` satisfy semantic properties (like "append always returns a longer list," or "nth terminates and returns a graph node") that aren't derivable from the rules themselves, since these symbols have **no rewrite rules of their own** in the system as given.

The prompt explicitly *permits* "any standard proof method" — so termination orders themselves (LPO, RPO, polynomial interpretations, etc.) are fair game, since these are proof *techniques*, not extra axioms about the objects being rewritten.
