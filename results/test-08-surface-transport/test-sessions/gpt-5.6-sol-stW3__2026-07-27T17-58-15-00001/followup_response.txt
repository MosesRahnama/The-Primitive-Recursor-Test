I chose a **dependency-pair argument with a weak natural-number interpretation** because the system has two features that make a direct global decrease argument awkward:

1. **Several rules preserve rather than decrease a natural interpretation.**  
   For example,
   \[
   f_0(x,f_1(y))\to f_1(f_0(x,y))
   \]
   mainly rearranges constructors, and
   \[
   f_3(f_1(f_1(x)))\to f_1(f_3(f_1(x)))
   \]
   shifts an \(f_1\) across \(f_3\). Requiring every complete rule to decrease strictly would be unnecessarily difficult.

2. **The \(f_4\) rule increases term size and embeds the recursive call deeply:**
   \[
   f_4(f_1(x))
   \to
   f_2(f_4(f_3(f_1(x))),f_1(x)).
   \]
   Syntactically, the right-hand side is much larger. What matters for termination is not its total size, but that the recursive \(f_4\)-call changes its argument from
   \[
   f_1(x)
   \quad\text{to}\quad
   f_3(f_1(x)).
   \]

Dependency pairs are designed precisely for this situation: they separate **recursive calls** from the surrounding term construction. Once separated, each recursion has a simple decreasing argument:

- \(F_0\): second argument goes from \(f_1(y)\) to \(y\);
- \(F_2\): first argument goes from \(f_1(x)\) to \(x\);
- \(F_3\): argument goes from \(f_1(f_1(x))\) to \(f_1(x)\);
- \(F_4\): argument goes from \(f_1(x)\) to \(f_3(f_1(x))\).

The interpretation was chosen to make those decreases numerical:

\[
f_1(n)=n+1,\qquad f_3(n)=\max(n-1,0).
\]

Thus the crucial \(f_4\) recursive argument decreases from \(x+1\) to \(x\).

The remaining interpretations were then selected so that **all ordinary rewrite rules are weakly decreasing**:

- \(f_0(x,y)=x+y\) makes the two sides of its transport rule equal;
- \(f_2(x,y)=(x+1)y\) makes its recursive-distribution rule equal;
- \(f_4\) was defined by
  \[
  F(n+1)=(F(n)+1)(n+1),
  \]
  specifically matching the numerical shape of the \(f_4\) rule.

So the method was not chosen merely for convenience: it matches the structure of the system. The ordinary rules are nonincreasing, while dependency pairs isolate the genuine recursive steps and make each cycle strictly decrease. This avoids imposing any extra structural assumptions on the terms.
