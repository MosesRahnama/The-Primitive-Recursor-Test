I chose the Recursive Path Ordering (RPO) because it is a standard, well‑founded rewrite order that works directly on the syntax of first‑order terms without needing any extra rules, guards, side conditions, or external memory. The given system is plain first‑order with no binding, no AC symbols, and no imported axioms—exactly the kind of setting where RPO is complete and straightforward to apply. With a carefully selected precedence and lexicographic status, every rule can be oriented strictly downward, which is sufficient to guarantee termination. No strengthening or external libraries are required, and the argument stays entirely inside the provided rewrite rules.
