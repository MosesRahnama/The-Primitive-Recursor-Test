I chose the **dependency-pair method** because the system is terminating for operational reasons that ordinary global size/order arguments do not express well.

In particular, the last rule contains the recursive call

\[
f_4(f_1(x))\to \cdots f_4(f_3(f_1(x)))\cdots
\]

whose recursive argument is syntactically **larger**: \(f_3(f_1(x))\) contains \(f_1(x)\) as a proper subterm. Consequently, no ordinary simplification order with the subterm property can directly orient that recursive call as decreasing. Simple symbol count or term depth also increases in several rules, and some right-hand sides duplicate terms.

Dependency pairs handle this by separating:

1. the recursive calls that could sustain nontermination, from
2. the intervening ordinary rewrites that make those calls decrease operationally.

After that separation, the dependency graph is especially simple: its only cyclic parts are the individual \(F_0\), \(F_2\), \(F_3\), and \(F_4\) self-components. Cross-symbol calls such as \(F_4\to F_2\) and \(F_2\to F_0\) cannot return to \(F_4\), so they cannot sustain an infinite chain.

The common resource consumed in each cyclic component is the available leading \(f_1\)-depth. For \(F_0,F_2,\) and \(F_3\), this decrease is nearly syntactic. The main reason for the more semantic measure was \(F_4\): although

\[
f_1(x)\mapsto f_3(f_1(x))
\]

looks like growth, the two \(f_3\)-rules ensure that, after rewriting, \(f_3(f_1(x))\) exposes one fewer leading \(f_1\) than would be required to invoke \(f_4\) again. The measure \(\mu\) formalizes exactly that fact.

Minimal dependency chains were used because their instantiated proper subterms are terminating. That permits defining \(\mu\) through reachable normal forms without assuming global termination in advance, avoiding circular reasoning.

So the method was chosen because it:

- accommodates syntactic growth and duplication;
- isolates the only potentially infinite recursive cycles;
- captures the operational decrease hidden behind the \(f_3\) rewrites;
- covers nonrecursive rules automatically as intervening \(R\)-steps;
- requires no added axioms, guards, rules, or assumptions about what the terms represent.
