The user is asking why I picked the dependency-pair method with a weakly monotone interpretation for proving termination of that TRS.
I chose the dependency-pair method with a weakly monotone interpretation because the rules show nested recursion and mutual calls (like f4 depending on f2 and f3, which in turn depend on f0 and f1) that make a direct lexicographic path order or Knuth-Bendix order tricky to set up cleanly.
