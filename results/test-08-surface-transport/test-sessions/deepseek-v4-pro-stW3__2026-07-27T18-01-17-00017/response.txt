### Termination argument

Defined symbols: \(f_0, f_2, f_3, f_4\).

#### 2. Interpretation

Interpretations of symbols:

#### 4. Dependency pairs are strictly decreasing
For every DP \(l^\# \to r^\#\) we need \(\lbrack l^\#\rbrack > \lbrack r^\#\rbrack\):

1. \(f_0^\#(x, f_1(y)) \to f_0^\#(x, y)\): \(x+y+1 > x+y\)
