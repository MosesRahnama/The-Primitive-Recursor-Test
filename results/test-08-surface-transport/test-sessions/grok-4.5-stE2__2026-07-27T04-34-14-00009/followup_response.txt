### Why RPO

I chose the recursive path order because it is a purely syntactic, off-the-shelf simplification order that works directly on the three rules as written. It needs only a precedence on the function symbols that already appear; it does not invent auxiliary algebras, polynomials, or extra rewrite rules, and it does not rely on any semantic reading of `append`, `nth`, `best_eclass_term`, etc.  

The same orientation can be obtained with a simple multiset measure on the third arguments of `extract_prefix` together with a count of `extract_prefix_k` symbols, but that measure is essentially the concrete instance of the multiset path order that RPO generalises. Stating the argument in RPO therefore makes the well-foundedness appeal immediate (RPO is known to be well-founded on any term algebra) while still discharging every rule by a routine precedence-and-subterm check. No weaker or more exotic method is required.
