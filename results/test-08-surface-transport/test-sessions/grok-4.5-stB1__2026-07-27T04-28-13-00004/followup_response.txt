The length measure is the most direct well-founded order that is immediately visible from the equations themselves.  

- The two base cases are non-recursive.  
- The single recursive equation replaces its list argument \(x\#y\#xs\) by the strictly shorter list \(y\#xs\).  
- List length is a natural number, the usual order on \(\mathbb{N}\) is well-founded, and every recursive call therefore decreases that measure by exactly 1.  

No richer order (multiset, lexicographic combination, etc.) is required, and nothing beyond the inductive structure already present in the list constructors needs to be assumed. That makes the argument both minimal and complete with respect to the given defining equations.
