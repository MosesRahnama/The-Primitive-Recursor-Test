The user is asking why I picked the method I picked for proving termination of the TRS.
I chose monotone algebraic interpretations into the natural numbers because the rules involve deeply nested exponential growth (especially with f2 and f4), which quickly outstrips what polynomials or even matrix interpretations can handle. While recursive path orders might seem tempting, the rapid growth and the way f4 unfolds into f2 make it tricky to find a precedence and status that works cleanly without extra rules or case analysis. Algebraic interpretations give a direct, monotone order on the naturals that respects the signature and makes the decrease in each rule obvious once the right exponential functions are picked.
