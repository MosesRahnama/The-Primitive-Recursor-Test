The helper theorems are sound. By definition of `kappa`, we have

\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,(\delta n))=\max(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n)+1

\[
\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,n)=\max(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n)

holds unconditionally (the `+1` case is triggered only by an outermost constructor `delta` on the third argument). This immediately yields the first identity. Substituting it into the second goal produces
\[
\kappa(\mathsf{app}\,s\,(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,n))=\max(\kappa s,\max(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n))=\max(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n),
\]
which is strictly smaller than the right-hand side, as required.
