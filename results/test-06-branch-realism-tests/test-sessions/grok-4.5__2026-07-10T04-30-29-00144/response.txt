### Assessment

The proposed helper strategy is **not sound** as written. Both claimed theorems are false (they do not hold for arbitrary \(b,s,n\)), so they cannot be used as intermediate lemmas in a termination argument for `Step` via the measure `kappa`.

#### Calculations showing `kappa_rec_delta_step` is false
By definition of `kappa` we always have
\[
\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,(\mathsf{delta}\,n))
=
\max\bigl(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n\bigr)+1.
\]
The value of the right-hand side \(\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,n)+1\), however, depends on the shape of \(n\):

- If \(n\) is *not* of the form \(\mathsf{delta}\,\_\), the second equation for `kappa` applies and we obtain equality.
- If \(n=\mathsf{delta}\,m\), the first equation for `kappa` applies instead:
  \[
  \kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,n)
  =
  \max\bigl(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa m\bigr)+1
  =
  \max\bigl(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n\bigr)+1,
  \]
  so the claimed equality becomes
  \[
  \max\bigl(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n\bigr)+1
  =
  \bigl(\max\bigl(\max(\kappa b,\kappa s),\kappa n\bigr)+1\bigr)+1,
  \]
  which is absurd.

Concrete counter-example (\(b=s=\mathsf{void}\), \(n=\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void}\)):
\[
\begin{align*}
\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,\mathsf{void}\,\mathsf{void}\,(\mathsf{delta}\,(\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void})))
&=
\max(\max(0,0),\kappa(\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void}))+1
=1,\\
\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,\mathsf{void}\,\mathsf{void}\,(\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void}))+1
&=
\bigl(\max(\max(0,0),\kappa\,\mathsf{void})+1\bigr)+1
=2.
\end{align*}
\]
Thus \(1\neq 2\).

#### Calculations showing `kappa_rec_succ_drop` is false
The same family of counter-examples also falsifies the second claim. With the values above we have
\[
\kappa(\mathsf{app}\,\mathsf{void}\,(\mathsf{recDelta}\,\mathsf{void}\,\mathsf{void}\,(\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void})))
=
\max\bigl(0,\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,\mathsf{void}\,\mathsf{void}\,(\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void}))\bigr)
=
\max(0,1)=1
\]
while
\[
\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,\mathsf{void}\,\mathsf{void}\,(\mathsf{delta}\,(\mathsf{delta}\,\mathsf{void})))=1,
\]
so the required strict inequality \(1<1\) fails.

(The same phenomenon occurs for any deeper nesting of `delta` constructors: after one application of `R_rec_succ` the measure stays constant rather than decreasing.)

#### Why the strategy cannot be repaired “as written”
Even if one ignores the first (false) lemma and tries to prove the drop inequality directly from the definition of `kappa`, the same counter-examples show that
\[
\kappa(\mathsf{app}\,s\,(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,n))
\not<
\kappa(\mathsf{recDelta}\,b\,s\,(\mathsf{delta}\,n))
\]
whenever \(n\) itself begins with `delta`. Consequently the two helpers, taken exactly as stated, do not establish that every `Step` decreases `kappa`, and the intended termination argument is blocked.

(The underlying measure itself is simply not a valid ranking function for the given rewrite rules; a correct measure would have to take the *depth* of the third argument of every `recDelta` into account.)
