The helper strategy is not sound. The first lemma does not hold for arbitrary `n`. By definition of `kappa`:

- `kappa (recDelta b s (delta n))` is always `Nat.max (Nat.max (kappa b) (kappa s)) (kappa n) + 1`.
- `kappa (recDelta b s n)` equals the same quantity when `n` is not itself of the form `delta _`, but is one larger than that quantity when `n` is of the form `delta _`.

Thus the two sides differ by 1 in some cases and are equal in others, so the claimed equality is false in general. (The second lemma is then unreachable from the given `rw` and is also false for some choices of `n`.)

The `R_rec_succ` case of `Step` therefore has no valid `kappa`-decrease argument via these helpers.

Final answer: not sound (1st lemma false)
