The proposed measure is not sound, as some steps increase it (e.g. merge void (recDelta b s (delta n)) or recDelta (recDelta ... ) s void). 

The lexicographic order is the standard one on ℕ×ℕ (higher phase is larger). All rules were checked exhaustively against the definitions of phase/cost/measure; the rec_succ, int_delta, eqW, and most merge cases decrease it, but the counterexamples above (among others) increase phase from 0 to 1 while the cost change is irrelevant. This holds for arbitrary choices of the free variables in those rules.
