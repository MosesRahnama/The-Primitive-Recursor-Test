The proposed measure is the pair \((\mathit{phase}(t),\mathit{cost}(t))\) ordered lexicographically, where
\[
\begin{align*}
\mathit{phase}(t)&=1&&\text{if }t=\mathtt{recDelta}\_\_(\mathtt{delta}\_),\\
\mathit{phase}(t)&=0&&\text{otherwise},
\end{align*}
\]
and \(\mathit{cost}\) is the obvious additive size function (with a constant offset of 3 on \(\mathtt{eqW}\)).

A measure is sound for termination precisely when every one-step redex contracts to a term of strictly smaller measure.  It is therefore enough to examine each of the eight rewrite rules in turn.

- \(\mathtt{integrate}(\mathtt{delta}\,t)\to\mathtt{void}\).  
  Both sides have phase 0 and the cost drops from \(2+\mathit{cost}(t)\) to 0.

- \(\mathtt{eqW}\,a\,a\to\mathtt{void}\).  
  Both sides have phase 0 and the cost drops from \(3+2\cdot\mathit{cost}(a)\) to 0.

- \(\mathtt{eqW}\,a\,b\to\mathtt{integrate}(\mathtt{merge}\,a\,b)\).  
  Both sides have phase 0 and the cost drops from \(3+\mathit{cost}(a)+\mathit{cost}(b)\) to \(2+\mathit{cost}(a)+\mathit{cost}(b)\).

- \(\mathtt{recDelta}\,b\,s\,(\mathtt{delta}\,n)\to\mathtt{app}\,s\,(\mathtt{recDelta}\,b\,s\,n)\).  
  The left-hand side has phase 1 while the right-hand side has phase 0, so the lexicographic measure decreases regardless of the (possibly larger) cost of the right-hand side.

The remaining four rules, however, can increase the measure.  Consider an arbitrary term \(u\) of phase 1 (for example \(u=\mathtt{recDelta}\,\mathtt{void}\,\mathtt{void}\,(\mathtt{delta}\,\mathtt{void})\)).  Then each of the following redexes has phase 0 on the left and phase 1 on the right:

\[
\begin{align*}
\mathtt{merge}\,\mathtt{void}\,u&\to u,\\
\mathtt{merge}\,u\,\mathtt{void}&\to u,\\
\mathtt{merge}\,u\,u&\to u,\\
\mathtt{recDelta}\,b\,s\,\mathtt{void}&\to b
\quad\text{(taking \(b=u\))}.
\end{align*}
\]
In every case the phase component rises, so the lexicographic measure rises.

Consequently the proposed measure does not decrease on every rewrite step and is not a sound termination measure for the calculus.
