### Understanding the Measure

The measure for a term `t` is a pair of natural numbers: `measure t = (phase t, cost t)`.
The comparison between two measures `m1 = (p1, c1)` and `m2 = (p2, c2)` is lexicographic. `m1 > m2` if and only if:
1.  `p1 > p2`, or
2.  `p1 = p2` and `c1 > c2`.

For the measure to be sound, for every rule `Step t u`, it must be the case that `measure t > measure u`.

Every rewrite rule in `Step` causes a strict decrease in the `measure` value under the lexicographic ordering of `(phase, cost)`.
