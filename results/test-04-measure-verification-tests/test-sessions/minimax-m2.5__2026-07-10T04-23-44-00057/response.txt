The measure `(phase, cost)` with lexicographic ordering is not sound for the given rewrite system, as it does not guarantee decrease for all rewrite steps. Specifically, several rules can lead to an increase in the measure:

- **R_merge_void_left** and **R_merge_void_right**: When `t` has `phase(t) = 1` (e.g., `t = recDelta b s (delta n)`), the left-hand side has `phase = 0` and cost `1 + cost t`, while the right-hand side has `phase = 1` and cost `cost t`. Since `1 > 0`, the measure increases.
- **R_merge_cancel**: Similarly, when `t` has `phase(t) = 1`, the left-hand side has `phase = 0` and cost `1 + 2 * cost t`, while the right-hand side has `phase = 1` and cost `cost t`, leading to an increase.
- **R_rec_zero**: When `b` has `phase(b) = 1`, the left-hand side has `phase = 0` and cost `1 + cost b + cost s`, while the right-hand side has `phase = 1` and cost `cost b`, again increasing the measure.

Thus, the measure fails to satisfy the required property that `measure(t') < measure(t)` for every step `t → t'`.
