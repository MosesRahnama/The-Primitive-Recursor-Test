The proposed lexicographic measure (phase, cost) is not sound. There exist Step instances that increase it (e.g. R_merge_void_left or R_merge_void_right or R_merge_cancel or R_rec_zero when the RHS has phase 1 while the LHS has phase 0). 

(The recDelta rules are the only ones that can involve phase 1, but the other rules apply unconditionally to arbitrary subterms and can increase the primary component of the measure.)
