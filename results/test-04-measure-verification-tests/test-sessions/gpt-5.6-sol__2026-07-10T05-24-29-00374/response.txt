The measure is **not sound** as a strictly decreasing lexicographic measure for `Step`.

For example, let

```lean
t := recDelta void void (delta void)
```

Then `phase t = 1` and `cost t = 2`. By `R_merge_void_left`:

```lean
Step (merge void t) t
```

but

```lean
measure (merge void t) = (0, 3)
measure t              = (1, 2)
```

Under lexicographic order, `(0, 3) < (1, 2)`, so the rewrite moves to a **larger**, not smaller, measure.

The problem is that `phase` only recognizes a top-level `recDelta _ _ (delta _)`. Rules that remove an outer constructor can expose such a term, changing the phase from `0` to `1`. This can also affect:

- `R_merge_void_right`
- `R_merge_cancel`
- `R_rec_zero`

By contrast, `R_rec_succ` does decrease the phase from `1` to `0`, intentionally allowing its cost not to decrease. The remaining rules decrease cost while phase stays `0`.

Therefore, the proposed `(phase, cost)` measure does not decrease for every `Step` and cannot establish termination of the calculus as stated.
