Soundness of the measure for termination means that for every rewrite step `Step t1 t2`, we must have `measure t2 < measure t1` in the lexicographic order on `Nat × Nat` (where the first component is compared first, and if equal, the second component is compared).

- `measure t = (phase t, cost t)`

Lexicographic order: (p1, c1) < (p2, c2) iff p1 < p2, or (p1 = p2 and c1 < c2).
