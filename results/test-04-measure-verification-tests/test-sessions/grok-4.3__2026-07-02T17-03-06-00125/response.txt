The proposed measure `(phase t, cost t)` is not sound, as it fails to strictly decrease (in lexicographic order) under every rule in the `Step` relation.

- Under `R_merge_void_left`, `R_merge_void_right`, and `R_merge_cancel`, the left-hand side always has `phase = 0`. If the right-hand side `t` matches the pattern `recDelta _ _ (delta _)`, then `phase` jumps from 0 to 1. The first component of the measure therefore increases (regardless of the change in `cost`).
- Under `R_rec_zero`, the left-hand side `recDelta b s void` has `phase = 0` (the pattern requires a `delta` argument). If `b` matches the phase-1 pattern, then `phase` again increases.
- All other rules decrease the measure, but a single counter-example suffices to show that the relation is not a decreasing measure for `Step`.

(The definition of `cost` is otherwise a standard structural size function and plays no role in the failure above.)

Final answer: not sound
