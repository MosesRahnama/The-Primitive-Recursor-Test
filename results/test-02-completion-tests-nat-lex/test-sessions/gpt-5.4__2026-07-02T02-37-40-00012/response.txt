Important note: two of the remaining cases as written are not obviously provable by the stated `(kappa, mu)` measure without extra lemmas or a different argument:

- `recDelta b s void --> b`
- `eqW a a --> void`

For `eqW a a --> void`, `drop_right` requires
`kappa void = kappa (eqW a a)`, i.e. `0 = kappa a`, which is false in general.

For `recDelta b s void --> b`, similarly `kappa b = kappa (recDelta b s void)` means
`kappa b = max (max (kappa b) (kappa s)) 0`, also false in general.

So a genuine completion likely needs one of:

1. a strengthened/changed measure,
2. side conditions on the rules,
3. or a different orientation/auxiliary lemma than the current `drop_left` / `drop_right`.
