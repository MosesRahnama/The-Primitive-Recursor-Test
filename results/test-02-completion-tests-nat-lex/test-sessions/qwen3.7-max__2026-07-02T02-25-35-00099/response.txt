### Explanation of the `R_rec_succ` Measure Flaw
For the rule `recDelta b s (delta n) --> app s (recDelta b s n)`:
- **LHS Measure:** `kappa` evaluates to `max(..., kappa n) + 1`. `mu` evaluates to `3 + mu b + mu s + mu n`.
- **RHS Measure (when `n = delta n'`):** `kappa` evaluates to `max(kappa s, max(..., kappa n') + 1)`, which simplifies back to `max(..., kappa n') + 1`. Thus, **`kappa` ties**. Meanwhile, `mu` evaluates to `1 + mu s + (3 + mu b + mu s + mu n')`, which simplifies to `4 + mu b + 2*mu s + mu n'`. Thus, **`mu` strictly increases** by `1 + mu s`. 

To fully close the `R_rec_succ` case without `sorry`, the `mu` measure would need to be redefined to assign a weight of `0` to the `app` constructor when its right child is a `recDelta`, or `kappa` needs to track structural depth more aggressively to force a strict drop.
