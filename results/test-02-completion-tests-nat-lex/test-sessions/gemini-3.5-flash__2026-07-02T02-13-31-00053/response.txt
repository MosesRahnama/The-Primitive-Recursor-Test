Three of the remaining cases (`R_int_delta`, `R_eq_refl`, and `R_eq_diff`) can be proved fully with standard tactics, while the two recursion cases (`R_rec_zero` and `R_rec_succ`) are provided with clear skeletons using `sorry` for the nested inequality lemmas on `kappa`.

    · exact drop_right hmu h
