| _, _, Step.R_eq_diff a b => by

This skeleton mirrors the style of the three proved `merge` cases (using `drop_left`/`drop_right` after establishing the required `kappa`/`mu` relations via `simp` + `omega`). The mixed cases (`R_int_delta`, `R_rec_zero`, `R_eq_refl`) branch on whether `kappa` drops strictly (using `drop_left`) or stays equal (using `drop_right` with a `mu` drop, which holds in those rules). The `R_eq_diff` case is purely `drop_right` (kappa equal, mu drops by 1). The `R_rec_succ` case uses the same branching pattern; the `kappa` comparison holds by case analysis on the shape of `n` (when `n` is not headed by `delta` the `kappa` drops; when it is, `kappa` ties and the `mu` relation is asserted for the skeleton).
