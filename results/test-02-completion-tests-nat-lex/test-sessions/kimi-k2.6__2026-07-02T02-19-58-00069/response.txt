**Explanation of the remaining cases:**

- **`R_int_delta` / `R_eq_refl`**: The reduct is `void`, so its measure is `(0,0)`. The source term may have `kappa = 0` (in which case kappa ties and `mu` strictly drops) or `kappa > 0` (in which case `kappa` strictly drops). A `by_cases` on the equality of the first components decides which helper lemma applies.
- **`R_rec_zero`**: Here `kappa b ≤ kappa (recDelta b s void)`, but the inequality may be strict or an equality depending on whether `kappa s` is larger. Either way the lexicographic measure drops, so again we branch on equality.
- **`R_rec_succ`**: This is the recursive-call rule. Unwrapping the `delta` removes the `+1` from `kappa`, so the first component of the measure strictly decreases regardless of the subterms.
- **`R_eq_diff`**: Kappa is preserved (`max` is idempotent in the sense that `kappa (eqW a b) = max (kappa a) (kappa b) = kappa (merge a b) = kappa (integrate (merge a b))`), while `mu` drops by exactly 1.
