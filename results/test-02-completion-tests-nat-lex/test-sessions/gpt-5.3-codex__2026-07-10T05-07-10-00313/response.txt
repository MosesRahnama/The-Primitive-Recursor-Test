### Important note
Two of your intended `drop_right` equalities are generally not true as stated:

- `kappa b = kappa (recDelta b s void)` (only if `kappa b` dominates `kappa s`)
- `kappa void = kappa (eqW a a)` (only if `kappa a = 0`)

So in those cases, you’ll likely need to prove **left drop** (`kappa` strict decrease) instead, or adjust the local proof strategy accordingly. The other three cases are straightforward with the above shape.
