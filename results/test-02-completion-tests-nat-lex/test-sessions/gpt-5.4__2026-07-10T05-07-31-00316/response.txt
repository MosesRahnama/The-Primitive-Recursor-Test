Notes on what this reveals:

- `R_int_delta` and `R_eq_diff` should go through exactly by `drop_right` with `simp`.
- `R_rec_succ` should go through by `drop_left`; the result has kappa at most the predecessor-max, while the source has that value plus `1`.
- `R_rec_zero` and `R_eq_refl` appear not provable with the current `kappa`:
  - `kappa (recDelta b s void) = max (max (kappa b) (kappa s)) 0`, not generally `kappa b`.
  - `kappa (eqW a a) = kappa a`, not generally `0 = kappa a`.

So for those two, a completed proof likely requires one of:
1. a different intended measure,
2. side conditions on the rules,
3. or a typo in the current definitions/rules.
