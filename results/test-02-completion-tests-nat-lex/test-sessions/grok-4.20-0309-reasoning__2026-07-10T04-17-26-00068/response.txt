    · exact drop_left h
    · have hk : kappa b = kappa (recDelta b s void) := by simp [kappa] at *; omega

For `R_rec_succ` the structure is analogous but requires splitting on the shape of the third argument to `recDelta` (via `split` on the `kappa` definition) to establish the relationship between the measures in each subcase.
