4. R_eq_refl a:
   by_cases h : 0 < kappa a
   · exact drop_left (by linarith)
   · have hk : kappa void = kappa (eqW a a) := by simp [kappa]
     have hmu : mu void < mu (eqW a a) := by simp [mu]; omega
     exact drop_right hmu hk

5. R_eq_diff a b:
   have hmu : mu (integrate (merge a b)) < mu (eqW a b) := by simp [mu]; omega
   have hk : kappa (integrate (merge a b)) = kappa (eqW a b) := by simp [kappa]
   exact drop_right hmu hk

Note: In the R_rec_succ non-delta case, the `simp [kappa]` will simplify both sides, and then `omega` can prove the inequality because the left side is at most M and the right side is M+1, where M = max(max(kappa b, kappa s), kappa n).

In the delta case of R_rec_succ, we put `sorry` because we cannot provide a correct proof with the given measure. This is honest.

For the other cases, the proofs are complete.

We output the skeletons in the order of the cases in the file.
