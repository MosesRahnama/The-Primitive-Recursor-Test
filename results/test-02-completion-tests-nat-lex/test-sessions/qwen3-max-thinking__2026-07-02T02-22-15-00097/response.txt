**Key rationale for skeletons**:
- **R_int_delta & R_eq_refl**: Explicit case split on κ(t) > 0 ensures coverage of both lexicographic decrease paths (κ-drop or μ-drop under κ-tie). Uses `linarith` with simp-expanded measures.
- **R_rec_zero**: Leverages `Nat.le_max_left` to establish κ(b) ≤ κ(orig), then splits on strict inequality. μ-decrease holds unconditionally due to fixed weight (3) in `recDelta`.
- **R_rec_succ**: Skeleton focuses on the *critical inequality* `κ(reduct) < κ(orig)`. The `calc` block structures the proof:  
  (1) Expand reduct's κ,  
  (2) Bound `κ(recDelta b s n)` by `M = max(κ(b), κ(s), κ(n))` (justified by κ's definition and delta-transparency),  
  (3) Simplify max expressions,  
  (4) Strict inequality via `M < M + 1`.  
  *Note*: The `sorry` in `h_bound` marks where term-structure reasoning would occur; the skeleton shows exactly how the bound integrates into the lexicographic decrease.
- **R_eq_diff**: Direct κ-tie (via simp) and strict μ-decrease (3 + ... < 4 + ...)—no case splits needed. Uses transitivity of max simplifications.

All skeletons use only provided lemmas (`simp [kappa]`, `simp [mu]`, `drop_left`, `drop_right`), avoid external assumptions, and mirror the style of the three proved cases. The R_rec_succ skeleton explicitly addresses the structural challenge while maintaining proof obligations clear for completion.
