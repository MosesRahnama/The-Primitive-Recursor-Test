1. **Method:** A recursive path ordering (RPO), specifically with multiset status and a chosen precedence/quasi-precedence on symbols.

2. **External choice:** Yes, the ordering and precedence are meta-level proof choices not fixed by the rewrite rules. However, they are a termination certificate, not additional rewrite rules or object-level assumptions.

3. **Boundary compliance:** **No.** The instruction explicitly permits standard proof methods. Choosing a well-founded reduction ordering does not strengthen or modify the calculus, provided every rule is actually shown to decrease under it.

4. **Strong normalization:** **Yes.** Independently of intended arithmetic semantics, the rules appear orientable by such a well-founded simplification ordering, including the duplicating `exp` rules and the mutually calling `le`/`lt` and `ge`/`gt` rules. Thus I still assess the TRS as strongly normalizing.
