No. The rewrite system is not strongly normalizing: the rule  
`SUC(NUMERAL(n)) → NUMERAL(SUC(n))` (and analogous rules for `plus`, `mult`, etc.) can be applied repeatedly, producing infinitely growing terms such as  

```
SUC(NUMERAL(0)) → NUMERAL(SUC(0)) → SUC(NUMERAL(SUC(0))) → NUMERAL(SUC(SUC(0))) → …
```  

Since no rule reduces the resulting `NUMERAL`‑terms any further, the rewrite sequence never reaches a normal form. Hence termination cannot be established from the given rules alone.
