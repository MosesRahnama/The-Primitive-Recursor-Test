The given TRS (including the uninterpreted symbol `if`) is strongly normalizing.

All rules are length-decreasing on the binary spine of their numeric arguments (or replace a defined symbol by strictly simpler defined symbols whose own arguments are likewise smaller). Consequently a lexicographic path order (or the dependency-pair framework) oriented by any precedence
```
exp > mult > plus > SUC > PRE > minus > \ldots > {BIT0,BIT1,NUMERAL,if,\ldots}
```
with lexicographic status on the numeric arguments orients every rule. The comparison and Boolean functions terminate by an even simpler recursive-path argument on the same spine. No rule duplicates a redex in a way that could create an infinite chain, and the absence of rewrite rules for `if` merely freezes certain residual terms; it does not enable divergence.

Thus SN follows from the syntax and the rewrite relation alone by a standard termination order, without any additional equations, guards or semantic hypotheses.
