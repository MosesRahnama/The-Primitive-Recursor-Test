1. **Method**: Decreasing measure / structural recursion argument (mentioned RPO or monotone interpretation as examples).

2. **Relies on external assumptions?**: Yes. To apply RPO or a polynomial interpretation one must *choose* a precedence on function symbols and *prove* the rules decrease under that ordering. The rewrite system itself does not fix this precedence; it must be supplied externally.

3. **Outside the boundary?**: Yes. Choosing a precedence or interpretation is an additional proof-theoretic commitment not derivable from the given rules alone. It "strengthens" the argument with extra structure.

4. **Still strongly normalizing?**: Yes. The underlying intuition—that all recursive calls are on smaller numeric arguments—remains valid. The system is a standard definition of arithmetic on binary numerals; it terminates. The boundary issue concerns how we *prove* it, not whether it is true.
