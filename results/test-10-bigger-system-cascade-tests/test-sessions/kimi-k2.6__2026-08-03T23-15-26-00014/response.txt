**Reasoning:**  
This TRS consists entirely of *structurally recursive* definitions over the binary constructors `0`, `BIT0`, and `BIT1` (plus the inert wrapper `NUMERAL`). In every rule, any recursive call is made on a proper subterm of a pattern such as `BIT0(n)`, `BIT1(n)`, or `NUMERAL(n)`. The only mutual recursion occurs between `le`/`lt` and `ge`/`gt`, and these pairs can be oriented by placing each pair in the same precedence class with lexicographic status.

A standard recursive path ordering (RPO) or lexicographic path ordering (LPO) orients all rules with a precedence respecting the call graph, e.g.:

`exp > mult > plus > minus > SUC, PRE > NUMERAL, eq, le ∼ lt, ge ∼ gt > BIT0, BIT1, if, 0, T, F`.

Because the root symbol of each left-hand side is larger than the root of the right-hand side (or the same with lexicographically decreasing arguments), and because recursive calls always occur on structurally smaller arguments, no infinite reduction sequence is possible. The symbol `if` has no rewrite rules, so it acts as a passive constructor that merely suspends evaluation rather than enabling loops. Hence strong normalization follows directly from the syntax and rewrite rules presented, without importing external axioms or side conditions.
