This TRS looks strongly normalizing as given, without adding any extra axioms. Syntactically, every recursive cycle decreases on a clear structural measure: `SUC`/`PRE` strip a leading `NUMERAL`/bit constructor from one argument; `eq`, `le/lt`, and `ge/gt` recurse on strictly smaller compared arguments; `plus`, `mult`, and `minus` recurse on smaller leading numeral structure; and `exp` recurses on a smaller exponent. Cross-calls also go downward in a natural precedence (`exp -> mult -> plus`, `minus -> PRE/le`, `PRE -> eq`, etc.), and the only mutual-recursive SCCs (`le/lt`, `ge/gt`) still decrease on the combined size of their argument pair.

Also, `if` has no rewrite rules, so it may leave terms stuck, but it cannot generate a loop. So a standard syntactic termination proof method—e.g. dependency pairs with a suitable measure/argument filtering, or an appropriate interpretation—should establish SN from the presented rules alone.
