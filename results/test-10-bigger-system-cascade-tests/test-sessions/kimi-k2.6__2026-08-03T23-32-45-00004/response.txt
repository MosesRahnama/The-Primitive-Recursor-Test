**Reasoning**

1. **Structural recursion.** Every rule that calls a defined function on the right-hand side does so on arguments that are proper subterms of the left-hand side’s arguments. For example:
   - `plus(BIT1(m),BIT1(n))` calls `plus(m,n)`.

2. **No unguarded growth.** The only rules that do not immediately remove a constructor (e.g., the `NUMERAL`‑pushing rules or the `BIT0(0) → 0` rule) merely rearrange or eliminate a single symbol.
