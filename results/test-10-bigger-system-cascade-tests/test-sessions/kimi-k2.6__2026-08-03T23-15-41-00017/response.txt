1. **Structural recursion on binary numerals.** In every recursive rule (e.g., `plus`, `mult`, `exp`, `minus`, `PRE`, `SUC`, `eq`, `le`/`lt`, `ge`/`gt`) the recursive calls are on proper subterms of the left-hand side arguments (`m` or `n` inside `BIT0(m)`, `BIT1(n)`, or `NUMERAL(...)`). Because the total number of `NUMERAL` occurrences in any starting term is finite, only finitely many such administrative steps are possible before the operator applies to a bare `0`/`BIT0`/`BIT1` argument and proceeds with structural recursion.

Equivalently, the subterm criterion on dependency pairs suffices to prove termination. No hidden assumptions, side conditions, or additional rewrite rules are needed.
