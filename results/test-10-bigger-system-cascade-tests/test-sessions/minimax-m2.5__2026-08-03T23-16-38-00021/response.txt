The rules do **not** guarantee strong normalisation.  

A simple infinite reduction can be built using only the SUC‑rules that are present:

```
SUC(0) → BIT1(0)
SUC(BIT1(0)) → BIT0(SUC(0))
SUC(BIT0(SUC(0))) → BIT1(SUC(0))
SUC(BIT1(SUC(0))) → BIT0(SUC(SUC(0)))
…
```

Each step rewrites a term of the form `SUC(t)` to another term that again begins with `SUC`, and the argument to `SUC` grows without bound. No rule ever eliminates the leading `SUC` or reaches a normal form, so the rewrite sequence is infinite.  

Since an infinite rewrite chain exists, the term‑rewriting system is not terminating (hence not strongly normalising). This conclusion follows solely from the given rewrite rules, without any additional axioms or hidden assumptions.
