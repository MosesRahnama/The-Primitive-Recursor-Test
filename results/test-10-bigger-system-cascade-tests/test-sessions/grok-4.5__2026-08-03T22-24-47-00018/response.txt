All recursive calls strictly decrease a well-founded measure extractable from the syntax:

Constructors (`0`, `BIT0`, `BIT1`, `T`, `F`, `NUMERAL`) and the undefined symbol `if` are least in the precedence, so they never create new redexes at the root. Consequently every rewrite sequence is finite; no extra rules, guards or semantic side-conditions are required.
