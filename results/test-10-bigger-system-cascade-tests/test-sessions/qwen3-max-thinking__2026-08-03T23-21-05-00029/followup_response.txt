The boundary explicitly prohibits such imports: "without importing structural axioms [...] beyond what is derivable." 4. **Strong normalization status**: **Yes**. The system *is* strongly normalizing. Critical observation: `if` has *no reduction rules* (confirmed in RULES), making it a constructor. Thus:
