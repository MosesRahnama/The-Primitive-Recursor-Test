No external axioms or assumptions are needed.

Note that `le` and `lt` are equivalent, as are `ge` and `gt`, and I'd assign left-to-right lexicographic status to all binary symbols.

Let's quickly go over why this works. The core idea is that, by construction, under this ordering, every rule in the TRS will strictly decrease the term's "size" according to the LPO.

Since the precedence is a well-founded quasi-ordering and the rules strictly decrease in every case, this LPO guarantees that this term rewriting system is strongly normalizing, i.e., terminating.
