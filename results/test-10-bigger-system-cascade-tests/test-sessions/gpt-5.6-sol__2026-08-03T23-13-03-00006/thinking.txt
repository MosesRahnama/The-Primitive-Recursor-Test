**Exploring normalization in multiset theory**

I'm diving into the idea of strong normalization, which seems to allow for finite branching duplication if it's depth-founded. I'm curious about whether multiplication can duplicate expressions in a way that maintains the same smaller n. A well-founded multiset should handle this, but it raises questions about arbitrary arguments that aren't digit-headed, making the root stuck. I wonder if there could be nested terms and if duplication might affect an active ancestor-like term through a variable.
