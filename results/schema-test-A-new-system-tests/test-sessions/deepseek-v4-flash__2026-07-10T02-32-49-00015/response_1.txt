Yes, strong normalization can be established from the given rewrite system alone.

The system is terminating because each reduction step strictly decreases a simple well-founded measure: the lexicographic pair (number of S symbols, number of Z symbols). The second rule reduces the S count by one (since it removes an S and adds no new S), while the first rule reduces the Z count by one (the Z in the third argument is removed) and leaves the S count unchanged. Since both counts are finite natural numbers, every reduction sequence must eventually stop. No additional axioms or external assumptions are needed.
