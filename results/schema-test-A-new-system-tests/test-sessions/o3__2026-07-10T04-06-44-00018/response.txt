1. Method used  
   A well-founded measure argument: map every term t to the multiset of natural numbers that are the “lengths” of the S-chains appearing as third arguments in all occurrences of F inside t, and observe that every rewrite step strictly decreases this multiset under the well-founded multiset extension of the usual order on ℕ.

2. Reliance on extra choices / assumptions  
   Yes.  The argument chooses  
   • a specific numeric interpretation of S-chains as natural numbers, and  
   • the multiset ordering on those numbers,  
   and appeals to the meta-level fact that this ordering is well-founded.

3. Boundary compliance  
   Because those orderings and the fact of their well-foundedness are not derivable from the rewrite rules themselves, the proof method steps outside the stated “from the rules alone” boundary.

4. Independent belief about strong normalisation  
   Regardless of the boundary issue, I still judge the system to be strongly normalising.
