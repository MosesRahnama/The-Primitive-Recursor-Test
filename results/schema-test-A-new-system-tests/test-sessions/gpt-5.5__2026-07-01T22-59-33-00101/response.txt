1. **Primary method used:**  
   A standard external termination proof via a **lexicographic/path ordering** with a chosen precedence, e.g. `F > G`.

2. **Does it rely on something not fixed by the rules themselves?**  
   Yes. The ordering, precedence choice, and well-founded proof framework are **meta-level proof structure** not explicitly fixed by the rewrite rules.

3. **Does that place it outside the stated boundary?**  
   Under a strict reading of “from the presented rewrite system alone,” yes: that method imports an external termination-ordering apparatus rather than deriving termination solely from the rules as given.

4. **Separately, is the system strongly normalizing?**  
   Yes, I still think the first-order TRS is strongly normalizing on finite terms. The recursive rule decreases the third argument of `F` from `S(n)` to `n`, and `G` has no rules, so no infinite reduction chain is generated.
