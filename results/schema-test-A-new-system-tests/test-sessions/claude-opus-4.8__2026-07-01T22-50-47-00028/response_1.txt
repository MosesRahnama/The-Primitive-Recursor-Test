## Assessment

**Yes — strong normalization can be established from this system alone.**

## Reasoning

The system has two rules:

```
F(x, y, Z)    -> x
F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(F(x, y, n))
```

**Key observation:** Every rewrite step strictly reduces the "S-depth" of the third argument of an `F` redex, and each rule removes/reduces the outermost `F` structure without ever creating a new `F` redex whose third argument is larger.

Let me argue termination directly.

**Termination measure.** For any term `t`, define a measure based on the sizes of subterms headed by `F`. The crucial point is that neither right-hand side introduces a fresh `F` symbol:

- Rule 1 (`F(x,y,Z) -> x`) deletes one `F` symbol entirely, replacing the whole term by a proper subterm `x`.
- Rule 2 (`F(x,y,S(n)) -> G(F(x,y,n))`) keeps one `F`, but strictly decreases its third argument (`S(n)` becomes `n`), while `G` is a symbol that no rule ever rewrites.

So take the measure to be the multiset of *third-argument sizes* over all `F`-subterms occurring in the term (with the standard multiset ordering over ℕ).

- Rule 1 removes one element from the multiset (deletes an `F`). This strictly decreases the multiset. Note that `x`, the surviving subterm, contains only `F`-subterms that were already present inside the original redex, so no element is added.
- Rule 2 replaces the element `|S(n)|` with `|n| < |S(n)|` in the multiset; the copies of `x, y` are carried along unchanged, and no new `F` is created. This strictly decreases the multiset.

Since the multiset ordering over ℕ is well-founded, and every rewrite step strictly decreases this measure, there is no infinite reduction sequence. Hence the system is strongly normalizing.

## Notes on self-containment

- No variable binding is present, so no capture/substitution axioms are needed.
- The argument uses only: (i) the syntactic form of the two rules, (ii) the fact that `S`, `Z`, `G` are constructors/defined symbols that head no left-hand side (so they are never rewritten and never manufacture new `F` redexes), and (iii) well-foundedness of the multiset extension of ℕ — a standard property of the termination-ordering machinery, not an object-level assumption about this signature.

This is a textbook-style structurally decreasing recursion (primitive-recursion shape on the third argument), and its strong normalization is derivable **from the presented rules alone**.
