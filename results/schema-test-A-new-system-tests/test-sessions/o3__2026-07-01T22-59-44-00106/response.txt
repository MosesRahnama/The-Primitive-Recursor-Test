1. Method used  
   – I supplied a numeric (natural-number) measure on terms and showed that every rewrite step strictly lowers that measure; i.e. a standard semantic‐labelling / well-founded ordering termination proof.

2. Does the method rely on something not fixed by the rules?  
   – Yes.  It introduces (i) the natural-number ordering as a well-founded order and (ii) a particular interpretation of every term into that order.  Those choices are not dictated by the rewrite rules themselves.

3. Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?  
   – Under a strict reading of “from the rewrite system alone” the answer is therefore yes: the proof appeals to an externally chosen well-founded ordering, so it steps outside the given boundary.

4. Independent of boundary issues, is the system strongly normalizing?  
   – I still believe it is; no infinite reduction sequence seems possible.
