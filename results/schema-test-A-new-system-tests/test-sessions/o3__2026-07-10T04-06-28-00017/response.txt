1. Primary method used  
   A well-founded measure argument: assign to every term the multiset of “depths” of the third arguments of all F-occurrences, and appeal to the multiset extension of the > order on ℕ to show that every rewrite step strictly decreases that measure.

2. Does the method rely on extra choices or assumptions?  
   Yes.  It presupposes (i) the natural numbers as an ordered set that is well-founded under >, and (ii) the multiset extension of that order, neither of which is specified or derivable from the two rewrite rules themselves.

3. Does that put the method outside the stated boundary?  
   Under a strict reading of “no structural axioms or object-level assumptions beyond what is derivable from the given rules,” the use of an external well-founded ordering and its multiset extension is indeed outside the allowed boundary.

4. Independent of the boundary issue, do I still believe the system is strongly normalizing?  
   Yes; the depth of the S-chain in the third argument of any F strictly decreases along every rewrite path, so infinite reductions are impossible.
