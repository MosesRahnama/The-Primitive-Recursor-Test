A lex measure (#F symbols, #S symbols) on any term strictly decreases on every rewrite (verifiable directly from the two rules alone): the S-rule preserves #F while removing exactly one S; the Z-rule drops #F by ≥1 (discarding the F, the Z, and all symbols in y) while never increasing #S. The measure lies in ℕ×ℕ under lexicographic order, which is well-founded, so every reduction sequence is finite. No structural axioms, freeness assumptions, or other object-level properties are required; symbol counting follows immediately from the given rules and first-order term structure.
