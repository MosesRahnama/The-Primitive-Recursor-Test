Yes. Strong normalization can be established from the rewrite rules alone; no structural axioms or object-level assumptions are needed.

For example, use a lexicographic path ordering with precedence \(F > G\), and give \(F\) a lexicographic status prioritizing its third argument. Then:

- \(F(x,y,Z) \to x\) decreases by the subterm property.
- For \(F(x,y,S(n)) \to G(F(x,y,n))\), the third argument decreases from \(S(n)\) to \(n\), while the outer \(F\)-term is also ordered above the \(G\)-term because \(F > G\).

Thus every rewrite step strictly decreases a well-founded ordering, including for terms containing variables. The system is therefore strongly normalizing.
