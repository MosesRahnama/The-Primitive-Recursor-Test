1. **Primary method:** A recursive path ordering (RPO) argument.

2. **Dependence on choices:** Yes. It requires choosing, for example, the precedence \(F > G\) and a lexicographic status for \(F\) that compares the third argument.

3. **Boundary issue:** No, not under the stated boundary. Those are meta-level choices used to construct a termination proof, not imported object-level axioms or assumptions about \(Z\) and \(S\). If “from the rules alone” were read as forbidding any externally chosen proof ordering, then the method would be excluded, but that is not the usual meaning.

4. **Strong normalization:** Yes. With those choices, both rules strictly decrease in a well-founded RPO, so the rewrite system is strongly normalizing.
