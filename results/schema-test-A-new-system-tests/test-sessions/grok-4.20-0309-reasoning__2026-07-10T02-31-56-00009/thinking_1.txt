F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(F(x, y, n))

Assuming x,y are variables, Z constant (zero), S unary function, G unary function.

No rules for G, 0, S themselves.

In first-order TRS, reductions are by substituting the patterns.

Possible infinite reductions?
