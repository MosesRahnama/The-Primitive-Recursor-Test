Yes. Strong normalization follows from the rewrite rules alone, treating the symbols purely syntactically.

For example, use a polynomial interpretation over \(\mathbb N\):

\[
Z=0,\qquad S(n)=n+1,\qquad G(a)=a,\qquad F(x,y,n)=x+y+2n+1.
\]

Then both rules strictly decrease:

- \(F(x,y,Z)=x+y+1>x\).
- \(F(x,y,S(n))=x+y+2n+3>x+y+2n+1=G(F(x,y,n))\).

All interpretations are monotone, so reductions inside arbitrary contexts also decrease. Thus no infinite rewrite sequence exists; no additional structural axioms about \(Z\), \(S\), or the other symbols are needed.
