1. **Method used:** Polynomial interpretation (specifically, a linear symbol-weight function).

2. **Reliance on external choices:** Yes. It relies on choosing a specific meta-level interpretation (mapping symbols to $\mathbb{N}$) and a well-founded strict ordering ($>$). These are not fixed by the rules themselves; they are chosen by the prover.

3. **Boundary compliance:** No, it does not place the method outside the boundary. The boundary prohibits importing *object-level assumptions* (e.g., adding equations assumed to hold between terms, or assuming the terms represent Peano naturals) and *structural axioms* (e.g., adding associativity or commutativity to the system). A polynomial interpretation is a *meta-level* proof technique used to establish a well-founded ordering. Using standard mathematical logic and well-founded orderings in the meta-theory is strictly necessary for any termination proof and does not alter or add to the object-level TRS. 

4. **Strong normalization status:** Yes, the system is strongly normalizing. Stripping away the meta-level math, it can be seen purely syntactically: every rewrite rule strictly decreases the total count of `F` and `S` symbols in the term, and no rule creates new `F` or `S` symbols. Since this count is a non-negative integer, infinite reduction is impossible.
