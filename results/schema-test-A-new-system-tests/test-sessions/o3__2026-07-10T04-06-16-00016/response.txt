1. Primary method:  
   I used a simple semantic/weight interpretation – a natural-number measure that counts the number of leading S’s in the third argument of every F-subterm – and appealed to the well-founded order on ℕ to show every rewrite step decreases that measure.

2. Dependence on extra structure:  
   Yes. The argument assumes (a) the particular choice of that interpretation and (b) the meta-level fact that the natural numbers are well founded. Neither of these items is dictated by, or encoded in, the two rewrite rules themselves.

3. Boundary status:  
   Because the proof imports an external well-founded ordering and a chosen interpretation, it falls outside the strict “no additional structural axioms or assumptions” boundary as phrased in the question.

4. Independent judgment on SN:  
   Regardless of the boundary issue, I still believe the system is strongly normalizing.
