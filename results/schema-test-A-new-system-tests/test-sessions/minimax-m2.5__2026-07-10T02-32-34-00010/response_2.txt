1. **Method:** A measure/weight argument counting the number of S constructors in the third argument of F.

2. **Relies on external assumptions?** Yes. This method implicitly:
   - Assumes a numerical interpretation where S represents "adding 1"
   - Assumes Z represents "0"
   - Relies on well-foundedness of natural numbers as meta-theory
   - Assumes an ordering on terms based on this interpretation

3. **Outside the boundary?** Yes. The rewrite rules themselves are purely syntactic—they say nothing about S being a "successor" or Z being "zero." The interpretation into natural numbers is an external structural assumption.

4. **Still think it's strongly normalizing?** Intuitively yes—the system "feels" terminating because each rewrite reduces the "S-depth" of the term. But formally proving that from the rules alone, without importing any external ordering, well-foundedness assumptions, or semantic interpretation, would require a different approach (e.g., showing no infinite rewrite chains exist syntactically, which is not trivial to establish purely from the rule syntax).
