1. Primary method used  
   A standard termination/strong-normalisation proof by a well-founded measure: I counted the number of S symbols and, as a secondary component, the number of F-redexes, showing that every rewrite step strictly lowers the resulting lexicographic pair of natural numbers.

2. Does that method rely on anything not fixed by the rules?  
   Yes.  It presupposes (a) the choice of a particular measure (“count of S’s, count of F’s”) and (b) the well-foundedness of the usual > ordering on the natural numbers.  Neither of those facts is part of the rewrite specification itself.

3. Does that place the method outside the stated boundary?  
   Yes.  Because it appeals to an external well-founded ordering and to a hand-picked interpretation of terms, it steps beyond “what is derivable from the given rules”.

4. Separate from the boundary question, do I still believe the system is strongly normalising?  
   Yes.  Even though the above proof may be inadmissible under the stated restriction, the system itself still appears to terminate (and hence be strongly normalising).
