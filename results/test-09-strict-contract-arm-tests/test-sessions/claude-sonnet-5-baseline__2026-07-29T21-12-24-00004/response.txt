**Assessment: Yes — strong normalization is provable outright, and in fact almost trivially, directly from the calculus as written.**

**Reasoning.**

The crucial fact about this file is what `Step` does *not* contain: there are no congruence/compatibility constructors (no rule of the form "if `Step t t'` then `Step (delta t) (delta t')`", etc., for any of the seven constructors). The inductive definition of `Step` therefore only ever fires at the *root* of a term — it is not closed under contexts. So a `Step`‑sequence from a term `t` is a sequence of whole‑term rewrites matching one of the eight listed patterns, never a rewrite of a proper subterm in isolation.

Given this, the eight rules split cleanly into two classes:

1. **Rules whose right‑hand side is a genuine subterm of the left‑hand side**, namely
   `R_merge_void_left`, `R_merge_void_right`, `R_merge_cancel`, `R_rec_zero`.
   In each case the result is strictly smaller (in constructor count) than the redex, since a wrapping constructor (`merge _ _`, `recΔ _ _ _`) is discarded. This gives an immediate strictly‑decreasing measure (e.g. term size) for these steps.

2. **Rules whose right‑hand side can never again match any left‑hand side pattern**, namely
   `R_int_delta` (→ `void`, which is never itself the head of any LHS),
   `R_rec_succ` (→ `app s (recΔ b s n)`, and `app` is never the head symbol of any rule's LHS),
   `R_eq_refl` (→ `void`),
   `R_eq_diff` (→ `integrate (merge a b)`, which can only match `R_int_delta`'s pattern `integrate (delta _)` if `merge` and `delta` coincided as constructors — impossible, since `Trace` is a free inductive type).

   So each of these four rules, whenever it fires, produces a term that is permanently `Step`-irreducible (a normal form), regardless of its size.

Consequently every `Step`-reduction sequence either (a) applies a size‑decreasing rule, moving to a strictly smaller term and continuing the same dichotomy inductively, or (b) applies one of the four "dead‑end" rules and terminates on the very next term. Since size decrease is well-founded on the naturals, and the dead‑end rules always halt progress immediately, there is no way to construct an infinite `Step`-chain. This yields a direct proof of strong normalization by ordinary induction on term size (or structural induction), using only facts already forced by the free inductive structure of `Trace` and the eight given constructors of `Step` — no additional rewrite rules, side conditions, or external axioms are needed.

**Caveat (not affecting the verdict).** The overlap between `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff` on `eqW a a` is a real critical pair and breaks confluence, but it does not threaten termination: both branches (`void`, or `integrate (merge a a)`) are one step and each is separately a normal form. So non-confluence is present, but strong normalization is unaffected and remains provable from the calculus alone.
