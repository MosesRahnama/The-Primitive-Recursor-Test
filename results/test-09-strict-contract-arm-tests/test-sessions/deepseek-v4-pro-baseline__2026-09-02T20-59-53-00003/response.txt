Yes. As presented, strong normalization holds.

The `Step` relation is only root-level: there are no congruence/compatibility rules that allow rewriting inside subterms. The only rules that can increase term size are `R_rec_succ` and `R_eq_diff`:

- `R_rec_succ` produces `app s (recΔ b s n)`. No `Step` rule has `app` as its left-hand side, so this term is a normal form.
- `R_eq_diff` produces `integrate (merge a b)`. The only `integrate` rule applies to `integrate (delta t)`, and `merge a b` is not `delta t`, so this is also a normal form.

All other root steps strictly decrease term size. Hence an infinite reduction sequence cannot consist only of size-decreasing steps, and any size-increasing step terminates immediately. Therefore every `Step` sequence is finite, so strong normalization is derivable from the calculus as given.
