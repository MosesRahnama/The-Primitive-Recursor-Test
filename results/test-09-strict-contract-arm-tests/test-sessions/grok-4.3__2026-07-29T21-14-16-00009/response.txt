The rule R_rec_succ duplicates subterm \(s\) (and likewise \(b\)).

For any additive measure \(M\) (e.g., constructor count / term size), the step gives

A possible fix would require a primary measure (e.g., multiset of rec-argument sizes, lex-ordered with size) that is unaffected by the duplicated \(s\). The indispensable premise for that fix is that \(s\) contains no rec subterms. This premise is not derivable from the given syntax or rewrite rules (any Trace may appear as the second argument to recΔ). Hence the premise cannot be verified.
