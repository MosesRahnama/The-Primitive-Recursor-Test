**Yes.**

The rewrite rules induce an abstract reduction system on the inductively defined set of ground Trace terms. Each rule is either strictly size-decreasing (merge-void, merge-cancel, rec-zero, int-delta, eq-refl) or performs a single finite unrolling (rec-succ steps exactly once per nested delta; eq-diff produces a single integrate/merge pair whose sub-reductions are again size-decreasing). No rule emits a fresh eqW, recΔ, or delta constructor outside existing subterms, and the only constructors that grow the term (app, integrate) are inert or immediately reducible only when they match a size-decreasing rule.  

Consequently a structurally recursive ranking function on Trace (lexicographic combination of eqW-count, recΔ-unfolding measure given by delta-chain depth, merge/integrate potential, and total size) is definable by induction on the syntax alone and is shown to decrease on every Step by case analysis on the eight rules. This establishes that the relation is well-founded, hence strongly normalizing, with no additional axioms or guards required.
