**Assessment:** Yes, strong normalization can be established from the presented calculus alone.

**Reasoning:** The system admits a well-founded measure that strictly decreases with every rewrite step. Define the measure as the lexicographic triple:

1. **The multiset of delta-depths** of the third arguments to all `recΔ` constructors (ordered by multiset extension of the usual ordering on naturals).  
2. **The number of `eqW` constructors** in the term.  
3. **The syntactic size** of the term.

This measure decreases as follows:
- `R_rec_succ` replaces `recΔ b s (delta n)` with `app s (recΔ b s n)`, removing one delta-depth $d+1$ and adding one delta-depth $d$, thereby strictly decreasing component (1).
- `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff` each remove one `eqW` constructor, strictly decreasing component (2) while preserving component (1) (they only shuffle existing subterms).
- All other rules (`R_int_delta`, `R_merge_*`, `R_rec_zero`) preserve components (1) and (2) while strictly decreasing the term size (component 3).

Since no rule creates new `eqW` constructors (they appear only on the left-hand sides) and `R_rec_succ` is the only rule affecting `recΔ` (which it strictly decreases in component 1), every reduction chain must terminate. Thus the calculus is strongly normalizing.
