**Yes.**

Strong normalization follows directly by case analysis on the 8 base rules (assuming the standard contextual closure of Step). Assign to each Trace the measure \(\mu(t) = (\#_{\text{eqW}}(t), \#_{\text{delta}}(t), |t|)\), where the components count eqW constructors, delta constructors, and total nodes in the term tree, respectively. Order triples lexicographically over \(\mathbb{N}\), which is well-founded.

- eqW reductions (R_eq_refl, R_eq_diff) strictly decrease the first component.
- delta-removing reductions (R_int_delta, R_rec_succ) strictly decrease the second component.
- All remaining reductions (R_merge_*, R_rec_zero) keep the first two components unchanged but strictly decrease the third (node count).

Hence every one-step reduction produces a strictly smaller triple, so no infinite reduction sequence exists. The argument uses only the given inductive syntax and the listed rules; no additional rewrite rules, guards, or structural axioms are required.
