**Assessment: Yes, strong normalization holds.**

**Reasoning:**

This first-order rewrite system is strongly normalizing. Termination can be established using a lexicographic measure on terms:

$$M(t) = \bigl(N_{\mathsf{eqW}}(t),\; \sum_{\mathsf{rec\Delta}\,b\,s\,n \in \text{sub}(t)} |n|,\; |t|\bigr)$$

where:
1. $N_{\mathsf{eqW}}(t)$ counts $\mathsf{eqW}$ symbols (first component),
2. The sum ranges over all $\mathsf{rec\Delta}$ subterms with their third argument's size (second component),
3. $|t|$ is the total syntactic size (third component).

**Why each rule decreases this measure:**

- **$\mathsf{R\_eq\_refl}$ and $\mathsf{R\_eq\_diff}$**: Both eliminate one $\mathsf{eqW}$ constructor, decreasing the first component regardless of the other changes (the second rule increases size by 1, but the lexicographic priority of $N_{\mathsf{eqW}}$ dominates).
  
- **$\mathsf{R\_rec\_zero}$**: Eliminates a $\mathsf{rec\Delta}$, decreasing the second component (the sum loses a positive term).

- **$\mathsf{R\_rec\_succ}$**: Replaces $\mathsf{rec\Delta}\,b\,s\,(\delta n)$ with $\mathsf{app}\,s\,(\mathsf{rec\Delta}\,b\,s\,n)$. The $\mathsf{rec\Delta}$ persists, but its third argument decreases from $\delta n$ (size $1+|n|$) to $n$ (size $|n|$), so the second component decreases by 1. The number of $\mathsf{eqW}$ symbols is unchanged (both sides contain the same subterms from $s$ and $n$).

- **$\mathsf{R\_int\_delta}$ and the three $\mathsf{R\_merge}$ rules**: These decrease the third component (total size) and do not increase the first two components.

Since no rule creates $\mathsf{eqW}$ or $\mathsf{rec\Delta}$ constructors (they only eliminate them or move them to structurally smaller arguments in the case of $\mathsf{rec\Delta}$), and the measure is well-founded (lexicographic product of natural numbers), all reduction sequences must terminate.

No additional axioms or structural assumptions are required beyond the syntax and rules presented.
