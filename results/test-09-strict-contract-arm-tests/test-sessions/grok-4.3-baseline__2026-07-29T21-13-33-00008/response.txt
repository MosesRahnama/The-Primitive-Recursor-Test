The system is strongly normalizing. Proof by induction on term size (number of constructors), with a case analysis on the top constructor of \(t\):

- If \(t\) is a normal form (top constructor is `void`, `delta`, `app`, `integrate` whose argument is not `delta`, `merge` whose arguments are unequal and non-`void`, or `recΔ` whose third argument is neither `void` nor `delta`, or `eqW` paired with the appropriate rule choice), then the reduction length is 0 (finite).

In every case the possible reduction sequences from \(t\) are bounded in length, so there are no infinite chains. The argument uses only the inductive definition of `Trace` and the eight `Step` rules shown; no congruence rules, extra axioms, or external lemmas are required.
