## Assessment: Yes — strong normalization is provable directly from the given system.

**Key structural fact.** `Step` is defined *only* by eight root-level axioms; there is no congruence/context‑closure rule (no clause of the form "if `Step a b` then `Step (delta a) (delta b)`", etc.). Consequently a `Step`-derivation can only fire at the very top of a term — reduction never happens inside a subterm unless that subterm *is* the whole term being rewritten. This drastically restricts what reduction sequences can look like, and it is the fact that makes an elementary termination proof possible without any additional machinery.

**Size measure.** Take the ordinary structural size `size : Trace → ℕ` (number of constructors). Checking each rule:

- `R_int_delta`, `R_merge_void_left/right`, `R_merge_cancel`, `R_rec_zero`, `R_eq_refl`: the right-hand side is a proper subterm of the left-hand side (or `void`), so `size` strictly **decreases**.
- `R_rec_succ` (`recΔ b s (delta n) → app s (recΔ b s n)`) and `R_eq_diff` (`eqW a b → integrate (merge a b)`): `size` can **increase**.

**The increasing rules are dead ends.** Since `Step` has no rule whose left‑hand side is headed by `app`, the term `app s (recΔ b s n)` produced by `R_rec_succ` can *never* be the source of another `Step` — it is already a normal form. Likewise, the only rule with `integrate` at the head requires the argument to be syntactically `delta _`; `merge a b` is never of that shape (distinct constructors), so `integrate (merge a b)` produced by `R_eq_diff` is also permanently stuck.

**Termination argument.** Prove `∀ t, SN t` (accessibility under `Step`) by strong induction on `size t`. For any one-step successor `t'` of `t`:
- if the step used is one of the "decreasing" rules, `size t' < size t`, so `SN t'` follows from the induction hypothesis;
- if the step used is `R_rec_succ` or `R_eq_diff`, `t'` has no successors at all, so `SN t'` holds vacuously.

Hence every successor of `t` is `SN`, so `t` is `SN`. This closes the induction and establishes strong normalization for *all* terms.

This argument uses nothing beyond: the inductive definition of `Trace` (giving finite size and standard structural/well-founded induction on ℕ), and a finite case analysis on the eight given rules. It requires no extra rewrite rules, no side conditions, no congruence rule, and no externally imported well-foundedness axiom — everything needed (finiteness of terms, the size function, ℕ-induction) is already implicit in the inductive type and available in Lean's core library.

**Caveat (not relevant to normalization, but worth noting).** The overlap between `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff` on `eqW a a` (no side condition `a ≠ b` is imposed) creates branching/non-confluence, but this does not threaten *strong* normalization — SN only requires every path to be finite, and both possible reducts of `eqW a a` are shown finite/terminating by the same argument.

**Conclusion.** Strong normalization of this exact system is establishable purely internally, by a straightforward size-decrease-plus-dead-end case analysis proved by well-founded induction on term size — no extra axioms, guards, or side conditions need to be introduced.
