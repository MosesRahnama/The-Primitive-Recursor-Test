**Assessment: YES, strong normalization can be established from the presented calculus alone.**

The system is strongly normalizing via a lexicographic measure combining three components: (1) the count of `eqW` constructors, (2) a multiset of the sizes of third arguments to all `recΔ` subterms, and (3) the total term size.

---

**Step 1: Additive Failure for the Duplicating Rule**

Rule `R_rec_succ` duplicates subterm `s`:
```
recΔ b s (delta n)  →  app s (recΔ b s n)
```

Using constructor count M(·):
- M(before) = 2 + M(b) + M(s) + M(n)
- M(after) = 2 + M(b) + 2·M(s) + M(n)
- **M(after) = M(before) + M(s)**

Expressed in contract form: M(after) = M(before) − 1 + (M(s)+1). Since M(s) ≥ 1 (minimum term size is 1 for `void`), we have **no strict drop**: the step increases total mass whenever M(s) ≥ 1.

---

**Step 2: The Fix and Key Premise**

**Chosen fix:** Use a lexicographic measure **(E(t), Φ(t), M(t))** where:
- **E(t)** = number of `eqW` subterms in t
- **Φ(t)** = multiset { |n| : recΔ b s n occurs in t } where |·| is constructor-count
- **M(t)** = total constructor count of t

**Key Premise:** The Dershowitz-Manna multiset order on ℕ is well-founded, and replacing an element k+1 with k (where k ≥ 0) is a strict decrease in this order.

**Verification of the premise:**
For `R_rec_succ`, the multiset changes from Φ ⊎ {1+|n|} to Φ ⊎ {|n|}. Since |n| < 1+|n|, the multiset strictly decreases regardless of the context or the duplication of `s`. The increase of M(s) in the third component is absorbed by the lexicographic ordering: the second component drops in a well-founded order, so the pair (Φ, M) decreases.

---

**Step 3: Checking All Rules**

1. **E-component (strictly decreases or preserved):**
   - `R_eq_refl` and `R_eq_diff` remove one `eqW` (E decreases by 1).
   - No right-hand side introduces new `eqW` subterms; E never increases.

2. **Φ-component (when E=0, strictly decreases for recursor steps):**
   - `R_rec_zero`: removes `{1}` from Φ.
   - `R_rec_succ`: replaces `1+|n|` with `|n|`; multiset strictly decreases.
   - `R_int_delta`: may delete subterms, so Φ decreases or stays same.
   - Merge/eqW rules: preserve recΔ subterms and their arguments; Φ unchanged.

3. **M-component (when E=0 and Φ=∅):**
   - No `recΔ` or `eqW` remain.
   - `R_int_delta`, `R_merge_*` all strictly decrease M.
   - `R_eq_diff` requires E>0, so not applicable here.

Since each rule strictly decreases the lexicographic triple and each component is well-founded, the system is strongly normalizable. The duplication of `s` in `R_rec_succ` is compensated by the structural decrease of the recursor's inductive argument in the multiset measure.
