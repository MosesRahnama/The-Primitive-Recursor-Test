**Assessment: Yes, strong normalization can be established from the presented calculus alone.**

**Reasoning:**

The system is strongly normalizing because all possible reduction sequences terminate. This can be shown using a standard ordinal-valued measure (or equivalently, a well-founded multiset ordering) that decreases with every reduction step:

1.  **Primitive Recursion (`recΔ`):** The rules for `recΔ` implement primitive recursion over the "height" of the third argument (which represents a natural number built from `void` (zero) and `delta` (successor)). Assign `recΔ b s n` the weight $\omega^{|n|} + |b| + |s|$ where $|n|$ is the number of `delta` constructors. The step `recΔ b s (delta n) → app s (recΔ b s n)` reduces the height of the third argument from $|n|+1$ to $|n|$, decreasing the ordinal $\omega^{|n|+1} = \omega^{|n|} \cdot \omega$ to roughly $\omega^{|n|} + \text{finite}$, which is a strict decrease. The base case `recΔ b s void → b` also decreases the weight (from $\omega + |b| + |s|$ to $|b|$).

2.  **Merge and Integrate:** The rules for `merge` are strictly decreasing with respect to the sum of the sizes of subterms (e.g., `merge t t → t` replaces two copies of $t$ with one). The rule `integrate (delta t) → void` strictly decreases size. These subsystems are terminating.

3.  **Equality Witness (`eqW`):** The rule `R_eq_diff` replaces `eqW a b` with `integrate (merge a b)`. While this preserves or slightly increases immediate syntactic size, it eliminates the `eqW` constructor entirely. Since `merge` is terminating and never introduces new `eqW` or `recΔ` subterms, and `integrate` only rewrites to `void` (terminating), the expansion created by `R_eq_diff` cannot lead to an infinite loop. The `eqW` constructor never reappears once eliminated.

Since every reduction either: (a) decreases the ordinal measure associated with `recΔ`, (b) decreases the finite size measure for `merge`/`integrate`, or (c) eliminates an `eqW` (followed by terminating reductions), there can be no infinite reduction sequences. Thus, the system is strongly normalizing without requiring additional axioms or side conditions.
