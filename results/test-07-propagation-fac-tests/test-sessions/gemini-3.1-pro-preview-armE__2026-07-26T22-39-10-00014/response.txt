2. **Rule 2: `F(x, y, S(n)) -> G(y, F(x, y, n))`**

The direct subterms of the RHS are `y` and `F(x, y, n)`.

Since `LHS > RHS` holds for all rules under LPO, the system is terminating.

### Method 2: Polynomial Interpretation

* $[S](n) = n + 1$

Now we check that every rule results in a strict mathematical decrease from LHS to RHS for all variables $\ge 1$:

1. **Rule 1: `F(x, y, Z) -> x`**
