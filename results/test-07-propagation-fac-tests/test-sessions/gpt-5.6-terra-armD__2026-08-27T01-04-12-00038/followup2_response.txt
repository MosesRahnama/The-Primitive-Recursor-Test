\[

=2\llbracket u\rrbracket\llbracket v\rrbracket

> If all function-symbol interpretations are monotone, every rewrite rule strictly decreases under a well-founded order, and rewriting is closed under contexts and substitutions in the usual first-order sense, then the rewrite system terminates.

Here the well-founded order is ordinary strict \(>\) on \(\mathbb N\). This is not an imported object-level axiom or a strengthening of the TRS; it is the external mathematical basis of the proof method.
