### Verdict
**Yes**, termination of the given TRS is established from the rules alone (via the lexicographic path order, a standard simplification order).

### Termination argument
Let \(\mathcal{F} = \{0, s, p, \mathsf{plus}, \mathsf{times}\}\) be the signature. Equip \(\mathcal{F}\) with the total precedence
\[
\mathsf{times} \succ \mathsf{plus} \succ p \succ s \succ 0.
\]
Let \(>_{\mathsf{lpo}}\) be the induced lexicographic path order on terms (status: all symbols lexicographic, left-to-right). It is enough to verify that each rule \(\ell\to r\) satisfies \(\ell >_{\mathsf{lpo}} r\); the well-foundedness of \(>_{\mathsf{lpo}}\) then yields termination.

- \(\mathsf{plus}(x,0) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} x\)  
  Immediate (right-hand side is a proper subterm of the left-hand side).

- \(\mathsf{plus}(x,s(y)) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} s(\mathsf{plus}(x,y))\)  
  Root comparison \(\mathsf{plus}\succ s\) reduces the claim to \(\mathsf{plus}(x,s(y)) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} \mathsf{plus}(x,y)\).  
  The latter holds by the lexicographic status: the first arguments are identical and the second arguments satisfy \(s(y)>_{\mathsf{lpo}} y\).

- \(\mathsf{times}(0,y) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} 0\)  
  Root comparison \(\mathsf{times}\succ 0\) together with the fact that the right-hand side is a proper subterm.

- \(\mathsf{times}(x,0) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} 0\)  
  Identical reasoning.

- \(\mathsf{times}(s(x),y) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} \mathsf{plus}(\mathsf{times}(x,y),y)\)  
  Root comparison \(\mathsf{times}\succ\mathsf{plus}\) reduces the claim to the two strict inequalities  
  \(\mathsf{times}(s(x),y) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} \mathsf{times}(x,y)\) and \(\mathsf{times}(s(x),y) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} y\).  
  The second is immediate (proper subterm). The first holds by the lexicographic status: \(s(x)>_{\mathsf{lpo}} x\) (proper subterm) while the second arguments are identical.

- \(p(s(s(x))) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} s(p(s(x)))\)  
  Root comparison \(p\succ s\) reduces the claim to \(p(s(s(x))) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} p(s(x))\).  
  The latter holds by the lexicographic status on the arguments: \(s(s(x)) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} s(x)\) (again by lexicographic status, using \(s(x)>_{\mathsf{lpo}} x\)).

- \(p(s(0)) >_{\mathsf{lpo}} 0\)  
  Root comparison \(p\succ 0\) together with the fact that the right-hand side is a proper subterm.

Every rule is therefore strictly decreasing in \(>_{\mathsf{lpo}}\). Hence the TRS is terminating.

(Note that the argument uses only the syntax of the signature and the seven rewrite rules; no additional rewrite rules, equational theories, guards, or semantic interpretations of the function symbols are required.)
