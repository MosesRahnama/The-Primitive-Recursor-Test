**Termination Argument:**
We can prove the system terminates on all terms by mapping the terms to a well-founded set, using a **Polynomial Interpretation**. We choose the domain to be the natural numbers strictly greater than zero ($\mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$). We will map each symbol to a polynomial such that all operations are strictly monotonic and closed over $\mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$, and then show that for every rule, the interpretation of the left-hand side (LHS) is strictly greater than the interpretation of the right-hand side (RHS).

Let the interpretation of each term be defined as follows:
- $[0] = 1$
- $[s](x) = x + 1$
- $[p](x) = 2x$
- $[plus](x, y) = x + 2y$
- $[times](x, y) = x(2y + 1) + 2$

*Closure and Strict Monotonicity:*
For any $x, y \ge 1$, all functions yield values $\ge 1$. 
- $[0] = 1 \ge 1$
- $[s](x) = x + 1 \ge 2 \ge 1$
- $[p](x) = 2x \ge 2 \ge 1$
- $[plus](x, y) = x + 2y \ge 3 \ge 1$
- $[times](x, y) = x(2y + 1) + 2 \ge 1(3) + 2 = 5 \ge 1$

Furthermore, the polynomials are strictly monotonic with respect to all of their variables because the coefficients of variables in every polynomial are positive integers ($\ge 1$).

*Evaluating the Rules:*
We now evaluate the LHS and RHS for all 7 rewrite rules to verify that $LHS > RHS$ for all $x, y \ge 1$:

1. **`plus(x, 0) -> x`**
   - LHS: $[plus](x, 1) = x + 2(1) = x + 2$
   - RHS: $x$
   - $x + 2 > x$ (Strict decrease by 2)

6. **`p(s(s(x))) -> s(p(s(x)))`**

7. **`p(s(0)) -> 0`**
