I will use a lexicographic path order (LPO) with a suitable precedence. Define the precedence ≻ on function symbols by  
times ≻ plus ≻ s ≻ 0.  

The LPO is defined as usual: a term t is 0-irreducible if it contains no variable; otherwise t ≻ u holds if either  
- t = f(t₁,…,tₙ) and u = g(u₁,…,uₘ) with f ≻ g, or  
- t = f(t₁,…,tₙ) and 0 ≻ u (i.e., u is a proper subterm of some tᵢ), or  
- t = f(t₁,…,tₙ) and u = f(u₁,…,uₙ) with tᵢ ≻ uᵢ for all i (lexicographic status). 0-irreducible terms are compared only by the first clause.
