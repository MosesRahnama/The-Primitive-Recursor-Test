The Dependency Pair (DP) framework combined with DP-narrowing was selected because standard simplification orderings inherently fail on the non-subterm recursive call `fac(p(s(x)))`.

Initial logic and structural parameters of the given rewrite system are validated as incompatible with basic Lexicographic Path Orders (LPO), Recursive Path Orders (RPO), or Knuth-Bendix Orders (KBO). Standard processing applied to such systems demonstrates that holistic term comparison cannot succeed here, as the argument `p(s(x))` is not a strict subterm of `s(x)`. 

Consequently, rather than attempting to brute-force a global ordering, extracting the isolated dependency `FAC(s(x)) -> FAC(p(s(x)))` and directly applying DP-narrowing evaluates the hidden reduction `p(s(x)) -> x`. This immediately forces the strict decrease condition on the cycle, bypassing the structural blockade and proving termination.
