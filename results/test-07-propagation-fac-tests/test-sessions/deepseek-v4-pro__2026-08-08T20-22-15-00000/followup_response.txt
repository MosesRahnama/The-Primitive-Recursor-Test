The dependency‑pair method with a polynomial interpretation was chosen because the system’s recursion is not purely structural. In the rule  
`fac(s(x)) → times(fac(p(s(x))), s(x))`  
the recursive call `fac(p(s(x)))` does not use an immediate subterm of `s(x)`. Hence a direct subterm‑based order (e.g., a naive recursive path order) would fail. The DP framework lets us separate the recursive calls from the rest, and a natural interpretation into ℕ (where `0`, `s`, `p`, `plus`, `times`, `fac` mean their arithmetic counterparts) directly reflects the actual decrease in value of the arguments. This yields simple, strict inequalities for every dependency pair without needing complex precedence choices or non‑simplifying transformations.
