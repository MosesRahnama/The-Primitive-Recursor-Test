## Verdict

**Yes.** Termination follows from the rules alone. A lexicographic path ordering (LPO), based purely on the syntax of terms, strictly orients every rule.

Choose the precedence

\[
\mathsf{times} \succ \mathsf{plus} \succ \mathsf{p} \succ \mathsf{s} \succ 0
\]

and give all symbols lexicographic status. LPO is well-founded on finite first-order terms and is closed under substitutions and contexts.

Write \(>_{\mathrm{LPO}}\) for this ordering.

### Addition rules

1. \[
\mathsf{plus}(x,0) >_{\mathrm{LPO}} x
\]

because \(x\) is a proper subterm of the left-hand side.

2. \[
\mathsf{plus}(x,\mathsf{s}(y))
   >_{\mathrm{LPO}}
   \mathsf{s}(\mathsf{plus}(x,y)).
\]

First,

\[
\mathsf{plus}(x,\mathsf{s}(y))
   >_{\mathrm{LPO}}
   \mathsf{plus}(x,y),
\]

because the roots agree, the first arguments agree, and
\(\mathsf{s}(y)>_{\mathrm{LPO}}y\) by the subterm property. Since
\(\mathsf{plus}\succ\mathsf{s}\), the left-hand side is therefore greater than
\(\mathsf{s}(\mathsf{plus}(x,y))\).

### Multiplication rules

3. \[
\mathsf{times}(0,y)>_{\mathrm{LPO}}0
\]

because \(0\) is a proper subterm.

4. \[
\mathsf{times}(x,0)>_{\mathrm{LPO}}0
\]

for the same reason.

5. Let \(A=\mathsf{times}(\mathsf{s}(x),y)\). Then

\[
A>_{\mathrm{LPO}}\mathsf{times}(x,y),
\]

because the roots agree and \(\mathsf{s}(x)>_{\mathrm{LPO}}x\). Also
\(A>_{\mathrm{LPO}}y\) by the subterm property. Since
\(\mathsf{times}\succ\mathsf{plus}\),

\[
\mathsf{times}(\mathsf{s}(x),y)
>_{\mathrm{LPO}}
\mathsf{plus}(\mathsf{times}(x,y),y).
\]

### \(p\)-rules

6. Let \(A=\mathsf{p}(\mathsf{s}(\mathsf{s}(x)))\). Since
\(\mathsf{s}(\mathsf{s}(x))>_{\mathrm{LPO}}\mathsf{s}(x)\),

\[
A>_{\mathrm{LPO}}\mathsf{p}(\mathsf{s}(x)).
\]

Together with \(\mathsf{p}\succ\mathsf{s}\), this gives

\[
\mathsf{p}(\mathsf{s}(\mathsf{s}(x)))
>_{\mathrm{LPO}}
\mathsf{s}(\mathsf{p}(\mathsf{s}(x))).
\]

7. \[
\mathsf{p}(\mathsf{s}(0))>_{\mathrm{LPO}}0
\]

because \(0\) is a proper subterm of the left-hand side.

Thus every rewrite step strictly decreases a well-founded, substitution-stable, context-stable ordering. Hence no infinite rewrite sequence exists, so the presented system is **terminating**, without any additional axioms, guards, rules, or object-level assumptions.
