The numerical assignments

\[
[s](n)=n+1,\qquad [p](n)=\max(n-1,0),
\]

and similarly for `plus`, `times`, and `fac`, are **meta-level interpretations of syntax**, not additional object-level axioms. In particular, the proof does **not** add or assume rewrite rules such as

\[
p(s(x))\to x
\]

or equations such as \(p(s(x))=x\) inside the TRS. The fact that

\[
[p(s(x))]=[x]
\]

only says that these two terms receive the same value under the chosen termination interpretation. Terms are allowed to have equal interpretation even when the TRS cannot rewrite one to the other.

Likewise, assigning

\[
[fac](n)=n!
\]

does not assume that the rewrite symbol `fac` “really computes factorial.” An interpretation in a termination proof may assign any total function to a symbol, provided every required rule inequality is verified. Here the assignment is selected because it validates

\[
[fac(s(x))]

[times(fac(p(s(x))),s(x)))].
\]

Nothing is added to the rewrite relation.
