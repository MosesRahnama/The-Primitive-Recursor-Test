### Verdict
**Yes**, termination of the given TRS can be established from the rules alone (via a standard syntactic termination method).

### Termination Argument
We use the **recursive path order (RPO)** induced by the precedence
\[
\texttt{times} > \texttt{plus} > \texttt{s} > 0
\]
with lexicographic status (left-to-right) on all function symbols. RPO is a well-founded strict order on terms; it therefore suffices to show that each of the six rules is oriented by \(>_{\mathrm{RPO}}\). (We recall the relevant clauses of the definition: \(t>_{\mathrm{RPO}}u\) holds if
- some immediate subterm of \(t\) is \(\ge_{\mathrm{RPO}}u\), or
- the root symbol of \(t\) is strictly greater than that of \(u\) and \(t>_{\mathrm{RPO}}\) every immediate subterm of \(u\), or
- the root symbols are identical, the tuples of immediate subterms are lexicographically \(>_{\mathrm{RPO}}\), and \(t>_{\mathrm{RPO}}\) every immediate subterm of \(u\).)

1. \(\texttt{times}(x,0)\to 0\)  
   Root \(\texttt{times}>0\), and the right-hand side has no arguments.

2. \(\texttt{times}(x,\texttt{s}(y))\to\texttt{plus}(\texttt{times}(x,y),x)\)  
   Root \(\texttt{times}>\texttt{plus}\). It remains only to check the two immediate subterms of the right-hand side:  
   - \(\texttt{times}(x,\texttt{s}(y))>_{\mathrm{RPO}}x\) (subterm property);  
   - \(\texttt{times}(x,\texttt{s}(y))>_{\mathrm{RPO}}\texttt{times}(x,y)\) (identical roots; the argument tuples satisfy \((x,\texttt{s}(y))>_{\mathrm{lex}}(x,y)\) because \(\texttt{s}(y)>_{\mathrm{RPO}}y\), and the left-hand side exceeds the remaining argument \(y\)).

3. \(\texttt{plus}(x,0)\to x\)  
   Immediate subterm property.

4. \(\texttt{plus}(0,x)\to x\)  
   Immediate subterm property.

5. \(\texttt{plus}(x,\texttt{s}(y))\to\texttt{s}(\texttt{plus}(x,y))\)  
   Root \(\texttt{plus}>\texttt{s}\). It remains only to check \(\texttt{plus}(x,\texttt{s}(y))>_{\mathrm{RPO}}\texttt{plus}(x,y)\): identical roots, argument tuples satisfy \((x,\texttt{s}(y))>_{\mathrm{lex}}(x,y)\) (because \(\texttt{s}(y)>_{\mathrm{RPO}}y\)), and the left-hand side exceeds the remaining argument \(y\).

6. \(\texttt{plus}(\texttt{s}(x),y)\to\texttt{s}(\texttt{plus}(x,y))\)  
   Root \(\texttt{plus}>\texttt{s}\). It remains only to check \(\texttt{plus}(\texttt{s}(x),y)>_{\mathrm{RPO}}\texttt{plus}(x,y)\): identical roots, argument tuples satisfy \((\texttt{s}(x),y)>_{\mathrm{lex}}(x,y)\) (because \(\texttt{s}(x)>_{\mathrm{RPO}}x\)), and the left-hand side exceeds both remaining arguments \(x\) and \(y\).

Every rule is therefore strictly decreasing in a well-founded order. Hence the TRS is terminating. No additional rewrite rules, guards, side-conditions, interpretations that rely on an intended model, or other object-level assumptions are required; the argument uses only the syntax of the signature and the six given rules.
