That metatheorem is part of the *foundations of the method*, not an axiom added to the system. It's the same status as "lexicographic product of well-founded orders is well-founded" — a tool, not an assumption about the object system.

If the prompt's intent was to forbid *any* appeal to general well-foundedness results, then literally no termination method would be allowed (not LPO, not polynomial interpretations, not dependency pairs). The natural reading is that the *object system* shouldn't be augmented — and on that reading, RPO is fully compliant.
