### Precedence

Take the precedence
$$
\mathsf{times} \;>\; \mathsf{plus} \;>\; \mathsf{p} \;>\; \mathsf{s} \;>\; 0,
$$

Recall the two basic LPO facts we will use repeatedly:

* **(Subterm)** If $t$ is a proper subterm of $s$, then $s >_{lpo} t$.
* **(Precedence step)** If $f>g$ in the precedence and $s = f(s_1,\dots,s_n) >_{lpo} t_i$ for every argument $t_i$ of $g(t_1,\dots,t_m)$, then $f(s_1,\dots,s_n) >_{lpo} g(t_1,\dots,t_m)$.
