### Why this method
The dependency-pair (DP) framework combined with a monotone algebraic interpretation was chosen because it is a standard, self-contained technique that directly applies to the given first-order TRS and stays strictly inside the allowed boundary (only the eight rules, their syntax, and ordinary well-founded orders on \(\mathbb{N}\); no extra rules, no guards, no side conditions, no imported lemmas about arithmetic).

Concretely:
- The rules for \(\mathrm{plus}\) and \(\mathrm{times}\) are the usual primitive-recursive definitions; they generate dependency pairs that simply decrease one argument. An exponential interpretation makes those DPs (and the original rules) decrease for trivial arithmetic reasons.
- The symbol \(\mathrm{p}\) is a “delayed predecessor.” Its two rules produce a single DP that is easily oriented by a floor-division interpretation; again pure arithmetic, no external facts required.
- The critical rule is the one for \(\mathrm{fac}\). It nests a call to \(\mathrm{fac}\) under \(\mathrm{p}\) and then feeds the result into \(\mathrm{times}\). Ordinary recursive path orders or plain polynomial interpretations struggle with the combination of the slow decrease of \(\mathrm{p}\) and the very fast growth of \(\mathrm{times}/\mathrm{fac}\). The DP method isolates exactly the two recursive calls that matter, turning the problem into the construction of a single numeric function \(f\) that majorises those calls. Defining \(f\) by a trivial primitive recursion on \(\mathbb{N}\) is legitimate (it uses only the well-foundedness of the naturals) and makes both \(\mathrm{fac}\)-DPs strictly decrease by construction.

Because every rule and every DP is checked against the same reduction pair, the argument is exhaustive and does not leave any rule unconstrained. Simpler candidates (RPO alone, a single global polynomial interpretation, Knuth-Bendix order, \ldots) either fail to orient the \(\mathrm{fac}\) rule or require additional ad-hoc justifications that would step outside the “rules alone” mandate. DP+interpretation avoids that difficulty while remaining elementary and fully formalisable from the presented syntax.
