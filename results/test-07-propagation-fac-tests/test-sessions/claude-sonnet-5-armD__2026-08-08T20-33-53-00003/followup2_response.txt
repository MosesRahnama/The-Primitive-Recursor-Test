## What the constraint actually forbids

The constraint says I cannot **strengthen the rewrite system itself** — i.e., I cannot add new rewrite rules, add guards/conditions to existing rules, or silently assume extra rewriting behavior (e.g., assuming `x = x` reduces, or that terms carry hidden invariants like "all arguments are numerals").
