The choice of the **Dependency Pair (DP) Framework** combined with **Algebraic Interpretations** was driven by specific structural challenges in your Term Rewriting System (TRS). 

Here is why this specific method was chosen over other standard termination techniques (like Lexicographic Path Orderings (LPO) or global polynomial interpretations):

### 1. The Bottleneck: The Predecessor Function `p` in `fac`
The most difficult rule in your system is:
$$\text{fac}(s(x)) \to \text{times}(\text{fac}(p(s(x))), s(x))$$

To prove termination, any method must show that the argument of the recursive call to `fac` decreases. That is, we must prove that $s(x)$ is "strictly larger" than $p(s(x))$. 
* **Why standard Syntactic Path Orderings (like LPO or RPO) fail:** Syntactically, the term $p(s(x))$ contains the function symbol $p$ at the root, which is not present in the left-hand argument $s(x)$. Because $p(s(x))$ is not a subterm of $s(x)$, standard LPO cannot orient this step without complex argument filtering. LPO does not "know" that $p$ acts as a predecessor that reduces the size of $s(x)$.
* **How the chosen method solves this:** By using **Algebraic Interpretations**, we can define the mathematical meaning of $p$ as a predecessor ($[p(x)] = \max(0, x-1)$) and $s$ as a successor. This allows us to mathematically prove that $s(x)$ is strictly larger than $p(s(x))$ (i.e., $x+2 > x+1$), bypassing the syntactic limitation.

### 2. Modularity (Decomposition via SCCs)
In a global termination proof (like finding a single global polynomial interpretation), every single rule in the system must strictly decrease simultaneously. Finding one global interpretation that satisfies `plus`, `times`, `p`, and `fac` all at once is highly constrained and mathematically difficult.

* **How the DP Framework solves this:** It builds a dependency graph and isolates the recursive loops (Strongly Connected Components). This allowed us to:
  * Prove `plus` terminates independently.
  * Prove `times` terminates independently.
  * Prove `p` terminates independently.
  * Focus the complex algebraic interpretation *only* on the `fac` loop, ignoring the irrelevant details of `plus` and `times`.

### 3. Strict Compliance with your Constraints
Your prompt demanded a proof "from the presented rules alone, without importing structural axioms or object-level assumptions."
* Many automated tools prove termination of such systems by secretly adding induction axioms or assuming that variables represent positive integers.
* The DP framework with weak monotonicity on $\mathbb{N}$ is entirely self-contained. The proof relies only on the standard properties of natural number arithmetic ($\mathbb{N}, +, >$), which is the standard, mathematically rigorous meta-language for termination proofs.
