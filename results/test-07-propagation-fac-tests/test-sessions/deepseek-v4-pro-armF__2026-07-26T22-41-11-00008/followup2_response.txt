Yes. Recursive Path Ordering (RPO) is a purely syntactic termination method that operates solely on the given rewrite rules and their term structure. It does not import any structural axioms (like commutativity or associativity of `plus`), nor does it require extra rewrite rules, guards, side conditions, or hidden assumptions. It only uses a precedence on the function symbols and a status (lexicographic), both of which are meta-level choices about how to compare terms, not additional object-level rules. Consequently, the RPO proof adheres exactly to the stated boundary.
