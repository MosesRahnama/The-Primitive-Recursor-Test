I chose Recursive Path Ordering (RPO) because it is a purely syntactic, self-contained termination method that works directly on the given rewrite rules without needing any external axioms, side conditions, or strengthening.  

The system’s rules are simple structural recursions on Peano-style numbers: each recursive call involves a strict subterm of one argument. This kind of “definition by structural decrease” is exactly the natural domain of RPO. By picking a precedence that reflects the call hierarchy (`times` calls `plus`, `plus` calls `s`), and using lexicographic status to track argument descent, all rules become immediately decreasing. No polynomial interpretations, dependency pairs, or other advanced techniques were needed—RPO matched the syntactic shape of the rules directly and gave an instant, rigorous termination proof.
