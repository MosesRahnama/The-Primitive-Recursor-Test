Yes, the proposed method complies with those requirements.

A dependency-pair argument or a syntactic well-founded measure is a metatheoretic proof technique applied to the rules as written. It does not add rewrite rules, assume that `0` and `s` are natural numbers, assume injectivity/disjointness of constructors, or rely on intended meanings for `plus`, `times`, `p`, or `fac`.

The only background assumptions such a proof uses are the ordinary definition of first-order rewriting: terms are finite syntax trees, rewriting uses the displayed rules and substitutions, and termination means that there is no infinite rewrite sequence. Those are not extra object-level axioms or structural rules.
