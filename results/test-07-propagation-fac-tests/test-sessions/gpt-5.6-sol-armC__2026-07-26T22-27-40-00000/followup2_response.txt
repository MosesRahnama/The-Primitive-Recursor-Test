Yes—**the proposed method complies**, provided semantic labeling is used as a meta-level termination technique rather than as an extension of the rewrite system.

The arithmetic interpretation does **not** assert new object-level equations such as

\[
p(s(x)) \to x
\]

or assume that all terms are numerals. It merely assigns semantic values to terms and verifies that both sides of every given rule have the same value. Semantic labeling then constructs an auxiliary labeled system for the proof; it does not add executable rules, guards, or side conditions to the original TRS.

In particular, the fact that \(p(s(x))\) receives the value of \(x\) follows from the chosen interpretation, not from treating that equation as an additional rewrite rule. The labels expose a well-founded decrease in recursive calls, including the otherwise syntactically hidden decrease in `fac`.
