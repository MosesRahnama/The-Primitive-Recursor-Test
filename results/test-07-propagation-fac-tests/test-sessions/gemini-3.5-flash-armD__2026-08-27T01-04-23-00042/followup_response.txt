I chose **Multiset Path Ordering (MPO)** for several key reasons, making it the most suitable and elegant tool for this specific rewriting system:

### 1. Purely Syntactic and Self-Contained
The prompt emphasized establishing termination *from the presented rules alone, without importing external structural axioms or object-level assumptions*. 
* If I had chosen **Polynomial Interpretations** (mapping terms to the natural numbers), the proof would have relied on external algebraic properties of natural numbers (e.g., that addition and multiplication are monotonic and well-founded).
* **MPO** is a purely syntactic, structural simplification ordering. Its well-foundedness is a meta-theoretic property of the algebra of terms themselves. It requires no external mathematical domain—only a strict partial order (precedence) on the function symbols of the system itself.

### 2. A Perfect Fit for Structural Recursion
The rewrite rules in this system are classic examples of structural recursion, where a function processes a constructor-based data type (natural numbers represented by `0` and `s`):
* In `plus(x, s(y)) -> s(plus(x, y))`, the argument is structurally stripped of one `s` constructor.
* In `times(s(x), y) -> plus(times(x, y), y)`, the first argument structurally decreases from `s(x)` to `x`.
* In `p(s(s(x))) -> s(p(s(x)))`, the argument structurally decreases from `s(s(x))` to `s(x)`.

Path orderings like MPO are specifically designed to capture this exact type of structural decrease. By assigning a higher precedence to the defined operations (`times`, `plus`, `p`) than to the constructors (`s`, `0`), MPO trivially handles the "peeling off" of constructors during recursive steps.

### 3. Avoidance of Unnecessary Complexity (Occam's Razor)
While more powerful frameworks like **Dependency Pairs** or **Matrix Interpretations** exist, they are overkill for this system. 
* This system has no mutually recursive loops, no nested active functions in the arguments, and no complex non-terminating loops. 
* MPO is a *simplification ordering*. According to Dershowitz's theorem, any term rewriting system that is compatible with a simplification ordering is terminating. Since a simple, intuitive precedence solves all 7 rules simultaneously in one step, MPO provides the most direct and mathematically "cleanest" proof.
